The parity lemma correctly chooses an unfolded interior lattice point with an odd coordinate by dividing the even-even endpoint by its largest common power of2. Integer-line reflections preserve each coordinate parity. No assertion about a bounded collection of directions is used.

At each of the four smooth seam endpoints, the copy change and actual wall reflection negate complementary components. The folded velocity reverses completely. Before the first reversal, the projected path is ordinary reversible billiard motion. After first reversal it must retrace to its last initial occurrence of a before reaching b. Taking the last visit to a after folding is essential because several physical points may map to a. The revised text states that point and the finite-leg convention explicitly. Axis-parallel physical paths cannot connect S,T because their two coordinates differ, so positive-length seam-gliding does not enter the argument.
