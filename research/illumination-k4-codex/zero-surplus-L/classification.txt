1. Scope and explicit goal
Use strictly simple canonical45-degree triangle disk rooms with exactly three full A stars at S=(0,0), U=(2,0), T=(2,2), and no other interior A points. Full cell coordinates are F={(0,0),(1,0),(1,1)} after dividing A coordinates by2.
Let H be the union of these three closed stars. Its exposed-cell-side count is p(F)=4I-2e(F)=12-4=8.
Assume ZERO SURPLUS:
  Q+s_N=8.
Here Q is3 per smooth boundary A point and the positive triangle incidence at every genuine A corner; s_N counts smooth boundary B/C points. k=J+s_N counts all interior or smooth non-A points. The budget of interest is W<=21,k<=6, with actual marks S,T.
Missing implication attacked: does equality eliminate arbitrary partial fans and distant geometry, leaving a precise bounded optical family? Success is an exhaustive necessary reduction, plus a family-level optical consequence. A single extra illuminated example would not qualify.
Strongest objection: equality may still admit smooth A fans or two-triangle bridges, and extending a reflecting wall can remove an old path. Both are treated below.

2. Equality in side charging
For each nonfull occupied A cell v, let h(v) be the number of exposed sides of full cells that it touches with at least one occupied triangle. Each such side is charged once to v. The other exposed sides have neither opposite triangle occupied, so their C midpoints form a set M of distinct smooth C points.
Thus p(F)=sum_v h(v)+|M|, with M a subset of the smooth non-A points. For a partial m-triangle fan, q(v)=m except q(v)=3 at a smooth four-triangle fan. The number of touched sides is at most m for a nonsmooth fan, and at most3 for a smooth half-star. Hence q(v)>=h(v).
The exact slack decomposition is
  Q+s_N-p(F) = sum_v(q(v)-h(v)) + (s_N-|M|),
with every term nonnegative. Under equality:
 (i) q(v)=h(v) for every occupied partial cell;
 (ii) every smooth non-A point is one of the C midpoints in M. In particular there are NO smooth B points.
No full cell outside F is possible because I is exactly3. Any occupied partial cell with no adjacent full side has h=0<q and is therefore impossible. This also rules out all distant additions.

The seven external neighboring cells, in PHYSICAL A coordinates, are
 L=(-2,0), B=(0,-2), D=(2,-2), R=(4,0), E=(4,2), Tcap=(2,4), N=(0,2).
All have at most one full-cell neighbor except N, which has two (below and right). Therefore h<=1 except h(N)<=2. Equality q=h rules out every fan of size>=3, including the smooth size4 case whose q=3. Every occupied external cell has one triangle, except N may have two.
If N has two triangles, h(N)=2 forces precisely
 conv{(0,2),(0,1),(1,1)} and conv{(0,2),(1,1),(1,2)}.
These fill the missing90degree sector at B_mid=(1,1). The core already contributes270degrees there, so the midpoint becomes interior. ST is then a legal direct segment. Such a room is illuminated without any reflected-path argument.
Consequently every potentially DARK equality room consists of H plus at most ONE triangle in each of these seven cells. This is a complete geometric reduction; it does not assume the room contains the four original reference caps.

At the five convex B corners, adding45degrees on both sides would make a forbidden smooth180degree B point. Thus the following simultaneous pairs are forbidden:

These are all B points affected by the attachments; the cap's B endpoint is always an existing boundary B of H.

Conversely a selection obeying these rules yields a zero-surplus room. Each cap attaches to H along one exposed half-side; different caps have distinct new A vertices and can meet only at existing B endpoints. All local occupied fans remain contiguous. The union retracts onto the L core by collapsing the added triangles, has no extra boundary component and no pinch. It is a strictly simple disk. The listed B rules remove exactly all smooth B points. Its only smooth C points are the exposed side midpoints without an opposite cap. Therefore it has precisely the equality ledger below.

4. Exact ledger and budget
Let n be the number of single-cap triangles. All new A points are45degree corners, so Q=n and s_A=0. No new interior non-A point is created: an exposed C gets at most one extra90degree quadrant, and every B remains a boundary corner. Thus J=2, as in H. Each cap removes one of the8 exposed smooth C points, so s_N=8-n. Hence
  N_triangles=24+n, k=10-n, W=8+2n.
With W<=21,k<=6:
  n in {4,5,6},
  (W,k) in {(16,6),(18,5),(20,4)}.
In particular no odd-W equality case is being hidden in the reduction.
Darkness additionally forces B+ and E- by the two already proved one-reflection paths in exception-geometry/darkness-constraint.txt section4. If either is absent, that path remains legal regardless of the other caps. These forced caps already remove the two flat B exceptions, but do not alone prove illumination.

5. Two concrete paths cover ALL equality superrooms of the reference R
Let R be H plus the four caps L+,B+,E-,T-, the previously verified16wall room. Claim:
Every zero-surplus room with the same three full stars that contains R illuminates S,T. This claim does NOT require W<=21 or k<=6.
If the two-triangle N bridge is present, section2 gives direct illumination. Otherwise the single-cap classification applies.
The fixed13bounce path p in cap-return-review/findings.txt requires only B+ and T- outside H. Among all16 single-cap options, its forbidden tiles are
  B-,L-,Nb-,Nb+,T+.
Its other two forbidden tiles are not available in the equality classification. Since R is present, B-,L-,T+ are excluded by the one-cap-per-cell rule; Nb- is excluded by the smooth-B rule with L+. Thus p can be blocked within this subfamily ONLY by Nb+.

Use the isometry
  sigma(x,y)=(2-y,2-x).
It preserves H, sends S to T and T to S, and permutes the four R caps as B+<->E-, L+<->T-. Reflect p by sigma and reverse its time orientation, giving another actual S-to-T connection p_sigma. The added room need not itself be symmetric: the reflected fixed path's own tile requirements are checked separately.
Path p_sigma requires only E- and L+ outside H. Its forbidden cap options are
  E+,L-,Nr+,Nr-,T+.
Again, R excludes E+,L-,T+; the smooth-B rule with T- excludes Nr+. Hence p_sigma can be blocked ONLY by Nr-.
But Nb+ and Nr- occupy the SAME external A cell N. The potentially dark equality classification allows at most one triangle in N. If both are present, they are exactly the two-triangle direct bridge already handled. Therefore at least one of p,p_sigma is legal, or the direct connection is legal. Every equality superroom of R is illuminated.

This covers a whole family through one structural argument, not one new witness per completion. It also eliminates the need to inspect the three W20/k4 residual fixtures I initially proposed on the board: sigma13 already covers all three.
