# Construction geometry and related mathematics

The specified strictly simple orthogonal room has 22 maximal sides, area 15 and marked points (0,0), (2,2). Its hand proof folds three copies onto a five-square staircase and controls four smooth seam endpoints by reversal.

Wolecki's displayed 22-edge polygon has diagonal edges and a boundary contact. The orthogonal figure reconstructed in `novelty-geometry-check.json` has 34 walls. These are geometric comparisons of specified figures.
