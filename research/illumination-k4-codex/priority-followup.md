# Coordinate comparison and literature gaps

The frozen construction has 22 cyclic corners, area 15 and two interior marked points. The canonical fingerprint in `priority-followup.json` supports comparison under translation, axis rotations/reflections, common scale, cyclic relabeling and mark interchange. It is an identity aid, not a novelty proof.

Wolecki's published Theorem 1.3 supplies a 22-edge upper bound. The exact identity of the present strictly simple orthogonal realization with earlier examples remains unresolved. The construction must not be presented as a new unrestricted upper bound or as confirmed original research.

The checked Monteil papers page and 2011 Oberwolfach summary discuss finite blocking and translation surfaces without supplying the geometry needed for a coordinate comparison. Missing original Tokarsky figures and inaccessible historical geometry remain source-access gaps.
