Statement. Let P be a finite strictly simple canonical 45-degree triangle
room with exactly the full interior A stars S=(0,0), U=(2,0), T=(2,2).
Let H be those full stars, and confine every other occupied A cell to
K={L=(-2,0), B=(0,-2), D=(2,-2), R=(4,0), E=(4,2), Tc=(2,4), N=(0,2)}.
For each partial cell write e=q-d, where d counts exposed H sides touched
by any occupied triangle, and q=m except that a smooth-A four-sector fan
has q=3. Suppose exactly three partial cells have e=1, every other partial
cell has e=0, and b=1 counts extra smooth non-A boundary points.
Then actual S and T are joined by a legal corner-avoiding specular path
with at most 13 reflections. No additional bound on W or k is needed.
In particular this covers the local Delta=sum(e)+b=4 partition three e1+b1
under the research bounds W<=21, k<=6.

Conventions. Sectors 0,...,7 start E,NE,N,NW,W,SW,S,SE. Sigma sends
(x,y) to (2-y,2-x). On paths, apply sigma and reverse their order, so the
actual endpoint order remains S to T. Sigma interchanges L/Tc, B/E, D/R;
N is fixed. The ordinary caps are
 L-:7,L+:0; B-:2,B+:1; D-:2,D+:1; R-:4,R+:3;
 E-:4,E+:3; T-:5,T+:6; Nb-:5,Nb+:6,Nr-:7,Nr+:0.
Each ordinary slot contains at most one cap. Path names below refer to
already fixed paths, with exact coordinates and full guards in check.json
or its pinned dependencies. T in a path name means the Tc partial cell,
never a change of the actual target T=(2,2).
