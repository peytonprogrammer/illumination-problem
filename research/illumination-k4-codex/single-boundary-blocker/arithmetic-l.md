# Arithmetic six-wall L limits: next theory target

[in progress] Codex. Disjoint from Opus's non-parity folding expansion.

## Question

In a strictly simple commensurable six-wall L-shaped billiard, can a smooth boundary point z be essential between distinct interior s,t? Essential means there is an ordinary connection, but every ordinary connection meets z. We seek either a removal theorem or a precise algebraic obstruction; do not assume the desired theorem is true.

The non-torus genus-two case is already excluded by Apisa's published theorem and independently accepted by Opus. Ordinary-cylinder connections reduce any possible essential case to an invariant central core, and rational connections have the reflex-saddle/central-core dichotomy. Neither gives existence of a bypass in this remaining arithmetic case.

## First tractable model

Use the prescribed three-unit-square L with boundary (0,0),(2,0),(2,1),(1,1),(1,2),(0,2). Its four-copy unfolding is genus2 and admits a degree3 covering of R²/(2Z²), branched at the image of the reflex corner. This is one fixed analytic model, not a room search.

Start by deriving its exact corner-fiber and smooth-boundary-fiber structure. Determine whether a two-point lifted blocker can close the connections left by the corner lifts. Work with arbitrary interior marks symbolically; rational mark assumptions must be explicit when a finite orbit argument needs them. A missing torus midpoint blocker alone is not proof of a physical connection: the lift must reach a target sheet, which is the monodromy issue to resolve.

## Deliverable

An explicit torus-cover/monodromy criterion for the fixed model, with scope checked against the known cylinder obstruction. Extend to arbitrary commensurable L dimensions only if that criterion supplies a valid general mechanism. No optical searches or claims of a global lower bound.

## Why this work matters

The isolated smooth-step degeneration retains a collapsed boundary blocker at no decrease in wall-plus-blocker cost. Removing that blocker would close an obstruction in the degeneration program toward lower bounds beyond the folding class. A counterexample would show which additional hypothesis the program needs. Neither result is guaranteed.

## Outcome: an elementary route closes the boundary-singleton question

The torus-cover route was not needed. `l-monotone-proof.md` constructs an increasing-y connection using vertical reflections only, and the transposed construction supplies a horizontal-only connection. Thus every smooth boundary singleton can be avoided for any strictly simple six-wall L with distinct interior marks, regardless of rationality. A separate Opus review accepts this proof, and972 prescribed-formula connections pass exact validation.

The degree3 fiber/monodromy program is therefore unnecessary for this specific question and was not run. The new result does not claim to classify arbitrary finite blocker sets, especially interior blockers or boundary blockers on both wall orientations. General higher-genus orthogonal singleton removal remains open.
