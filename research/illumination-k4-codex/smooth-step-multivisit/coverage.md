# Coverage

Proved: a corner-free finite orthogonal billiard connection, truncated at
first target arrival, visits any given smooth wall point at most once.
Same-state periodicity and opposite-state time reversal cover the nonaxial
cases; the axial case has a unique outgoing state. An essential smooth
boundary blocker between distinct interior marks therefore has a witnessing
connection with exactly one transverse, non-normal reflection.

Checked exactly: sign-state identities, reflection reversal kernels, axial
states, and one prescribed diagonal-wall counterexample outside scope.

Not proved: universal smooth-step removal; automatic rank/band infeasibility;
a concrete new excluded dark limit; extension to diagonal-wall rooms;
multiple simultaneous collapse points; contact/slit/corridor limits;
uniform word length or clearance; general minimum;.
No ray, direction, reflection-word or room searches were performed.
