source wall, avoiding the previously exhausted first-left-pocket launch.

gate crossings are not reflections. Each identity follows by intersecting

The finite verification uses exact rational arithmetic, without tolerances:
(a) S and T are strictly interior by horizontal crossing parity.
(b) Each intermediate Pi is strictly within one maximal boundary wall.
(c) Reflection across that wall takes Pi-P(i-1) to a positive multiple
    of P(i+1)-Pi (cross product zero and dot product positive).
(d) For each segment p->q and each wall a->b, set v=q-p, w=b-a.
    If det(v,w) is nonzero, the intersection parameters are
    t=det(a-p,w)/det(v,w), u=det(a-p,v)/det(v,w).
    None has 0<t<1 and 0<=u<=1. Parallel collinear wall intervals have
    no positive-length overlap with the segment. Each segment midpoint
    is strictly interior. Thus every open segment is entirely interior,
    and every listed reflection is the actual first boundary contact.

The identical four tests pass in both polygons, in reverse, and after
