1. Exact class and coordinates
The model is the fixed canonical45-degree integer triangulation from ../restricted-theorem-note/theorem.txt: A points are even/even, B odd/odd, C mixed parity. Each unit square has its A-B diagonal. A star is the eight-triangle square of side2 centered at A. A room is a finite union of these triangles with one strictly simple Jordan polygon boundary and connected interior. Marks are two distinct interior A points, and walls are maximal boundary segments.
This task restricts the room to ORTHOGONAL boundaries. Hence either both triangles of every unit square occur or neither occurs: otherwise an exposed A-B diagonal would be a nonorthogonal boundary edge. Thus these rooms are unit-square unions on this fixed grid.
Let I count ALL interior A points, not merely the two selected marks. Let J count interior B/C points, s_N smooth boundary B/C points, and k=J+s_N. Boundary A points are not included in k. Let s_A count smooth boundary A points. At a genuine boundary A corner let m_v count its incident triangles, and put Q=3s_A+sum m_v.

4. I2: a complete displacement range, then exclude distance8

The d=8 cases are all impossible under orthogonality. Here is the complete geometric argument, rather than a direction or room search.
Let e=|U intersect(R minus F)| be the occupied external cells and o=|U minus R|. The two full-cell neighborhoods are disjoint and far apart. Each full cell needs an occupied neighbor to connect to the rest of U, so e>=2. Any connecting path between the neighborhoods uses at least5outside cells, so o>=5. Since b=8,
  P=e+o, m=8-e.
By(1) with I=2, Q+s_N<=20. By(3) and m<=s_N,
  Q+s_N>=2P+m=8+e+2o>=8+2+10=20.
Every inequality is therefore equality. In particular e=2,o=5,P=7,m=6, and Q=2P. ALL seven partial A cells are single occupied UNIT QUADRANTS, with charge2; no other partial cells exist.
A single occupied quadrant is one unit square in its A cell, with exactly one odd/odd B corner. Its two outer BC edges meet that same B corner. Two such partial A cells can connect across their common A-cell side only by sharing one of those BC edges; therefore their selected unit squares have the SAME global B corner. Every connected component made only of these quarter-fan partial cells consequently has one common B corner and contains at most FOUR A cells, the four around that B.
But U contains only the two full cells and seven partial cells. A simple occupied-cell path between the full cells has at least d=8edges, hence at least seven intervening partial cells. They form one connected partial-only chain. Such a chain cannot fit in a component of at most four cells. Contradiction.
Thus ALL five normalized d8 offsets(4,4),(5,3),(6,2),(7,1),(8,0) are excluded. No selected room or ray has been enumerated to obtain this exclusion.
