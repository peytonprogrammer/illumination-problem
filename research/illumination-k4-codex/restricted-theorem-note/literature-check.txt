Primary sources and precise comparison

1. Amit Wolecki, Illumination in Rational Billiards, arXiv1905.09358v3 (30November2024).
https://arxiv.org/html/1905.09358v3
Theorem1.3 proves an UPPER bound of22 sides. Section3 supplies a reflected right-isosceles-triangle construction with every B/C preimage a true corner; Lemma3.1 supplies a seed nonreturn obstruction. Neither the theorem nor the construction proves that smaller rooms in that seed class are impossible. The inspected paper contains no matching at-most-six-exception exclusion, finite support census or contradiction-certificate lower bound. Our class allows transparent B/C preimages, so merely sharing the seed does not subsume the theorem. Theorem1.2 concerns finiteness of dark pairs, not a lower wall count.

2. Lelièvre–Monteil–Weiss, Everything is illuminated, Geometry & Topology20(2016),1737–1762.
https://msp.org/gt/2016/20-3/gt-v20-n3-p11-p.pdf
Theorem2 gives finite dark targets per fixed source on translation surfaces; Corollary3 gives the corresponding rational-polygon result. Section6 constructs/discusses blocking configurations; it does not supply the proposed wall/exception-budget exclusion. The source's question about infinitely many dark pairs in a rational polygon is later answered by Wolecki's Theorem1.2. That chronology must not be mistaken for a wall-minimality result.

3. Hubert–Schmoll–Troubetzkoy, Modular fibers and illumination problems, arXivmath/0602394v2.
https://arxiv.org/html/math/0602394v2
Theorems1–2 concern countability/finiteness of dark targets for prelattice/Veech surfaces. The introduction records earlier nonillumination constructions and an unpublished-letter antecedent. Those statements do not imply the numerical lower-bound exclusion in theorem.txt. Targeted text checks for minimal/computer returned no matching wall-minimality or census claim; this is not a full theorem-by-theorem subsumption analysis of every special surface classification in the paper.
