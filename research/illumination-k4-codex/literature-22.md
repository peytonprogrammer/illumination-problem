# Literature check for the 22-wall candidate

Updated 2026-10-31. Supersedes the initial bounded check.

The completed deep audit is in [novelty-audit.md](novelty-audit.md); coverage is in [novelty-coverage.md](novelty-coverage.md), source ledger in `novelty-sources.json`, and reproducible vector comparisons in `novelty-geometry-check.py/json/log`.

Judgment: potentially new specific strictly simple orthogonal 22-wall geometry, not an established priority claim. General 22-edge examples are already known (Wolecki2019, revised2024; unpublished Monteil acknowledged). Folding/parity and removable-corner retracing are established ideas. Tokarsky–Boese2000 explicitly discusses reflected-copy constructions and orthogonal invisible corners.

Source vector check: Wolecki v1/v3 figure PDFs are identical and have a degree4 point contact; O'Rourke's orthogonal figure has34 walls; our exact candidate has22 and a single degree2 boundary cycle. Those facts do not establish that24 was the historical minimum for strictly simple rooms.

Critical gaps: Monteil's unpublished22 geometry; complete original Tokarsky1995 paper; reliable geometry of Tokarsky–Boese2000 figures. Compare those before claiming first or record. Darkness and geometry of our candidate are independently verified regardless of novelty.
