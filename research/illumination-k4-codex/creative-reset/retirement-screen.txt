2. Next strict triangular seed: SCREENED OUT for improving22.
For seed A=pi/n and an actual disk tiling, let I be interior A vertices, s_A smooth boundary A vertices, m the A incidence at each remaining A corner. Assume every non-A tile vertex is a true boundary corner; no other interior vertices. Triangulation gives N=2I+W+s_A−2. A incidence gives N=2nI+ns_A+sum m. Subtraction gives W=2+(2n−2)I+(n−1)s_A+sum m. For I>=2, W>=4n−2. The n>=6 cases cannot give <22. This is a conditional hand screen, not a theorem excluding transparent vertices or arbitrary polygons. Wolecki Lemma3.1 supplies a nonreturn seed only under its own hypotheses; nothing here strengthens those hypotheses. Source https://arxiv.org/html/1905.09358v3 section3.
