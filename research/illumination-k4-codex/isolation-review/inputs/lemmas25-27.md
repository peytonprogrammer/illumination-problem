

Step 2 of the plan needs every dark room of a shape to be isolated. Theorem 6 shows this one room at a time. This section records the structure that all four certified rooms share, and what it reduces the general question to. It's Claude's work from 2026-11-01, not yet reviewed by Codex.

### Lemma 25: parity rooms are dark

Call a room in the canonical tiling a *parity room* for marks `s, t` at A points when every non-A lattice point of the closed room is a corner. A parity room is dark.

*Proof.* This is the argument of Lemma 1, written by Codex for Jain's room in `jain28/darkness.md`. Unfold a path from `s` to `t`. Reflections in tiling lines keep A points as A points, so the unfolded target is `s + 2g·w`, with `w` primitive and `g >= 1`. The point `s + w` is a non-A lattice point inside the segment. It folds back to a non-A lattice point of the closed room, which is a corner. That stops the path. ∎

All four certified rooms are parity rooms: the 24-wall room, Wolecki's pinch room, the 34-wall room, and Jain's room. A direct check of each room's lattice points confirms this.

### Lemma 26: every event sits at a midpoint

In a parity room, each event of Lemma 5 has unfolded target `s + 2w`, with `w` primitive, and its corner is the fold of the midpoint `s + w`.

*Proof.* An event path meets exactly one corner. The lattice points of the segment from `s` to `s + 2g·w` are `s + k·w`, and they're non-A exactly when `k` is odd. So the segment holds `g` non-A points, each a corner, and `g = 1`. ∎

All 932 events in the four certificates have this form. That covers 168, 179, 403, and 182 events respectively, with `R` = 12, 18, 18, and 18.

**Consequence: events are combinatorial.** Every primitive direction `w` from `s` reaches a corner `c_w` at unfolded distance `|w|`, because no lattice point lies before it. Past `c_w` on side `σ`, the next lattice point is `s + 2w`, an A point. An event occurs exactly when that point folds to `t`. If it folds elsewhere, the path continues to `s + 3w`, a corner, and stops. So a parity room's events come from folding alone, with no ray tracing.

### Lemma 27: the taut-string form

Write `γ1` for the leg from `s` to `c` and `γ2` for the leg from `c` to `t`. By Lemma 26, both legs have holonomy `w = (p, q)` and length `a = |w|`. Let `δω = δx + i·δy` be the first-order change of the periods of the unfolded surface. Then

    g_e(δ) = σ_e · ⟨p·δy − q·δx, η_e⟩,   η_e = [γ2] − [γ1].

Here `η_e` is a relative homology class with zero holonomy, and `∂η_e = s + t − 2c`.

*Proof.* Lemma 5's functional is the derivative of `hol γ1 × hol γ2`. It equals `b·(w/a) × δhol γ1 − a·(w/a) × δhol γ2`, up to sign. Set `a = b` and `δhol γ = ⟨δω, γ⟩`. ∎

So `g_e / a²` is the first-order change of the bend angle at `c`. Darkness requires the angle on the incoming side `σ_e` to stay at least `π`. Each event is a string pulled taut around a corner, and isolation asks whether any deformation can keep every string taut.

GL(2,R) changes `δω = Aω` give `⟨Aω, η_e⟩ = A·hol(η_e) = 0`. So the symmetries lie in every kernel, as Theorem 6 requires.

### What the data rules out

Three cheaper mechanisms fail on all four rooms:

- **No two-sided events.** No event passes straight through a corner, so no event gives a linear equality.
- **No cancelling pairs.** No two events have opposite functionals, even modulo closure. All 932 rays give distinct functionals.
- **No natural weights.** The combinations `Σ w_e·g_e` with `w_e` = 1, `1/a²`, `1/a³`, or `1/|g_e|` leave residuals of 3 to 31 percent of `Σ w_e·|g_e|`. Random positive weights leave 4 to 11 percent. So no visible integral identity forces the certificate.

The certificates are global combinations. A general proof has to show that the cone generated by the vectors `σ_e·(p_e·η_e^y − q_e·η_e^x)` is all of `(V/sym)*`, where `V` is the room's deformation space. The vectors `η_e` grow sublinearly in `|w|`, because the two legs cross each wall about equally often. A log-log fit of `|g_e|/a` against `a` gives exponents 0.22 to 0.64 on the four rooms, with `a` up to 32. Their growth is governed by the deviation spectrum of the Kontsevich–Zorich cocycle on the square-tiled surface. That's the likely route. It's open.

### What it changes

- Darkness of a parity room needs no ray tracing, and neither do its events. A parity room's certificate can be built by folding.
- Every known dark room in the 45° family is a parity room. A dark room that isn't a parity room would need a different mechanism, and none is known. Real (non-lattice) dark rooms of a shape are therefore a separate open question. Lemma 25 says nothing about them.
