# Unequal-depth corridor objective

Prove or refute: for all positive d,g,h_-,h_+, every pair S=(s,-a),
T=(t,g+b), with 0<s,t<2d, 0<a<h_-, 0<b<h_+, illuminates in
P=[0,2d]×[-h_-,0] union [d,3d]×[0,g] union [0,2d]×[g,g+h_+].
A valid connection is finite, reaches actual T from actual S, obeys the
specular law on smooth actual walls, and avoids every polygon corner.
No uniform reflection bound is required. Full independent acceptance or
independently validated actual dark counterexample is necessary to complete.
Other room families/interior-pair types/universal wall minimality excluded.
