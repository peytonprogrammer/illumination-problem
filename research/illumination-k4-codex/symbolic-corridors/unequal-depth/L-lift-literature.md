# Prescribed endpoint copies in an L-room: targeted literature check

No applicable all-pairs theorem verified. Close this shortcut rather than assume ordinary physical L-room illumination implies all target copies illuminate.

Primary source: Paul Apisa, *Periodic Points in Genus Two: Holomorphic Sections over Hilbert Modular Varieties, Teichmüller Dynamics, and Billiards*, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.05505, Theorems 4 and 6.

Theorem 4 concerns primitive genus-two translation surfaces. The definition excludes pullbacks from lower genus. Theorem 6 explicitly says that rational horizontal and vertical arm ratios make the L-room unfolding a torus cover. Our remaining rational L-subrooms are in that excluded arithmetic class. Finite blocking on torus covers does not imply darkness, but it also does not prove every prescribed lift pair illuminates.

In addition, even an unmarked regular-pair illumination statement would require accounting for forbidden regular convex-corner lifts. The accepted physical L theorem does not prescribe its ending copy or reflection parity. The new one-fiber transport lemma avoids that extra requirement by backwards lifting; it does not establish both parities for L paths.

No new coverage is claimed from this source. This does not prove that no arithmetic L-surface illumination theorem exists; it records that the attractive primitive-genus-two theorem cannot be applied here.
