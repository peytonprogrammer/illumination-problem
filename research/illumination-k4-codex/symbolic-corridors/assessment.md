## What changed in the strategy

An exact straight-passage transfer alone was insufficient. The two portal
constraints can be incompatible even when each strip separately folds
correctly. A proof must also choose a middle reflection mechanism. The example
gives a concrete, small obstruction to the tempting monotone-only extension
of our L theorem. The constructive proof demonstrates both variable bounce
counts and the need to handle exceptional equalities separately.

This differs from the retired reflection cutoff (there is no fixed cap),
local bypass counting (no physical-corner multiplication), and wavefront
charging (no global index assumed). It extends the existing real-parameter
L construction only to the expressly defined symmetric two-portal family.
It does not prove that all corridor networks admit such a finite symbolic
description, nor that any resulting description has a wall-count bound.
