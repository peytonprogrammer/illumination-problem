Let P be a bounded strictly simple orthogonal polygonal disk. Let X be its connected standard four-copy unfolding with differential omega and group G={1,H,V,iota=HV}. H,V are reflections. Source S and target T are distinct interior points; each fiber consists of four regular points. Actual corner-lift set F has one point per physical corner, including regular convex corner lifts. The involution iota has derivative -I, Fix(iota)=F, and X/iota is the doubled disk, hence a sphere. For r reflex corners, there are r zeros of order 2, genus g=r+1, and W=2g+2. For g>=2, iota is the unique hyperelliptic involution and is central among holomorphic automorphisms.

P1: The 16 ordered source-target lift pairs form only four diagonal G-orbits. Darkness is equivalent to no corner-avoiding segment from one fixed lift x of S to each of the four target lifts. Counting 16 independent singularity costs is therefore unjustified.

P2 (candidate): An affine INVOLUTION h of X with derivative -I is either iota, or h has EXACTLY FOUR regular fixed points outside F, and g is odd. Consequently Fix(h) contained in F implies h=iota. This latter involution pairs only lifts of the SAME physical point.
Proposed proof: h commutes with iota. Let tau=h*iota; it has derivative I and tau^2=1. A nonidentity translation automorphism cannot fix a regular point. At a zero of order 2, normalize omega=z^2 dz. A translation automorphism fixing that zero acts as z -> zeta*z with zeta^3=1. An involution of this form must be identity locally and hence globally. Thus nonidentity tau acts freely on all X. Hence h has no fixed point in F. The map h descends to a nonidentity involution bar(h) on the hyperelliptic sphere (otherwise h is id or iota). A nonidentity Mobius involution has exactly two fixed points. Neither can be a branch value, since its unique preimage would be an h-fixed point in F. Each fixed base point has two regular preimages exchanged by iota. If h swapped them, tau would fix them, impossible. So h fixes both points in each fiber: exactly four regular fixed points outside F. The free quotient by tau and Riemann-Hurwitz give g odd.

P3 (scope): The global midpoint certificate for a connection x -> h(x) is automatic only if every possible h-fixed midpoint is forbidden. Fix(h) contained in F is a sufficient condition but not a necessary condition for darkness of a particular pair. P2 eliminates this automatic certificate for distinct physical endpoints on all such orthogonal rooms. It does NOT prove that a particular pair is lit; regular h-fixed points need not be reachable as midpoints. No theorem says arbitrary dark pairs arise from global half-turns. Thus P2 retires the mechanism at every genus, rather than proving g>=10. The known 22-wall dark room has g=10 and cannot have an additional involutive global half-turn at all if P2 is true.
