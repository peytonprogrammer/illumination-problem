Result
Let a Delta1 room have exactly the three interior A stars S=(0,0), U=(2,0), T=(2,2), with a centered two-triangle defect at L=(-2,0). All other occupied partial cells are single caps by the reviewed first-surplus classification. Every such room is illuminated if at least one of B+, E-, T- is missing. If these caps are present, the room is also illuminated whenever
 D- is present, Nb+ is absent, and R- is absent.             (1)
No W/k cap is needed for this implication. It covers arbitrary other single-cap choices consistent with the room hypotheses, not only the saved W17 fixture. It does not close the whole paired-left family.

The coordinate labels are those in zero-surplus-L/classification.txt:
 B+ = conv{(0,-2),(0,-1),(1,-1)};
 D- = conv{(2,-2),(2,-1),(1,-1)};
 Nb+ = conv{(0,2),(0,1),(1,1)};
 R- = conv{(4,0),(3,0),(3,-1)}.
The defect contains both L+,L-. Missing B+ or E- gives the existing one-bounce path. If B+,E- are present but T- is missing, the bottom cap is still a single cap, so the existing four-bounce path applies.

1. Route and exact parameter
For d in (3/17,3/16), start at S with velocity(d,-1).
(a) Enter B+ at(d,-1). Three reflections (diagonal, vertical, diagonal) return to(3d,-1) with velocity(d,1).
(b) Hit H's source roof at(5d,1), reflect to(d,-1). The roof lies above the left half of the notch; Nb+ absent preserves this mirror.
(c) Enter D- at(7d,-1). Three reflections (diagonal, vertical, diagonal) return to(9d,-1) with velocity(d,1).
(d) Hit the target roof at(13d,3), reflect to(d,-1).
(e) Hit the elbow right wall at(3,16-3/d), reflect to(-d,-1).
(f) Hit the elbow bottom wall at(6-17d,-1), reflect to(-d,1).
(g) Hit the target roof again at(6-21d,3), reflect to(-d,-1).
At height2 the last leg reaches (6-22d,2). The actual target equation is
 6-22d=2,
so d=2/11, strictly inside the stated interval. There are11 reflections:3+1+3+1+1+1+1. No source/target surrogate is used.

In a paired-left room all B/D/T cells are single. B+ and D- therefore keep their other cap walls intact and exclude B-/D+. T- excludes T+. Every external triangle outside the single-cap alphabet is absent except the two L caps, neither of which intersects any required-absent triangle of this route. Condition(1) supplies the two remaining mirrors, Nb+ and R- absent. All legs otherwise remain in H or the two stated bottom caps.

4. Exact implication and reflection
The existing rational path checker recomputes required/forbidden triangles of the saved path, agreeing exactly with the prior actual-boundary check. Required caps outside H are ONLY B+,D-. Forbidden single caps are B-,D+,Nb+,R-,T+. The other two forbidden triangles are outer triangles at B and D, absent in this paired-left class. Hence(1) suffices for the full family implication above.
Apply sigma(x,y)=(2-y,2-x) and reverse the path to exchange actual endpoints back to S,T. The paired-left defect becomes paired-top. The analogous condition is R+ present, Nr- absent, D+ absent, with E- supplying the second required bottom-cap image. No symmetry of the unknown room is assumed.
