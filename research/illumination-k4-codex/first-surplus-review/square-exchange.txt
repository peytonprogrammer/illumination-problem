1. Gap, new idea, and success criterion

This differs from a centered two-triangle fan, which is a larger right-angle triangle and has a different return map. Do not apply this exchange to centered fans.

2. Local exchange lemma, with strict branch conditions
Normalize the original triangular pocket to vertices (0,0),(1,0),(0,-1), portal y=0, and its square completion to [0,1] x [-1,0]. Enter at (u,0) with velocity (d,-1), where
 0<u<1, 0<u+2d<1.                              (2)
These conditions imply |d|<1/2 and 0<u+d<1.
In the triangle, the diagonal/vertical/diagonal reflection points are
 ((u+d)/(1+d), (u-1)/(1+d)),
 (0,u+d-1),
 ((u+d)/(1-d), (u+d)/(1-d)-1).
All lie strictly inside their sides by(2). The excursion returns to
 (u+2d,0) with velocity(d,1).
In the square, go straight to the far wall at (u+d,-1), reflect once, and return to the SAME (u+2d,0) with the SAME velocity(d,1).
Both square legs remain inside the square: their horizontal coordinates interpolate between u, u+d, u+2d, all strictly in(0,1). No side or vertex is encountered. Reflection swaps/negates the indicated velocity components, so the formulas prove exact specularity. Translation/orthogonal reflection transports the lemma to each physical cap.
This gives identical boundary data for the retained rest of the trajectory. It is not a claim of monotonic illumination under arbitrary enlargement.
