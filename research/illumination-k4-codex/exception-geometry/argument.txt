Class and notation
Use exactly the strictly simple canonical 45-degree triangle disk class in ../restricted-theorem-note/theorem.txt. I counts ALL interior A points, not only selected marks. J counts interior non-A integer points; s_N counts smooth boundary non-A integer points; k=J+s_N. At smooth boundary A points put charge3, and at A corners charge their positive incident triangle count. The sum is Q. Interior A stars have eight triangles and charge0 in Q.
The existing exact identity is
  W = 2 + 6I + Q - 2J - s_N
    = 2 + 6I + (Q+s_N) - 2k.                 (1)
Use cell coordinates A/2. Let F be the I full cells, and let B(F) be the external axis neighbors of F; b(F)=|B(F)|.

Lemma: Q+s_N >= b(F).
Partition B(F) into occupied partial cells and empty cells.
An occupied external cell cannot be full, by definition of F. Its A vertex is a boundary point with a nonempty contiguous proper fan. If it is smooth, its Q contribution is3; otherwise its Q contribution is the positive fan size. It therefore supplies at least one charge. Different cells supply charges at different A vertices.
For each empty external cell choose one of its axis-adjacent full cells. The midpoint C of their common cell side is a smooth physical boundary point: the full cell fills its two incident quadrants on one side and the empty cell fills neither quadrant on the other. Thus it contributes one to s_N. Different empty cells give different chosen side midpoints. A midpoint identifies a unique unordered cell pair; that pair has exactly one empty cell and one full cell, so two empty cells cannot be assigned to the same midpoint.
The A charges and C smooth-point charges lie in separate ledgers. Summing gives the lemma. This retains the actual external boundary size in the prior Q+s_N>=4 argument; it is a strengthening of that argument, not a new optical mechanism.
