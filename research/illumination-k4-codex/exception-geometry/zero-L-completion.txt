THEOREM
Let P be a finite canonical45-degree triangle union with strictly simple
polygon boundary, no holes/slits/contacts, and exactly the three interior
A stars centered at S=(0,0), U=(2,0), T=(2,2). Assume
  Q+s_N=8,
using the exact boundary-charge definitions in restricted-theorem-note.
Then S illuminates T by a corner-avoiding specular path with AT MOST
THIRTEEN reflections. No wall-count or exception-count cap is required
within this fixed-support equality class.
The other two pairs of interior A points are adjacent and directly
illuminated, so every pair of distinct interior A points in this class
illuminates.
This is the full zero-surplus L-support class, not just extensions of the
four-cap reference room. It does not cover positive-surplus L rooms,
other nonadjacent I3 supports, arbitrary interior marks, or the universal
22-wall minimum.

1. Existing structural reduction (independently accepted)
Write H for the three full A squares. Their exposed side count is8.
The exact nonnegative slack decomposition
 Q+s_N-8 = sum_partial_cells(q(v)-d(v)) + (s_N-|M|)
forces every partial cell into the seven external neighbors of H. Every
occupied one is a SINGLE triangle, except possibly the unique two-triangle
fan in the northwest cell A=(0,2). Smooth B points are prohibited.
The two-triangle fan, if present, fills the missing quadrant at(1,1),
opening the direct segment ST. Handle it immediately and assume henceforth
that every occupied external cell contains at most one triangle.
Thus every additional triangle is one of the16 half-side cap options in
../zero-surplus-L/classification.txt. The reduction includes all rooms,
not just the four-cap reference fixture. Its proof and independent review
are in that note and mirror-exchange.txt.

Use four labels:
 B+ = conv{(0,-2),(0,-1),(1,-1)},
 E- = conv{(4,2),(3,1),(3,2)},
 L+ = conv{(-2,0),(-1,0),(-1,1)},
 T- = conv{(2,4),(1,3),(2,3)}.
One-triangle-per-cell means B+ excludes the opposite bottom cap B-, and
E- excludes the opposite east cap E+. This fact protects cap mirrors.
No smooth B points further forbids L+ with Nb- and T- with Nr+ at the
northwest slot. These are geometric consequences of exact equality.

2. The first two caps are forced by one-reflection paths

If E- is absent, the route

Therefore any hypothetical dark equality room must contain BOTH B+,E-.

3. The four-reflection path forces T-
Assume B+ is present and T- is absent. Take
 (0,0)
 ->(2/5,-8/5)
 ->(0,-3/2)
 ->(2/3,-4/3)
 ->(7/4,3)
 ->(2,2).
Initial direction is(1,-4). The first three reflections are on the bottom
cap's diagonal, vertical, diagonal walls. The last is on the exposed top
wall of the target star. No point is a genuine room corner.

Why the mirrors are compulsory under these cap conditions:
- The two nonportal edges of B+ cannot have occupied triangles across
  them: these would be additional triangles in the same bottom A cell,
  contradicting the single-cap reduction. The opposite bottom B- is also
  excluded by that rule.
- The target-top bounce is at x=7/4 in the LEFT half of y=3. Its unique
  exterior triangle is T-, which is absent by assumption. The right-half
  top option T+ cannot cover that bounce.
- The other open legs lie in the three full stars H or the interior of
  B+. Other allowed cap additions cannot remove an interior segment.
Hence the path works for every equality completion with B+ present and
T- absent, not merely a particular room.

4. A reflected four-reflection path forces L+

 ->(-1,1/4)
 ->(10/3,4/3)

It requires only E- outside H and forbids only E+,L+ within the16 options.
E+ is excluded by the occupied E- cell. Hence whenever E- is present and

The room itself need not be symmetric: symmetry constructs a fixed path
whose tile requirements hold separately in the arbitrary completion.
The formula reflection uses d=1/4; no claim that time-reversing the whole
variable-endpoint beam keeps its source at S for every d is needed.

5. The remaining case is the already closed family
A hypothetical dark equality room must now contain all four caps
 B+,E-,T-,L+.
These are precisely the four caps of the reference room R. The whole
reference-extension family was independently proved illuminated in
mirror-exchange.txt, as follows:
- The fixed13-reflection path requires B+,T- and can be blocked only by
  the northwest bottom cap Nb+.
- Its sigma-reflected/reversed13-path requires E-,L+ and can be blocked

- Those blockers occupy the same A cell. Both together form the unique
  two-triangle bridge, opening direct ST illumination. If at most one is
  present, at least one13-path survives.
Thus the supposed dark room is illuminated in every case, contradiction.
