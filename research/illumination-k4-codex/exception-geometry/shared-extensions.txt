What the three shared extensions cost
4 November 2026

Result
All THREE shared ways of invalidating the saved 10- and 13-reflection
paths can occur within W=17,k=6. Two need one further specified triangle
to restore the exception budget. Each of the three resulting fixed rooms
has a legal S-to-T connection from an already-saved certificate, with
11,10,12 reflections respectively. All three paths pass exact triangle
and actual-boundary verification. These are explicit illuminated rooms,
not candidate dark rooms, and not an exhaustive classification of their
possible further completions.

The useful negative result is quantitative: simultaneously interrupting
these two saved paths can cost only ONE extra wall with k unchanged.
Neither a second independent obstruction cost nor W>=22 follows from
these two paths.

Only these three prescribed additions were inspected. The stated repair
below was chosen by hand. No coordinate/room/direction/word enumeration,

There are no smooth A vertices in these unions. The Euler identity
 W=2+6I+Q-2J-s_N
and the triangle incidence identity N=8I+Q+s_A hold in every row.

Hand explanation of the ledger changes
For A, the left cap's A fan grows from one to two triangles (Q increases
by one). Its mouth midpoint (-1,0), previously a 270-degree corner,
becomes interior (J increases by one; W decreases by one). Other affected
vertices retain their corner status. Thus k increases to seven.
B does the same at the bottom-cap midpoint (0,-1).

D turns the smooth C point (2,-1) into a 270-degree corner, adds a new
45-degree A corner at (2,-2), and leaves the shared B endpoint a genuine
corner. It therefore adds two walls and lowers s_N by one. This repairs
the k7 defect in either R+A or R+B, giving W17,k6.

C adds a 45-degree A corner at (0,2), turns the smooth C point (0,1) into
a reflex corner, and turns the existing B corner (-1,1) into a SMOOTH
boundary point. The two non-A exception changes cancel. The new total is
W17,k6 directly.

The scalar charge Q+s_N rises from eight in R to nine in each of the three
eligible modified rooms. Equivalently W+2k rises from28 to29. So these
shared interruptions use just one unit of the available charge surplus.

Do not reject the A/B branches merely because the immediate single-tile
unions have k7: adding another tile can reduce k. D explicitly demonstrates
that nonmonotonicity. Conversely, D is one available repair, not a proof
that every completion must contain D.

4. Why the roof extension is especially instructive
In R+C, the newly smooth B point (-1,1) lies on the extended diagonal mirror
 y=x+2.
The selected 12-reflection path actually REFLECTS at this point (bounce6).
Its incoming leg is from (15/7,-1), with direction proportional to(-11,7).
The outgoing leg goes to (5/9,-13/9), proportional to(7,-11), exactly the
component swap required by reflection in a slope-one mirror.
Thus the same addition that removes the old source-roof reflection also
creates a legal reflection at a formerly forbidden physical corner. The
new certificate uses this change. It is not safe to treat a triangle
addition purely as a lost trajectory or an extra permanent obstruction.
The A/B additions similarly create interior C points; the repaired rooms
have different cap/bridge connections, which the saved paths exploit.

5. What this closes and what remains
Closed: the three proposed shared interruptions can all fit the budget;
the three specified eligible completions are illuminated, with actual
fixed witnesses, not just reliance on the aggregate restricted theorem.
The two-path independent-cost shortcut is invalid for these fixtures.

Not closed: other repairs of A/B; additional triangles around R+C; unions
of several shared extensions; all supersets of any listed fixture; other
I2/I3 supports; a wall-budget guarantee of compatible cap entry/return;
the universal22-wall minimum. A connecting path in one union need not
survive another extension of a required reflecting wall.

Assessment
This bounded test supplies another precise warning against local cost
counting, and explains how a newly transparent point can supply the next
connection. It does not advance the certified W21/k6 exclusion to a
computer-free proof. Turning each lost witness into another saved-witness
lookup would reproduce the existing computer-assisted guard process.
Further proof work should require a structural argument controlling the
exchange between lost mirrors and newly legal interior/smooth points.
No larger trajectory or room campaign is launched from these results.
