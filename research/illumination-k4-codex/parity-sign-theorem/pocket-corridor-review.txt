# Review of §26.7: corridor-side literal leaves

**Verdict: ACCEPT, scoped, with five required text corrections.** None of the corrections changes the conclusion. Two of them fill steps the hand proof currently leaves implicit (items 2 and 3), and one fixes an overstatement about frame signs (item 4).

Everything below was re-derived by hand. Nothing was taken from the script.

## What checks out

**Sharper count, P_A = 8.**
- If P_A = 9, equality in (26.2) forces q_A = d for every partial. The equality types are (1,1), (2,0) and (4,0).
  - The m=3 types have charge 3 but degree at most 2, so they are excluded.
  - (2,0) needs in = e = 2. (4,0) needs h = 1 and in = e = 2.
- Every partial then has indegree at least 1, so Z = 0, and (24.3) gives exactly one indegree-2 vertex.
- The other eight partials must be (1,1) leaves. The degree sum is at most 8 + 8 + 3 = 19, but 11 vertices need 20. This does not depend on whether H is connected.
- In connected H, N = (2,2) is at distance (2,2) from S and (−2,2) from T. So it is not a portal-neighbor of either full fan and is distinct from the seven neighbors. Hence P_A ≥ 8.
- The resulting 9-edge tree is exactly the eight full-incident edges plus M→N. The leaves are forced to have in = 1 and out = 0, so (m,q) is (1,1) or (2,0).
- The charge totals hold: Q = 10 + r with r ≤ 2 for connected H, and Q = 11 + r with r ≤ 1 for disconnected H.

**L shapes.**
- If the portal at C = (5,0) is the upper one, (2,0) forces the square [5,6]×[0,1]. The alternative pair at (5,0) has q = 2 and out = 1, and it would make (5,0) interior.
- (1,1) forces the triangle (6,0), (5,0), (5,1).

**Corridor.** S square, M's upper half-square and T square are joined through the portals (1,0)–(1,1) and (3,0)–(3,1). The open strip (−1,5)×(0,1) has no lattice points. The legs S→(4,1) and S→(3,1) lie in it.

## Required corrections

1. **B is forced in each upper subcase; it is not a free choice.**
   - Upper rectangle forces B = (5,1). If B were (3,1), the point (5,1) would receive 90° from T and 90° from L, a smooth boundary B, which rawness forbids. So the upper-left quadrant at c is missing.
   - Upper triangle forces B = (3,1). B = (5,1) would give 90 + 45 + 45 = 180°, which is not literal and not raw. So the upper-right quadrant is missing.
   - Replace "the choice of B … changes neither the construction" with this case split. Both cone computations are still needed, once each.

2. **Lower case: justify that the flank is a wall.**
   - Reflecting at b = (3,1) requires the left ray (2,1)–(3,1) to be a physical wall. That holds only if N's single triangle is [N,(2,1),(1,1)].
   - This follows from the literal 225° at B. If N's triangle were on the right, (3,1) would have 270° and that segment would be a portal.
   - State this explicitly. The link [π, 9π/4] depends on it.

3. **Analogous upper-case flank check.**
   - When B = (5,1), the left ray (3,1)–(4,1) is a wall because the other A=(4,2) triangle is absent (the leaf has one triangle).
   - When B = (3,1), the same holds for (4,1)–(5,1).
   - Add one line covering both.

4. **Frame signs under symmetry.** "Q(η) = −2u, det = 2" holds only in the normalized orientation with p = 0.
   - For the (0,2) leaf, obtained by x→4−x plus reversing the path, the fixed reflections x=−2, x=−1, or the D H₀ D composite now come before the corner. Then Q = −2·L_p·u = +2u in the source frame, with w = (−4,1) or (−3,1), giving det = −2.
   - Vertical reflection keeps Q = −2u but flips det to −2.
   - State the result as Q = −2·L_p·u ≠ 0 and |det(w,Q)| = 2, with the slide sign selected accordingly.

5. **Notation collisions.**
   - The charge sum "Q = 10 + r" and the defect Q(η) share a symbol. Rename the charge sum, for example to Σq_A.
   - L's fixed mirrors "D, H₀" collide with §1's moving D_t, H_t and with the DHD excursion criterion. Rename them, for example to D_L and H_L.

## Script scope

The script only checks the prescribed arithmetic. It does not verify:
- rawness, B-forcing, or wall-versus-portal status (including x=5 in the lower case and the flank rays);
- the rectangle-case portal crossing at (5,¾);
- whether open segments stay off the boundary (its convexity check tests vertices only; this needs the hand argument);
- the cone sectors.

The `90+45!=225` and degree-total asserts are tautological. The `PENDING` labels are appropriate.

## Scope statement

The result covers the two corridor-side leaves (4,2) and (0,2) in connected H with W ≤ 26, together with their D4 images. W ≤ 26 is used, through P_A = 8, to make L a leaf.

It does not cover the opposite-side leaves (0,−2) and (4,−2), the end leaves (−2,0) and (6,0), disconnected H, W > 26, or universal detection. Those all remain open, as the section already says.
