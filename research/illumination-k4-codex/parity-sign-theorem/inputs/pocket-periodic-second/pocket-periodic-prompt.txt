## 20. Toward a general periodicity reduction: the precise compactness step

**Fixed-surface part.** As in Section19, if no marked lift is periodic,
all source and target lifts map through the canonical minimal translation
cover pi:X->Y to one point p fixed by the descended halfturn j. Thus it is
enough to establish that every regular point of pi^-1(Fix j) is M-periodic.
The fixed-surface factorization and rotational descent are in
[Apisa-Wright, Section3.1](https://websites.umich.edu/~alexmw/ApisaWright.pdf);
the minimal translation quotient and equivariance are also proved in
[Moller, Theorem2.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0410262).

**Proposed fold-locus proof.** Put d=deg(pi), and consider pointed surfaces
(X',x') over M carrying some halftranslation cover f of degree exactly2d
which folds at the regular mark:

    f(x'+u)=f(x'-u) for all sufficiently small u.

No minimality is imposed on f. This condition is GL2R invariant, since
-I is central. Its fiber over X is finite. Indeed a cover to a translation
surface of degree2d contradicts the factorization of pi through every
translation quotient. Otherwise f lifts to the orientation double of its
target, giving a translation quotient of degree d. That quotient must be
Y itself. The negating involution must be j, so f=q pi. Its regular folds
are precisely pi^-1(Fix j).

If this fold locus is closed in the smooth pointed stratum, the marked
orbit closure of such an x lies in a closed invariant set with finite
fiber over X. A1-point orbit closure can have dimension dim(M) or
only dim(M)+1. The latter is the full free-point marking, incompatible
with that finite fiber. Hence x is periodic.

**Where compactness must be proved.** For convergent pointed source surfaces,
pull back the degree2d covering equivalence relations to one compact
source and take a subsequential limit. At regular points their branches
are local maps u->+u+c or u->-u+c. The main local safeguard is elementary:
if two same-sign branches approach each other, their composition gives a
nonzero translation v_n->0 preserving the covering map on a fixed disk.
For sufficiently small v_n, the2d+1points z,z+v_n,...,z+2d v_n stay in that
disk and have the same image, contradicting degree2d. This replaces the
review's less direct area estimate. Nearby distinct folds are similarly
excluded because their composition is a small translation.
