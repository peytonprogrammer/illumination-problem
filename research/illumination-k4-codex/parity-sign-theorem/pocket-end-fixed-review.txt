## 4. Intrinsic π at C (not 3π)

- **Link:** At C the link is [0, 3π/2]. Its flanks are the moving ray 0 (H_t) and the fixed ray 3π/2 (x=5, pointing down).
- **Germs:** The source germ is π−β and the physical outgoing germ is π+β, with β = atan(2/7).

- **Sector:** The interval [π−β, 2π−β] has length π. Its part [π−β, 3π/2] lies in the original link. Its part [3π/2, 2π−β] lies in the adjacent copy's link [3π/2, 3π].

## Corrections (non-blocking)

1. **"The lower leaf may have one or two triangles" is looser than necessary.** Rawness forces m=1. The other possible second triangle, (4,−2),(3,−1),(3,−2), would make (3,−1) equal 90+45+45 = 180°, which is smooth and forbidden. Similarly, the S-left leaf is forced to m=1 by (−1,−1). The proof does not need either fact, but the sentence should say "rawness forces one triangle; the used flank is a wall in any case."
2. **Checker coverage should be stated honestly.**
   - The `a.bounce` triples run only within each half, so the corner-flank reflection at C is not tile-checked. Only `sectors['(5, 0)']==[6]` is asserted.
   - `dot(n,p)==c` checks the supporting line, not interiority of the wall. That relies on the semantics of `audit.py`, which I could not inspect.
   - The π-sector is not machine-checked.
   
   The hand checks above cover all three gaps. The status should say they are hand-verified.
3. **The §1 scope block uses a different normalization** (B₀=(1,1), u=(0,−1)). §26.10 inherits §26.9's u=(0,1), D_t: x+y=6+t, H_t: y=t. A single cross-reference sentence would prevent conflation.
