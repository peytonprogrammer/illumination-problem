# Review of §26.13 (hand only, no tools)

**Verdict: I accept §26.13 in its stated scope.** I found no substantive gap in the forcing, the path, the development, the defect or the π-sector. Several statements need correcting before the status changes. The most important are a mislabeled point, a dependency on the withdrawn §26.8, and an overstated coverage sentence. Universal detection stays open.

## 1. Forcing chain

The chain is correct. Forcing Sleft to m1 is genuinely needed: with m2, D_L would be an internal diagonal, not a mirror.

Two hidden premises should be stated explicitly:

- **Single portal.** A two-triangle leaf such as {5,6} at (0,2) uses both unit sides. It would contribute 45° at (1,1) and break the left/right dichotomy. The census premise "one portal along a full unit side" excludes it and must be cited.
- **(1,1) and (-1,1) are non-A.** In the fixture, the only smooth boundary lattice point is M=(2,0). So A must include M, and the forcing needs these two points outside A. §26.9 makes the analogous statement for (5,-1).

## Required corrections

1. **Mislabeled point.** "At B=(1,1)" should read "At (1,1)", since B=(5,1) throughout.
2. **Dependency on withdrawn §26.8.** Don't rely on it for N's right portal. Inline the (3,1) count: T 90 + M 90, plus 0 for a left N, gives a forbidden 180°.
3. **Unstated premises.** State the single-portal premise and that (±1,1) are non-A, as described in §1.
4. **Defect formula.** Write Q = Ṫ′ − 2·L_P u, where L_P = diag(−1,1). It equals u here only by coincidence, so writing "2(0,1)" as 2u invites exactly the frame error §26.9 warned about.
5. **"The prefix is essential" is overstated.** The prefix is an isometry, so |det| is the same in the corner's local frame: det((9,1),(1,−1)) = −10. What is essential is frame consistency. Mixing frames gives det((−9,1),(1,−1)) = 8.
6. **Coverage claim needs two scope fixes.**
   - Left-end pockets: add that they follow by the reflection x→4−x with S/T exchange. This reflection preserves M's upper half-star.
   - m2 end leaves: scope the claim to the literal m1 triangle pocket. The m2 {2,3} end leaf (the square [5,6]×[0,1]) is not covered unless the census excludes it.
   - The three-way partition (Tdown R; L with Sleft LOWER; L with Sleft UPPER) is complete only under correction 3.
7. **Minor text fixes.** Mention the first-half entry at (−1,1/9) and the crossing heights. The legs pass through the S and T squares, not just the "corridor".
8. **Checker.**
   - Q is computed from literals, not from the asserted affine derivatives. Derive it from T′(±1/100) and B′(±1/100).
   - developed_actual_target is hard-coded in the output.
   - Strict interior hits and wall-ness are delegated to audit.py's `segment` and `bounce`, which I could not inspect.
   - The corner sector and forcing are unchecked, as the script itself admits.
   - None of these change the hand result.

**Suggested status:** "Independent hand review accepts scoped, with corrections 1–7." Together with §26.9 and §26.10, this covers literal upper end pockets at either end of connected-H W≤26. Lower-pointing pockets, disconnected H and universal detection remain open.
