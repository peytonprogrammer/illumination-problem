## 1. Source/target wavefront index

**Missing step:** actual one-corner S/T existence, before pocket response.

**New geometric idea:** use the entire source/target distance-front cell
complex on the unfolding, together with its reflection quotient to a disk.
Try to assign a signed turn/index to the frontier separating the two mark
labels. A disk Euler-characteristic or boundary-order argument might force
a frontier vertex where the two selected arms bound exactly a pi-sector.
Raw parity would then force primitive equal halves, by existing main26.
The proposed new ingredient is a GLOBAL index for the frontier, not
straightening one minimizing curve.

**Strongest objection:** singular shortest paths may have several corners,
and a cut-locus vertex can have a sector of 3pi or more. An index may be
absorbed by those vertices or by the physical boundary. Equidistance alone
does not imply the required straight continuation. The index must retain
actual S/T labels, not just two arbitrary Voronoi cells.

**One bounded hand test:** write the complete local turn ledger at one
frontier vertex of cone multiplicity n and at a boundary endpoint. Determine
whether all non-pi configurations have a compatible one-sided index sign.
If a 3pi/multiple-corner configuration can supply the same index without a
legal event, reject the proposed sign argument. Do not construct a global
Voronoi implementation or enlarge a fixture census.

**Comparison:** this is distinct from the retired shortest-representative
proposal only if it uses the complete frontier and excludes index absorption.
If it assumes the minimizing path avoids corners, it is the same failed
proposal. No such exclusion has been established. Priority: low.

## 2. Portal-return intersection argument

**Missing step:** couple the actual endpoints to a nonzero pocket response,
without first proving that cylinder cores detect the pocket.

**New geometric idea:** retain position AND direction at the portal. The
source and time-reversed target define labeled curves in this two-dimensional
phase space on each finite itinerary domain. Compose the exterior return
with the six known pocket scattering branches. Seek a relative intersection
or winding invariant whose boundary contributions force a transverse,
one-corner connection when the pocket slides. This is a global argument
about the actual source/target curves, not another local return-map table.

**Strongest objection:** arbitrarily many returns produce additional
branches and possible cancellations. An ordinary intersection may represent
a multiple-corner limit, grazing, or escape to unbounded travel length.
A scattering map being area-preserving does not force an intersection for
these two specified curves. An invariant total intersection count might
also be unchanged by the slide and therefore say nothing about detection.

**One bounded hand test:** on the existing full six-branch portal square,
keep the exterior return symbolic and derive the signed boundary terms
of a proposed relative intersection count. Check whether they cancel or
include an uncontrolled contribution from the exterior/long-return boundary.
If the claimed nonzero term requires assuming the desired endpoint contrast,
retire this approach before computing any return map. A nonzero universal
local term is not assumed; an arbitrary abstract exterior map is not a room
counterexample.

**Comparison:** main2/3/29 already contain scattering and sealing identities;
main32 contains average-flux contrasts. This proposal earns separate status
only if the labeled global intersection invariant supplies existence rather
than restating one of those contrasts. No invariant with that property is
currently supplied. Priority: secondary screening only.

## 3. Close empty triangles instead of shortening arbitrary paths

**Missing step:** combine compatible actual connections into a SINGLE legal
midpoint event, preserving target labels and the pi-sector geometrically.
This would address realization rather than module dimension.

**New geometric idea:** use an empty torus triangle to turn two compatible
edges into the third. Let det(u,v)=1 and develop

    Delta = conv(0, 2u, 2u+2v).

Its only integer points are its three A vertices and its three edge
midpoints, of the three different nonzero parity classes. Its interior
contains no branch value of the raw torus cover. An oriented lift of its
interior is therefore an ordinary flat triangle. A boundary midpoint
continuation seen from its interior bounds a pi-sector.

**Conditional triangle-closing claim to screen independently:** suppose
actual oriented one-corner connections s->a and a->t develop along the
first two sides, and their chosen pi-sectors face the triangle interior.
Suppose a is regular on X, and the shared corner sector uses the same
developed chart. The remaining side should connect those SAME s,t through
its one B/C midpoint, with primitive half-vector u+v and an embedded
pi-sector. Require physical s,t to be the intended S,T lifts. If a final
non-wall direction is needed, retain that proviso rather than dropping it.

The hand reason is that the chosen side of the first edge fixes one lift
of the branch-free triangle interior. Regularity at a identifies its next
edge with the supplied second connection on that side. The third edge of
the same lift then closes at s. Primitivity follows from
 det(u,u+v)=1. There is no intermediate integer point on either half of
that third edge. This is a feasibility argument PENDING independent
review, not a newly accepted theorem.

**Strongest objection:** arbitrary connections do not have determinant-one
directions or compatible interior sectors. An intermediate A corner can be
a true zero, where the required angular sector may fail. A connected graph
of arbitrary seed paths is NOT enough. It is also unproved that the
room's planar fan structure supplies a sequence of these compatible moves
connecting the actual S and T. Further, existence of the third edge alone
does not give nonzero slide response: main26.1's midpoint offsets and
main31.1's signed collar counts must still be retained.

**One bounded hand test:** independently audit the conditional triangle
claim, especially extension across the three boundary midpoints, actual
endpoint identification, and the pi rather than 3pi sector. Then state
precisely the extra condition at a singular intermediate A vertex; do not
assume it automatically. Stop after this local admissibility check and
assessment of whether a planar global lemma is plausible. No slope, word,
triangle-chain or room search is authorized.

**Comparison:** this does not delete a physical leaf, replace a sealed wall,
shorten k by an affine map, or claim that group generation implies one-step
reachability. Its prospective ingredient is a geometric composition rule
with explicit empty-face and side-compatibility conditions. Merely naming
that missing compatibility a Farey rule would not count as progress.
