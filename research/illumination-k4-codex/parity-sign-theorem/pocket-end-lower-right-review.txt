## Appended §26.14 cone inequality (algebra verified)

**Entry.** A half leaving B with velocity (−b, a) meets y=1 at q = 5 − 2b/a. The condition 3 < q < 4 is equivalent to 1 < a/b < 2. ✓

**Hypotenuse hit.**
- Entering the triangle cap, the path meets the hypotenuse x−y=2 at a point with x_h − 3 = 2(a−b)/(a+b).
