## Precise remaining question

For a raw parity room, fix the source and target and the maximal physical wall directions. Let Q_delta be the period differential of an allowed wall/mark variation. A one-corner event gives a zero-holonomy relative class eta=gamma2-gamma1 with its actual labeled endpoint and corner lifts. The remaining target is:

    Q_delta(eta)=0 for every admissible corner event
        implies delta is a true family symmetry.

The reviewed alignment and cylinder-mixing theorems convert this vector rigidity statement into signed spanning and local isolation. They do not prove this rigidity premise themselves. The new fan tree controls axis trajectories, and the first task here was to test whether that information already determines all wall positions.

It does not. There are many deformations invisible to every axis-cylinder period, even in the small frozen examples. Actual corner relations must detect them.

## 1. General obstruction: twists cannot create new information on the axis-isoperiodic subspace

Let U be the absolute-homology span of all horizontal and vertical row-annulus cores. Define D0 to be the subspace of allowed room/mark variations satisfying

    Q_delta(u)=0 for every u in U.

Alignment makes this equivalent to setting all scalar axis-core length derivatives to zero. The four source/target coordinate velocities remain free in D0: absolute core periods do not depend on moving regular marks.

The two axis shear actions have the form I+N_h and I+N_v, with both N images contained in U. Hence for delta in D0 and every relative class z,

    Q_delta((I+N_h)z)=Q_delta(z),
    Q_delta((I+N_v)z)=Q_delta(z).

The same holds for inverses and all products. Thus Q_delta(g_*eta)=Q_delta(eta) throughout the group Gamma generated by these two axis shears.

An entire Gamma orbit of a seed class can therefore impose at most TWO scalar constraints on D0, one for each component of Q(eta). Changing its developed direction can reveal those two components through different determinants, but it cannot generate a third independent component constraint there.

If d0 is the dimension of D0 modulo the family symmetries lying in D0, any collection of m seed orbits capable of full rigidity must satisfy

    2m >= d0.                                      (1)

Otherwise a nonsymmetry variation in D0 annihilates all m vector seed defects, hence every row in their Gamma orbits. Such a family cannot even have ordinary full rank, much less positively span.

This is a bound on seed ORBITS under the specified two axis shears. It is not a count of all ray directions, all affine maps, or physical corners. Several independent seed orbits can use the same physical corner. The earlier eight-corners-plus-one-diagonal observation is not contradicted by this bound.

It assigns the polygon corners their exact offset derivatives, smooth boundary grid points their wall-fraction interpolations, and unmarked interior grid points zero auxiliary velocity.     dim D0 = (25-10)+4 = 19,

Uniform scaling is not in D0, since it changes the nonzero core lengths. The only family symmetries in D0 are the two translations. Bound(1) gives m>=9 for this room. No collection of eight seed Gamma-orbits can prove its full rigidity.

## 3. A hand mechanism for a hidden wall motion: sliding a triangular pocket

The frozen room contains the triangular protrusion with corners

    B=(1,1), A=(2,2), C=(1,2),

attached across the vertical segment x=1, 1<y<2. The physical boundary immediately below and above the attachment lies on x=1. Its two outer mirrors are

    D_d: y-x=d, d=0,       H_b: y=b, b=2.

Slide these three corners down by t, keeping the rest of the polygon and both interior marks fixed. Then

    B_t=(1,1-t), A_t=(2,2-t), C_t=(1,2-t),
    d=-t, b=2-t.

This is an allowed small fixed-direction deformation. In the supporting-line chart it is exactly delta h2=-1, delta h3=+1, with all other offsets and mark coordinates fixed. Strict simplicity and positive wall lengths persist for sufficiently small t.

Every generic axis trajectory entering the protrusion crosses its vertical attachment horizontally. Its excursion reflects in the diagonal, top and diagonal mirrors. The exact developed return transformation is

    D_d H_b D_d = V_(b-d),

where V_a is reflection in x=a. Since b-d=2 throughout the slide, the protrusion is an unchanged V_2 virtual mirror to every such axis trajectory. Its entry window slides, but every fixed generic base-cylinder itinerary remains inside that window for sufficiently small t. Axis paths outside the protrusion are unchanged. A generic axis path started inside this triangular protrusion eventually exits its attachment, so there is no additional isolated axis core trapped there.

Consequently every axis-cylinder period is exactly constant along this small family, even though the room shape changes. This is an exact geometric phenomenon, not just a zero first derivative.

The saved event indexed0 has Q_delta(eta_0)=(0,0), so its ENTIRE two-shear orbit is blind to the slide by Section1. A different saved event, indexed1, has

    Q_delta(eta_1)=(-1,-1),
    w_1=(-12,5), sigma_1=+1,
    g_1(delta)=-sigma_1 det(w_1,Q_delta(eta_1))=-17.

That event detects the slide and opens a connection in its negative-row direction. Its physical corner is(-1,2), outside the protrusion: the information comes from the coupled wall itinerary, not merely local corner motion. The exact stored forms and ranks verify these values.

This example rules out proving corner-period rigidity solely by tree connectivity and axis period lengths. It does not refute corner-period rigidity itself.

## 4. The stronger module version of the positivity theorem

The reviewed cylinder-mixing proof actually derives

    all admissible Gamma-orbit rows nonnegative on delta
        implies Q_delta(K)=0,

where K is the full linear span of all orbit classes of the chosen seeds. Therefore it is enough that period derivatives on K detect all nonsymmetry variations. The original scalar full-rank-seed condition, and the later vector full-rank-original-seed condition, are stronger sufficient premises.

K is computable from fixed classes without tracing new paths. Write the normalized core operators as

    T_h = N_h/a,    T_v = N_v/b.

Starting with the actual core coefficient vectors T_h eta_i and T_v eta_i, close their span under T_h,T_v. On core coefficients these operations are given exactly by the fixed horizontal/vertical intersection matrix and reciprocal cylinder widths. The full K is spanned by the original seed classes and these generated core combinations, because N_h and N_v are differences of actual shear actions and identity, and conversely every group word expands into these operators.

Relations among core classes cause no difficulty: they map to zero under actual period cochains, and the core operator maps respect homology. Computing in the larger free coefficient space may retain redundant states, but cannot introduce new period information.

## 6. What a general tree-based proof must still supply

The fan tree proves alignment, and cylinder mixing supplies signs once the orbit module is rigid. It cannot supply the missing independent seed classes on its own. A universal proof must show that actual admissible one-corner connections with the correct source, corner and target lifts generate enough information on the axis-isoperiodic wall/mark modes.

A sufficient strategy would prove both:

1. The generated core relations determine all axis-period ratios, modulo a common scale.
2. The selected actual corner defects then determine every remaining axis-isoperiodic variation modulo translations.

The frozen nine-seed packet satisfies both linear conditions. Neither seed realization nor either rank statement is proved universally. The triangular-pocket slide shows why a proof must retain wall-hit terms even when the physical corner is elsewhere. It also shows why one seed plus arbitrary powers of the two shears is insufficient.
