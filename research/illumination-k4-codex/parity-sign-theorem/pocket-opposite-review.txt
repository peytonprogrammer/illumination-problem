# Review of 26.8: two hard-leaf constructions (tools disabled, hand-checked)

**Verdicts**
- **Opposite-side leaf: ACCEPT**, as the conditional statement it makes.
- **End leaf: REJECT as stated, CONDITIONAL after correction.** One of its "fixed walls" is not forced by the stated hypotheses. In one admissible leaf shape the prescribed path hits a second physical corner.

The script passes, but it does not encode whether each segment is a wall or a portal, so it cannot catch this gap.

## 1. Opposite-side leaf: ACCEPT

**Forced local shape.** Literal B=(5,−1) needs 225°.
- T contributes 90°, from its two triangles at its corner B.
- The pocket contributes 45°, spanning directions 180° to 225°.
- The remaining 90° can only come from the (6,0) leaf. This forces the m=2 lower rectangle [5,6]×[−1,0], with its portal at x=5, −1<y<0.
- The physical link at B is therefore [0, 5π/4].
- The B=(3,−1) orientation gives 90+45=135° because M has no lower tiles. That exclusion is correct.

**First half: S → (5/3,1) → B.**

| Step | Check |

| Hit at (5/3,1) | Lies on y=1, 1<x<2, which is a wall by hypothesis. It misses corners (1,1) and (2,1). |
| Second leg | (10/3,−2) = (2/3)(5,−3). It crosses x=3 at y=1/5 (M–T portal) and y=0 at x=10/3. The rest lies in the T square. No interior integer points. |

- The third segment crosses x=5 at y=−1/5, which is on the portal.

- Durations: 3/15 + 2/15 + 5/15 + 2/15 + 3/15 = 1.

**Development and cone.**
- Reflecting T through the mirrors in reverse order gives (4,0) → (2,0) → (2,−2) → (2,2) → (10,2) → (10,−4) → (10,6). So T′=(10,6), and T′−B′ = (5,3) = w.

- The interval [−α, π−α] crosses that ray once, has length π, and embeds in the 10π cone. The 10π comes from the dihedral group of order 8 generated by the horizontal and diagonal flanks.

**Defect.**
- Every mirror line used is fixed under the slide. As C moves by t, the y=−1 hit stays on the shortened wall for |t|<1/3.
- L_p is reflection in y=1, so L_p·u = (1,0) and Q = (−2,0).
- det(w,Q) = 6. The direction (5,3) is not wall-parallel.

## 2. End leaf: REJECT as stated, CONDITIONAL after correction

**What checks out.**
- First half S → (5/2,1) → C:
  - (5/2,1) lies on the hypothesized wall y=1, 2<x<3.
  - The leg crosses the portal x=3 at y=4/5 and misses (2,1), (3,1) and (4,1).
  - C′=(5,2).
- Link at C is [0, 3π/2]. Reflecting across the down ray maps π+α to 2π−α. The interval [π−α, 2π−α] embeds.
  - The cone here is 6π because the two flanks at C are perpendicular (group order 4). This differs from the 10π cone at a 225° corner.
- Durations sum to 1. T′=(10,4). Halves are (5,2) and (5,2).
- Q=(0,2) is correct, including the frame sign. |det(w,Q)| = 10.
- The x=5 wall below C is forced, because the pocket is the m=1 upper leaf.

**Gap: the hit at (7/2,−1) assumes y=−1, 3<x<4 is a wall.** Nothing forces that. The (4,−2) leaf is one of the seven forced leaves, and its portal may be either of T's lower BC edges. The r-budget allows all of these shapes:

| Shape of the (4,−2) leaf | Outcome for the prescribed path |
|---|---|
| Right portal (4<x<5), m=1 or m=2 | The wall exists. The proof holds as written. |
| Left portal, m=1, triangle (4,−2),(4,−1),(3,−1) | The path enters the leaf. It hits x=4 at (4,−6/5), then x+y=2 at (24/7,−10/7). It exits through the portal at (18/5,−1) and reaches T. This is a different word but has the same actual endpoint and the same T′=(10,4): reflecting T in x+y=2 gives (2,−2), and then x=4 gives (6,−2), as before. Durations: 2/5, 1/10, 1/10, 4/35, 2/7, summing to 1. No integer points. The leaf mirrors are fixed under this slide, so Q=(0,2) is unchanged. The event survives, but via a new word that the script does not cover. |
| Left portal, m=2 quadrant [3,4]×[−2,−1] | Reflections at (4,−6/5), then x=3 at (3,−8/5), then the path reaches **(4,−2), a physical corner**. The prescribed event fails. This needs r=2: one from the forced (4,2) leaf (see below) plus this one. That gives W=26, which is within scope. |

**Required correction.** Either:
- add the hypothesis "y=−1, 3<x<4 is a wall", equivalently that the (4,−2) leaf uses the right portal; or
- split into cases: right portal (as written), left m=1 (the modified word above, which needs its own prescribed control), and left m=2 (open).

**Second correction: record the forced shape at B=(5,1).** For B=(5,1) to be 225°, T contributes 90° and the pocket 45°. The (4,2) leaf must supply the other 90°, so it is the m=2 quadrant [4,5]×[1,2] with its portal at y=1, 4<x<5. This forced shape does not affect the path, but it consumes one unit of r. That is exactly why the left-m=2 failure above stays within W≤26.

## 3. Script

The algebra is correct for what is specified. However, the script does not check:
- whether each hit segment is a wall or a portal;
- open-segment integer points or containment in the room;
- leaf shapes or the r-budget.

So `PRESCRIBED_HARD_LEAF_CONTROLS_PASS` should not be read as validating the end-leaf geometry. Suggested changes:
- Add the corrected hypothesis to `proof_scope`.
- If the case-split route is chosen, add the left-m=1 leaf word as a third prescribed case.

**Scope.** Both remain partial, bounded, connected-H results with a fixed central-flank hypothesis. Neither gives universal detection.
