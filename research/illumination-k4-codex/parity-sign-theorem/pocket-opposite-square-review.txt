## 4. Target-specific equality

- D₊V₅ acts as (x,y)↦(y+2, 8−x), with linear part a rotation by −90°.
- V₃H₂ acts as (x,y)↦(6−x, 4−y), with linear part −I.

These are different maps. Setting them equal gives x+y=4 and x−y=4, whose unique solution is (4,0)=T. Both send T to (2,4), and neither depends on t. The equality is correctly stated as target-specific, not whole-plane.

## 5. Case split

- **Opposite pocket at (4,−2):**
  - B=(3,−1) gives only 135°, so it is not literal.
  - B=(5,−1) needs 90° from the (6,0) leaf.
  - m=1 there gives 180°, which is forbidden.
  - The other m=2 fan with ring 3–5 has two portals and makes (5,0) an interior C point, which is forbidden.
  - So the leaf is exactly the rectangle [5,6]×[−1,0]. This is correct.
- **N-right (26.8):** that event uses only y=1, 1<x<2, the rectangle, T's walls, and the fixed walls y=−1, 3<x<4 and x=3, −1<y<0. It is independent of the upper leaf's m and of the S-side leaves, so it holds at W26.
- **N-left:** rawness at (3,1) forces the upper leaf onto its left portal. Then m=1 is handled by 26.11 and m=2 by 26.12.

## 6. Required corrections

1. **Uniqueness of the m=2 shape.** State why the left-portal m=2 upper leaf must be [3,4]×[1,2]. The only other m=2 fan containing the left portal triangle is ring 5–7. That fan has both portals, which makes (4,1) a 360° interior C point, and that is excluded.
2. **26.11 at W26.** 26.11's own consequence is stated only for W≤25. The combined claim needs its m=1 event at W26 as well, where the second m=2 leaf could be N's left square or an S-side leaf. Add a sentence saying the 26.11 event uses only:
   - y=1, 2<x<3
   - the rectangle
   - the pocket
   - the upper triangle
   - x=5, 0<y<1

   none of which change in those cases. I checked this and it holds. In 26.12 itself r=2 is saturated, so every other leaf is m=1.
3. **Dependency labelling.** Mark the combined consequence as conditional on 26.11's pending review and on the 26.7 tree. For the symmetry and endpoint-exchange step, note that the reversed event's defect is −2L_p-transported. Nonzero defect and |det|=4 survive, but the source-frame expression changes.

No universal or unbounded theorem follows from this. End pockets, disconnected H and W>26 remain open.
