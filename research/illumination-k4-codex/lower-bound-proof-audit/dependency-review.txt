Proof-hardening map for the at-most-six-exception lower bound

[done] Initial dependency assessment, Codex,2026-11-02. No solving, guard discovery, room enumeration or trajectory search was run. This report does not upgrade any existing verification claim.

Exact target
No strictly simple slit-free room formed from the fixed canonical45-degree lattice triangles, with two distinct interior even-even A marks and at most six non-A lattice vertices that are interior or smooth boundary points, can be dark with at most21 maximal walls. The lattice presentation and mark restriction are essential. This is not universal22-wall minimality.

Logical chain that needs independent justification
1. Every eligible polygon obeys the counting bounds, has I=2,3 or4 full A stars, and admits an allowed full-star support/marked-pair normalization.
2. Every eligible room is contained in the finite triangle universe for one listed support. Neither a coordinate cutoff, a hidden assumption on exception locations, nor symmetry normalization may remove it.
3. Its triangle assignment extends to a satisfying assignment of all necessary geometry CNF clauses. This is the semantic direction that matters. It is acceptable for the formula to admit spurious rooms. Byte-identical regeneration alone cannot prove that the encoding includes every legal room.
4. Every optical guard is a universally valid sufficient condition for an actual corner-avoiding connection between the physical marks. Thus a dark room satisfies each added negated guard. This includes smooth lattice hits, endpoint stars, U/Z triangle conditions, symmetry and mark reversal, and triangles outside the case universe.
5. Every added CNF clause is derived from such a guard or from justified geometry. The saved CNF has no unexplained pruning, solver assumptions, timeout exclusions or unreconciled late clauses.
6. A proof checker verifies a contradiction for that precise CNF, with frozen case/source/certificate hashes. The I4 cases and all symmetries/marked pairs must be accounted for.
