Independent implementation

Sorted canonical triangles receive variables1,...,n, matching the encoder's first variable allocation. Universe sizes match the previously checked bindings in every case.

For every saved guard and compatible mark orientation, the checker constructs the eight square-lattice isometries directly, translates at the physical source or exchanged target, and transforms every required and absent tile. All required tiles must be inside the finite universe; otherwise that guard instantiation is skipped. Absent tiles outside the universe are omitted because they are necessarily absent under the finite-support theorem. The resulting implication clause has negative required-tile literals and positive in-universe absent-tile literals.

The checker normalizes duplicate literals and clause ordering without changing logical content. Hash matches are followed by exact packed-clause comparison, so a hash alone never establishes provenance.
