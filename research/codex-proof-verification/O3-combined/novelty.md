# Novelty assessment: local O3 illumination within nine reflections

November 5, 2026. **Novelty is not established.** The exact restricted
quantitative lemma is a plausible original contribution, but the sources
checked do not justify a historical-priority or publishability claim.

## The claim being compared

The combined statement proves actual S/T illumination within nine
reflections for the established local two-root O3=B701 e2+e1+b1 class,
at fixed interior A stars S=(0,0), U=(2,0), T=(2,2), conditional on the
inherited root/ordinary-cap classification. It allows specific interior
or smooth non-A lattice points. It is not a claim that every polygon is
illuminable, a universal 22-wall minimum, or optimality of nine reflections.

## What is and is not potentially new

- Standard unfolding, rational reflection calculations, finite-wall
  checks, and the observation that a source-revisiting path can have
  its prefix deleted are established or elementary techniques. The
  eleven-to-four shortening is a useful verification finding, not a new
  mathematical principle.
- The potentially new content is the **specific exhaustive cover of
  this relaxed local family with one uniform bound**, and reusable
  support lemmas extracted from its paths. No matching result was found
  in the inspected sources.
- No optimality of nine was proved. It is an upper bound from the
  selected cover, not a minimum-reflection result or a record.
- No standalone publishability assessment is established. A specialist
  needs an intrinsic, self-contained definition of the class and its
  upstream classification proof; private labels and a conditional proof
  map alone are insufficient for that assessment. Completing a broader
  structural theorem could make these lemmas more significant.

As an internal cross-check only, seven existing named project proof
catalogues containing 18 path fields had no exact coordinate match to
the four-reflection suffix. This was not a scan of raw trajectory
archives, and it establishes neither project-wide priority nor literature
novelty. Published prior work already contains dark-room constructions and related parity arguments. This does not settle the novelty of the different positive illumination lemma.
