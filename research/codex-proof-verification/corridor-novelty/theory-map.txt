Primary-source theory map, 2026-11-06. Explanatory follow-up, not a new proof or exhaustive literature comparison.

Rational polygon means rational angles, not rational lengths. Our orthogonal corridor qualifies even for irrational dimensions.

1. Unfolding: reflect polygon copies and glue to a translation surface. A billiard connection becomes a straight geodesic between suitable endpoint lifts, with polygon corner restrictions accounted for. Source Wolecki section 2 https://arxiv.org/html/1905.09358v3 . This is a framework, not a proof of complete illumination.
2. Lelièvre-Monteil-Weiss Theorem 2: each point of any translation surface has only finitely many unilluminated targets; structure of dark-pair locus. https://arxiv.org/html/1407.2975 . Wolecki Theorem 1.2 strengthens the rational polygon conclusion to finitely many TOTAL dark pairs. Neither gives emptiness of our outer-pair set or an explicit exhaustive list by itself.
3. Apisa-Wright, Marked points on translation surfaces, Theorems 3.6 and 3.16, section 3: in the non-torus-cover setting the finite-blocking alternatives involve periodic points/zeros or identification under minimal quotient maps. https://arxiv.org/html/1708.03411v3 . Darkness is blocking cardinality zero, but finite blocking by a nonempty set does not imply darkness. Our candidate application requires classification of actual corridor orbit closures, periodic points and covering maps, accounting for every endpoint lift, plus torus-cover parameter cases. No such complete implication established in this follow-up.
4. Hubert-Schmoll-Troubetzkoy: affine group orbit methods on Veech/prelattice surfaces, with quantitative Kronecker lemma 29. Full HTML now accessible at https://arxiv.org/html/math/0602394 ; earlier audit had abstract only. Theorem 2 gives per-point finite dark targets for Veech surfaces, not zero. Lemma 29 analyzes torus rotations and finite exceptional parameters, relevant to our phase/portal strategy. Conditions such as Veech/prelattice must be proved for a given parameter class, not inferred from the corridor outline.

Judgment: covering/periodic-point classification is the most consequential possible route to an existing-theory implication or broader extension; quantitative arithmetic is the closest route to simplifying the constructive argument. This is a proposed research direction, not a verified corollary.
