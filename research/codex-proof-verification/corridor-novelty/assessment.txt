Corridor theorem: mathematical scope

The four-parameter twelve-wall corridor theorem concerns actual outer-chamber source and target positions, with arbitrary real parameters satisfying its stated hypotheses. The constructive argument includes exact wall and corner guards.

General finiteness of dark partners in rational polygons does not by itself prove that this particular family has no dark outer-chamber pairs. The source-to-target conclusion requires the corridor argument or an additional implication from the general theory.

Related mathematical sources: Wolecki, Illumination in Rational Billiards, https://arxiv.org/html/1905.09358v3; Lelievre, Monteil and Weiss, Everything is illuminated, https://arxiv.org/html/1407.2975.
