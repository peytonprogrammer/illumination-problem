Label descent and a finite repair
Coordinator, 2026-11-07. Universal lower bound remains OPEN.

1. Reflection obstruction verified by hand

For A_n=[[1,n],[0,1]] and R=diag(1,-1), direct multiplication gives
A_n R A_n^-1=[[1,-2n],[0,-1]]. On the square torus with period48,
T^48 restores the horizontal/vertical permutations and all color labels.
Nevertheless the transported reflection derivative becomes[[1,-96],[0,-1]].
Different derivatives cannot be identified by a translation isomorphism.
Thus the FULL transported reflection is not a function of that coarse finite
state. This confirms the peer's obstruction. It does not invalidate functions
that use only the finite labelled surface.

2. Corner identities also need a descent check

The saved known22 certificate has10368 rooted states(h,v,color), each with
60 squares. Its color records four actual source lifts, four actual target
lifts, and forbidden square corners. C=v h v^-1 h^-1 identifies genuine zeros
as its nine length3 cycles; forbidden regular corners are transparent.

Anchored T:(h,v)->(h,v h^-1), U:(h,v)->(h v^-1,v) preserve C EXACTLY
before the common rooted relabelling. Individual zero/source/target IDs may
therefore be transported exactly. But a closed coarse-state word can permute
them. Our audit fixes one spanning-tree transport, checks ALL20736 existing
T/U edges, and stores a12-step closed-word witness. It returns to exactly
the initial h,v,color while swapping zero IDs0/7 and2/4, cycling3/6/5,
and swapping target lifts13/16 and14/15. Source IDs remain fixed.

The resulting identity-transport orbits under all fundamental loops are:
 zeros {0,1,7} and {2,3,4,5,6,8};
 source IDs {9},{10},{11},{12};
 target IDs {13,16} and {14,15}.
These numbers are features of THIS saved example and rooted conventions.
They are not a universal3+6 lower bound. The zero names themselves are arbitrary.

This explains a limitation of the preceding singleton-witness packet: each
witness is valid along its saved shear path, but its named corner cannot be
silently treated as a single-valued feature on the coarse state space.
The packet already specifies its chosen transport; its replay results survive.

3. What is now justified

Option A: Require a proposed feature to be invariant under every closed-word
identity permutation. Fixed identity weights must agree within each of the
orbits just listed. This leaves two possible zero weight classes here, not one.
General permutation-invariant relational features need not reduce to counts.
This observation is about identity-weight descent, not a proof that every
weighted aggregate distinguishes all required states.

Option B: Enrich the state with all r zero IDs and all four source/four target
IDs, retaining the entire cover and every regular sheet. There are at most
r!*(4!)^2 refinements of each coarse state, so its SL2 orbit remains finite.
T,U remain bijections on the refined orbit, and telescoping averages are valid
there. No refined orbit was enumerated. This is a finite encoding repair,
not a new illumination theorem or a degree-independent complexity bound.

The half-turn rho is different from the problematic reflection: its derivative
-I commutes with every shear. It preserves the source fiber. With a regular
root, an affine map with specified derivative is uniquely determined by the
image of that root: two maps agreeing there differ by a translation map fixing
a regular point, hence agree locally and by continuation everywhere. Thus rho
has at most four choices per coarse state, according to which source lift is
its image. Equivalently one may store its full finite vertex/sector action.
No full physical reflection or geometric planar-wall characterization is
claimed to follow from rho alone.

4. The positive step still missing

A finite bookkeeping repair does not produce the nine-corner inequality.
An arbitrary potential obtained by solving on a state graph merely restates
the desired average illumination inequality. A useful next candidate must
specify an actual relational feature (which source, which zero, and which
incoming sector or ordered intervening marks), prove its shear transition
rule, and obtain positive charge for every required low-wall orbit.

There is a second scope guard: a shear of an orthogonal unfolding need not
itself be the unfolding of an orthogonal Euclidean room. The entire orbit
still transports straight trajectories back to the original surface, but a
geometric lemma restricted to physical orthogonal representatives cannot be
applied pointwise throughout it without proof. An invariant assertion that
the orbit CONTAINS a physical representative is legitimate but not supplied
by a generic passport, the half-turn, or the labels alone.

Verdict: verified two descent issues and a valid finite repair; no positive
local charge, universal illumination result, new wall bound or novelty claim.
