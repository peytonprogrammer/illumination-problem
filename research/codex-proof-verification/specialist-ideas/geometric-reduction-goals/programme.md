## Target and meaning of progress

The first target is the orthogonal subcase of the canonical45 problem:
every finite strictly simple orthogonal lattice room D with r<=8 reflex
corners illuminates each specified distinct actual interior even-even pair
S,T, with no size, area, diameter, support, surplus or exception cap.
Since W=2r+4, this is the orthogonal part of W<=21. General diagonal-wall
canonical45 rooms remain outside this first target and remain open.

Seek a reduction D->D' preserving the actual S,T and the required room class,
strictly decreasing a specified well-founded complexity, and prove

    existence of a legal S-to-T ray in D'
        implies existence of a legal S-to-T ray in D.

A proof can select a different ray, direction and reflection word. It must
reach actual T with legal finite specular reflections and correct corner
conventions. A broken chosen path disproves only that proposed lifting map.
No inference from a disconnected sheet, mixed-direction walk, generic
torus-cover theorem, finite certificate, or unchanged endpoint projection.

Global induction additionally needs a proved availability theorem: every
remaining non-base room in the target class has at least one admissible
reduction. Endpoint-containing ears, thin corridors, interacting reflex
corners, remote attachments, and regular periodic rays cannot be omitted.
The base case must be proved; for a convex room the direct segment suffices.
If r is the complexity, require r(D')<r(D). A different complexity needs a
well-foundedness proof and proof that reductions remain in the induction
domain. No circular use of the desired illumination theorem.
