1. An exact all-direction arc model (hand derivation to challenge)
===============================================================
Use the mark-compatible torus E=R^2/(2Z^2) in the integer orthogonal wall frame. Absolute periods are even integral; corner images are two-torsion; the specified even-even A endpoints map to0. This is NOT necessarily the primitive period torus. Let M be the minimal four-copy unfolding, rho its intrinsic half-turn, Q=M/rho (a sphere), B=E/(-I), and h:Q->B the induced cover. The existing passport has local degrees1 (convex corner),2 (regular),3 (reflex). There are r+4 degree-one points and r degree-three points; degree-two count remains unbounded and all labels are retained.

For each primitive integer w=(p,q), the torus segment t*w, 0<=t<=1, projects to an embedded arc alpha_w in B from0 to the nonzero half-period [w]. No other two-torsion point is interior: if tp,tq are both integers then primitivity makes t integer. Nor do two interior points identify under +/- modulo2: t1-t2 or t1+t2 would have to be an even integer. Hence the interior avoids every branch value.

The full inverse image h^{-1}(alpha_w) is a finite graph. Its vertices over the two arc ends have valences equal to their local degrees. At degree2 the quadratic differential is regular and the two incident germs form a straight line. Degree3 is a true zero and stops light. Degree1 is a pi cone whose continuation on M retraces on Q; it cannot be an interior vertex of a simple graph path between distinct degree2 marks.

The two actual interior S lifts in Q and the two T lifts in Q are distinct labelled degree2 vertices over0. A component of h^{-1}(alpha_w) after excluding degree3 vertices containing BOTH colors supplies a straight connection: take a simple graph path, whose interior vertices all have degree2, then lift it to M and fold to the original room. No physical direction switch occurs. Conversely, a straight M connection projects to a nonzero torus loop with holonomy2d*w. Its repeated two-torsion crossings trace the SAME alpha_w forwards/backwards; zero avoidance keeps its endpoints in the same degree3-deleted graph component. Removable-corner retracing is handled on FULL physical fibers by the accepted cone-only equivalence.

Thus physical darkness is persistent monochromaticity of these graph components for EVERY primitive w. This is an equivalent direction encoding, not a new illumination theorem. Audit sector conventions, all sheets, degree-one retracing, torus choice and endpoints before using it.

2. Primary new proof target: exchange/discharging over slopes
==========================================================
Keep the WHOLE labelled cover and its real involution (the double of the actual polygon), not its unlabelled passport. Primitive pillowcase arcs are related by modular/Farey changes of slope; cover monodromy must be transported exactly. Derive how source/target monochromatic chains can change under these moves. Seek a discharging inequality showing that persistent monochromaticity for all slopes requires at least nine degree3 defects.

This is genuinely the missing step. Nine is a CONJECTURED threshold motivated by W=2r+4 and the known22 control, not proved by the passport. A single abstract graph can separate colors with fewer defects. The burden is simultaneous, physically realizable separation across all slopes. An explicit smaller physical cover violating the proposed charge retires it; a cover without the physical real structure is only a scope test.

A useful rigorous algebraic shape for the desired inequality is an orbit potential. On the complete finite labelled SL2Z orbit O, let H(s)>=0 count horizontal S-to-T connections before genuine zeros (or a Boolean indicator). Its sum is positive iff some direction illuminates, using the accepted exact decider. For bijective anchored shears T,U, the orbit sums of Phi(Ts)-Phi(s) and Psi(Us)-Psi(s) vanish. A PROVED local inequality

 H(s) >= (9-r)*a(s) + Phi(Ts)-Phi(s) + Psi(Us)-Psi(s),

with a>=0 and positive orbit sum, would exclude darkness when r<=8. The weight a may shrink with cover degree; no W-only positive density or orbit-size bound is assumed. No such inequality is currently supplied. The research is to derive one from labelled chain changes/physical symmetry, or disprove a concrete proposed potential. This method does not bound orbit size or infer illumination from mixing directions along one path. It averages exact certificates after changing the direction of the ENTIRE trajectory.

Candidate potential features must include source and target color runs, true-zero separatrix incidences and regular periodic components; raw genus, unlabelled connectivity and ramification totals alone have already failed. Do not fit coefficients on a few examples and call it universal; a symbolic transition proof is mandatory. No large new engine is needed for the first hand analysis.

4. Backup: use genuine affine conjugacies, not concatenated return maps
=====================================================================
A valid alternative to the failed monodromy argument is to transport BOTH marks by the SAME actual affine automorphism f of the surface. If f(x),f(y) lie in one open flat cylinder, they connect there (lift the cylinder to an infinite strip); applying f^{-1} gives one straight x-to-y segment. Composition of such automorphisms is legitimate because it acts on an entire segment, not on successive legs of a light ray. This is the known affine-orbit strategy, not a novel foundation.

A concrete new task would be a cylinder-incidence lower bound for the COMPLETE physical pair orbit at r<=8. It must not assume the affine orbit is dense (these marks are arithmetic), that chosen parabolic powers move every marked vertex, or that every connecting segment lies in a cylinder. The criterion is sufficient; prove completeness or use a separate saddle-chain contribution. If it only reconstructs the existing finite decider, do not count that as progress.
