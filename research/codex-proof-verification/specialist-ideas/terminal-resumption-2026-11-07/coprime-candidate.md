# A coprime-period construction for the spiral terminal pair

Candidate declared before prescribed tests. This is a new positive selection argument, not a larger ray cutoff or a repaired monotone iteration. No discovery search is proposed.

## Terminal-pair lemma

Let a>0 be rational, N>=5 an odd integer, l=(N-2)a, w=(N-1)a, A=Na. Let 0<b<c<h<H be integers. Put G=2(H-h) and assume gcd(G,N)=1. Use the original ten-vertex spiral polygon

(a,b),(a,h),(w,h),(w,c),(l,c),(l,0),(A,0),(A,H),(0,H),(0,b).

For every rational source S=(s,u), 0<s<a, b<u<=h with u integral, and every rational target T=(t,v), l<t<A, 0<v<c with v integral and c-v odd, claim that an actual finite corner-avoiding one-roof specular path connects S to T. There is no aspect-ratio, area, winding or reflection cutoff.

The choice of actual slope is arithmetic: q in 0..D-1 avoids three congruence classes; a coprime cap rotation selects M in 0..N-1; one further period of N fixes the actual target-image parity. These are finite constructive choices derived in the proof, not evidence from sampling rooms.

## Whole native-pair corollary

If a is a positive integer, c is odd and H=h+1, then EVERY distinct actual interior even-even S,T in this entire variable-metric room family illuminates. All other strict orders above remain. The cap has no integer-height interior marks, so accepted protected U/L coverage leaves only the terminal pairs of the lemma. The u=h artificial seam is included in the lemma.

This is a proposed complete optical theorem for this precisely restricted family of ten-wall lattice rooms. It does not prove all ten-wall rooms, arbitrary real endpoint pairs, all r<=8 rooms, or novelty. Independent mathematical review is still required.

## Principal audit risks

1. A union of three forbidden residue classes must not cover all q; justify the small covering classification and exclude its dangerous (2,4,4) pattern using even G and odd c-v.
2. Shifting slope by 2aM must preserve the relevant source/right and target congruences while actually reaching the cap window.
3. The final 2aN shift must preserve all portals and flip target-image parity.
4. Actual finite walls, signed velocities, roof corners, u=h and first actual T must be checked.
5. The whole-family corollary uses native even-even marks and the thin cap; do not silently extend it to arbitrary real marks.
