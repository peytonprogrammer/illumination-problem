# A bounded test of the 20-wall illumination conjecture

**Recommendation for Daniel.** A bounded run can disprove the working goal with one rigorously dark pair. A positive run can prove a specified finite slice, including arbitrary real endpoints if its continuum certificates close. There is no justified upper area bound for a positive proof of the working goal. I recommend one complete slice with a known room count and an explicit endpoint-coverage test. I would decline an exhaustive run through area 36. All figures below come from existing records or arithmetic on them. No new timing or search was performed.

## Scope and the missing reduction

The working goal concerns all distinct interior points in every strictly simple orthogonal lattice polygon with at most 20 maximal walls. With r reflex corners, W = 2r+4. Translate the room, apply the project's normalization (divide horizontal and vertical coordinates by their respective maximal-wall-length gcds, transforming the marks too), and quotient by the eight square symmetries. Axis scaling preserves straightness and reflection in horizontal and vertical walls. Arbitrary real marks stay admissible; lattice marks can become nonlattice. Classify marks **after** normalization. A finite denominator cutoff would miss rational pairs, and the reviewed real-to-rational theorem supplies no such cutoff. **Open BBBB target.** Normalized area n ≥ 21, 3 ≤ r ≤ 8, every canonical factor N_x equal to the base pillowcase B. A regular point of each parity is a cheap necessary filter; it also admits rooms with a proper factor. The open area has no upper bound. Even settling this same-class lattice branch would leave other endpoint obligations.

**Proposed finite slice.** Exactly n = 21, r = 3 (W = 10), all true BBBB rooms, all distinct interior real endpoints. This is the first open wall count above the eight-wall theorem. It gives an independently countable denominator and tests the difficult continuum step. It does not exercise the 20-wall boundary. Use d73's unique corner-coordinate skeleton and positive integer row heights and column widths, connectivity, no holes or pinches, canonical translation/D4 representative, and both gcds one. Keep rooms with zero or one interior lattice point. The stock d73 output suppresses them; use the pre-eligibility stream. At r = 3, 21 > 4r+4 = 16, so every normalized room in this slice is true BBBB by the project's reduction. Use direct four-factor computation for reviewer checks or if the reduction and implementation disagree. ## Certificate order

1. Join prior evidence by canonical room cells, transformed endpoints, and exact claim scope. Aggregate counts alone cannot clear a pair. Start with line of sight and same-rectangle coverage. Apply named family certificates and direct full-column placement, then the chain width test, then a single prescribed reversal facing the same physical run. Try both coordinate axes. For an isolated pair save strict rational overlap inequalities or a replayable unfolding. For a product of endpoint regions prove the inequalities throughout it, including owned lower-dimensional faces. A witness at a sample point clears only that point.
2. Reuse saved direction witnesses first. For remaining lattice pairs, batch directions by room, starting with the axes and primitive signed directions of height at most 12, escalating residuals to 60. Check a legal straight leaf or lifts in a common open cylinder (CC); reject corner hits exactly. The strip between two such lifts gives a legal segment. For same-parity lattice marks annotate a saved cylinder with the mod-4 criterion on displacement and circumference if wanted for diagnosis. A failed midpoint test costs **zero** previously lit pairs: CC already suffices. Mark different-parity pairs separately. [Cylinder certificates](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d72-cylinder-test/README.md) · [Winding test](two-class.md)
3. For the continuum, try whole-room cylinder covers before subdividing. Fall back to the existing exact tile-pair, rational-box, reciprocity and endpoint-dependent shear methods. Use both endpoint orders as required, the same-tile case, internal grid lines and vertices. Certify all off-diagonal interior points, or leave explicit residual boxes/faces. For isolated rational survivors use an exact path or a proved decision procedure applicable to those marks. Scaling rational endpoints to lattice coordinates is valid for replaying a path, but changes the area and cannot justify appealing back to this finite slice. [Continuum procedure and replay](../twenty/method.md)
4. For an unresolved same-parity lattice pair, try the d66 marked pure-braid orbit with a proposed ceiling of 20,000 states per lift-pair run. A positive witness suffices. A negative needs a complete orbit and all admissible target classes and endpoint lifts, plus an independent verification of the decision theorem's hypotheses. Exhausting the common-cylinder orbit alone cannot certify darkness. For different-parity or nonlattice survivors without a matching exhaustive decision theorem, retain the exact residual. Timeouts, exhausted direction bounds, arithmetic faults, memory ceilings and open boxes all mean **unresolved**. For scale, d73 estimated **10¹¹–10¹²** free rooms across areas 21–36 before exact normalized true-BBBB classification. Its measured shooting rates of roughly 10–53 microseconds per room imply about **278–14,722 CPU-hours** across the extreme combinations, before continuum verification, classification or replay. The 2,466 geometric BBBB examples with proper factors in d72 came from a covering-room dataset selected for factor structure. Their frequency is unsuitable for projecting savings. This plan avoids the large volume only by narrowing the claim.

**Proposed hard limit:** 2 aggregate CPU-hours, 3 hours elapsed, one worker, 2 GiB RSS and 4 GiB output. These are cutoffs, not measured requirements. Budget 0.10 CPU-h for enumeration, reconciliation and known certificates, 0.20 for placement and lattice tests, 1.35 for continuum and exact residuals, 0.35 for verification and shutdown. The d73 shooting rate alone corresponds to about 0.35 seconds for 34,501 rooms at 10 microseconds per room; it excludes almost everything in the proposed pipeline. No defensible all-real completion time is available. Within the cap, log per-stage CPU, peak RSS, disk and certificate sizes for the first 128 canonical rooms before committing the remaining allocation. Use that prefix for throughput only. Stop if even its evidence cannot be independently replayed within budget.

## Checkpoints, review and verdict

- Freeze source hashes, compiler and flags, theorem versions, exact eligibility rules, resource settings and deterministic room order before execution. Correct and review d73's boundary shift at j=0 in line_counts (the expression shifts by j−1 before masking); require defined integer arithmetic. Compare the unnormalized count against 34,501. Independently enumerate this slice by vertex edge lengths or cell growth, or supply an independently checked skeleton completeness proof and verify every enumerator branch/prune. Counts alone cannot detect a missing shape replaced by a duplicate.
- Stream shape records and write content-addressed evidence. Commit a checkpoint per 128 rooms and before each expensive fallback, using atomic completion records. Keep the skeleton/dimension cursor, canonical room key, pair or tile ID, live residue, solver settings, CPU totals and checksums. On resume, replay completed certificates, continue the first incomplete item, and charge prior CPU to the same cap. Reserve the last verification allocation even if there are outstanding rooms.
- A reviewer should regenerate the canonical room list and parity/factor labels, compare sorted hashes and cardinalities, reconstruct all eligible pairs/tiles independently, and reconcile every ID with an exclusion, certificate or residual. Replay discrete witnesses with d67's independent exact planar tracer; for continuum certificates independently verify rational inequalities, coverage and face ownership, ideally with d74-style arithmetic. Include R22's known dark pair as a negative control and a saved strip/reversal witness as a positive control. Require zero silent drops and record any dependence on shared code or an unreviewed lemma.
- **Finite success:** every normalized room in this slice reconciles and has independently checkable coverage for every distinct interior pair. If only all lattice pairs clear, report exactly that narrower result. **Counterexample:** any in-scope room and distinct interior marks with an independently reproduced proof that every legal path is excluded. A failed search is insufficient. **Inconclusive:** publish completed coverage and exact survivors on any cap, incomplete orbit, replay mismatch or uncovered stratum.
- **Universal success requires a new theorem.** One route would give a computable area bound for a minimal counterexample, preserve the relevant endpoint types under the reduction, then cover every remaining area and reflex count including non-BBBB continuum obligations. Another would certify whole skeleton families for all positive integer widths and heights and all endpoint strata. Neither reduction is available. A clean finite-slice run by itself gives no authority to extend the cap or claim the working goal.

**Decision requested:** approve the capped area-21, ten-wall all-endpoint slice only if a finite all-real result or an explicit surviving endpoint region is worth the budget. If the sole acceptable positive outcome is the universal theorem, defer computation until there is a finite reduction.
