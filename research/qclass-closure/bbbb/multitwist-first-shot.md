# One multitwist misses the first shot

## The exact pencil test

Use a horizontal pointwise rim-fixing multitwist H on the translation unfolding, marking **all** physical corner lifts as stops but allowing launch and arrival at the interior marks. In cylinder coordinates of circumference C and height h, its derivative is the shear c with ch/C integral in each cylinder. The shift at signed height y from the chosen rim is cy modulo C. Thus a mark at rational height fraction y/h=u/v in lowest terms has period v/gcd(v,ch/C) under H. A point on the stopped horizontal critical graph is fixed. For each choice of lifts the two rotations use the same exponent; even long individual orbits need not give independently specifiable positions.

Affine invariance does give the proposed equivalence: (kc,1) lights a lift pair iff the inverse images by H^k lie on one unobstructed vertical leaf segment. In a vertical cylinder both must have the **same transverse coordinate**. Within a stopped vertical boundary leaf, they must lie on the same unbroken segment. Coincidence of cylinder labels alone does not suffice. In an orthogonal room a vertical billiard ray has constant physical x throughout its life, also after any allowed reflection. These facts make the following all-power tests exact.

## Three BBBB controls

* **D129 dumbbell.** The marks S=(a,2) and T=(3a+2,2) both lie on stopped horizontal trajectories: at height 2 the lower lips of the two mouths are reflex tips. Hence H fixes **all** lifts of both marks pointwise. Their physical abscissas differ, so the vertical reference test fails and every direction (kc,1) fails. For a second check, the band 2<y<3 has physical length 4a+3 and doubled circumference 2(4a+3), so a rim-fixing c is an integer multiple of 2(4a+3). The reviewed one-reflection direction (a+1,1) is strictly between the zero and first positive shear. This holds for every parameter of the reviewed normalized family. [D129 construction](thin-gap-periods.md)
* **DB55.** For (2,2) and (10,2) the two lower lips again fix all marked lifts under H. In fact the least horizontal rim-fixing shear is 130 and fixes every lattice lift in this room. A vertical segment cannot pass between the two abscissas. The entire pencil therefore misses, while the reviewed (3,2) direction lights the pair. [Twist arithmetic](twist-overlap.md) · [Named witness](deletion-invariance.md)
* **Odd cross.** Take (6,0) and (0,6) at any reviewed odd b≥7. D91 proves that even arbitrary words from the full horizontal and vertical stabilizers keep each mark in its coordinate-sign orbit. All physical source images have x=±6; all target images have x=0. Interchanging horizontal and vertical also fails, by their y-coordinates. This rules out both axial pencils even with the extra symmetries that a primitive parabolic could permute at cylinder rims. The pair has the explicit two-reflection diagonal shot through (4,−2) and (−2,4). [Axial stabilizers](axial-stabilizers.md) · [Shot](every-direction.md)

The negative result concerns a fixed reference direction and powers of one affine twist. All three controls satisfy the every-factor regularity condition. A dimension-dependent shear can be larger than the needed displacement, or leave the endpoints fixed while missing an entire orbit of directions. No direction enumeration is required.

## The dumbbell witness occupies a different residue

Write U=2a for the left chamber width; a is the horizontal distance from S to its door. In the four-copy unfolding cut horizontal cylinders at every physical corner lift, including the metrically regular convex lifts. The bands below y=2 and above y=3 in the two chambers have respective (circumference,height) equal to (4a,2) and (4a+2,2). The open central band has (8a+6,1). In each case a horizontal leaf makes one round trip across its physical run. The cap and doorway corner trajectories form the rims. The least positive pointwise rim-fixing shear is therefore

    c = lcm(2a, 2a+1, 8a+6).

At the three d155 parameters a=10,20,60 this gives c=18,060; 136,120; 1,176,120, respectively. The witness has p=a+1, giving p=11,21,61. Since both lift sets are fixed, the affine equivalence now proves two different all-integer conclusions for directions (p,1): the residue p=0 mod c always misses, and the residue p=a+1 mod c always hits. These need not be the only successful or failing residues. The latter residue is found geometrically: reaching the ceiling at the corridor midpoint requires a rise of one over a horizontal run of a+1. The twist propagates that first shot after the offset has been determined. [Reviewed named parameters](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d155-review-allfactor/review.md)

**Even dropping pointwise rim fixing cannot repair the vertical seed in this family.** Let F be any affine automorphism preserving the set of physical corner lifts and the horizontal line direction. Its horizontal expansion in absolute value is one: it permutes the nonempty finite set of stopped cylinder circumferences, so the largest is unchanged. It therefore preserves each circumference type, as does its inverse. Every lift of S belongs to the closure of a circumference-4a cylinder; these project only into the closed left chamber. Every lift of T belongs to the closure of a circumference-(4a+2) cylinder; these project only into the closed right chamber. The third circumference is distinct from both. After applying the inverse, every physical source abscissa is still at most 2a, and every target abscissa at least 2a+2. No vertical shot joins them. Thus no image of vertical under the full corner-preserving horizontal stabilizer lights this pair, even if an automorphism permutes cylinders or acts nontrivially on the rims. This statement is about the specified seed and physical pair.

## Mixed walls and a diagonal twist

The affine equivalence is unchanged in a diagonal cylinder basis. The following control uses the named V-notch B at scalar content one (vertices (0,0),(2,0),(2,2),(1,1),(0,2)). It has area 3 and four clean sites. Use origin (0,2) and torus coordinates

    U=(x+y−2)/4,   V=(x−y+2)/4  (mod 1).

The absolute lattice is generated by (2,2) and (2,−2) in physical coordinates. For instance, the diagonal reflection opposite the origin gives the translation (2,−2) after composition with its linear reflection; symmetry gives (2,2). The axial translations (4,0),(0,4) are in their span. The two incident reflections at the origin generate the linear reflection group, so all eight lift projections are signed permutations of (U,V). A diagonal multitwist parallel to the U-axis descends to the absolute torus with linear part (U+cV,V), where c is integral because the absolute lattice is preserved. Any affine translation part cancels when comparing two endpoint images.

Take these **rational interior** marks:

| Mark | Physical coordinates | Torus coordinates |
|---|---|---|
| S | (3/5,3/5) | (−1/5,1/2) |
| T | (4/7,6/7) | (−1/7,3/7) |

Both lie in the convex bottom rectangle 0<x<2, 0<y<1 and are directly lit. For the transverse reference direction (0,1) in torus coordinates a necessary condition is equality of the inverse images' U coordinates modulo one. After subtracting the common translation, a source U coordinate is either ±1/5 + k c (±1/2), or ±1/2 + k c (±1/5). The first retains a factor 5 in its reduced denominator, the second a factor 2. Every target U coordinate has order dividing 7. Thus no lift choice works for any integer k, including negative powers. Swapping U and V proves the same failure for the opposite diagonal twist. This argument even rules out every integral shear parameter, independently of the least multitwist. The endpoints give same-parity interior **lattice** marks on the integral dilate by 70; that dilate is not scalar primitive. This control does not decide the scalar-primitive mixed lattice-only subcase. [V-notch geometry and degree](all-factor-first-shot.md)

**What area 72 does bound.** It applies in scalar-primitive mixed rooms with a clean whole torus fiber. In coordinates x+y,x−y the translation unfolding has area D=16 area(P)≤1152 and is a degree-D cover of the unit torus (integral developed corner coordinates and integral periods). For a horizontal decomposition in this chart, stopped cylinder circumferences and heights are positive integers: the rims pass through integral corner images and a closed core has integral horizontal displacement. Consequently C h≤D for each cylinder. With M=lcm(1,…,D), shear M fixes all rims and all integral lattice lifts, because each integral transverse level translates by a multiple of C. The least rim-fixing shear c satisfies M/c integral, with M/c≤DM since c≥1/D. For integral endpoint lifts this is a finite upper bound on the exponent period; for lifts whose chart coordinates have common denominator e one can use eM/c. These bounds control repetitions **within** the pencil. They give no guarantee that it contains a successful slope. The V-notch exhibits that distinction even at area 3.

**Verdict.** The proposed pencil fails on the motivating dumbbell and both other orthogonal controls. Exchanging the two diagonal axes does not fix the proposal for real marks in mixed rooms. Any first-shot theorem based on parabolics now needs a reason to select a successful *reference leaf orbit*; taking more powers inside one of the excluded orbits cannot help.

**D156 review.** An affine automorphism preserving the horizontal line field and corner set takes critical horizontal trajectories to critical horizontal trajectories, hence permutes their complementary cylinders. The circumference argument applies to inverse images without any requirement that the map fix the marked lifts. The pointwise rim-fixing convention matters: the reviewed half-shear c/2 fixes the dumbbell marks and preserves corners setwise while interchanging two convex lifts. The two residue conclusions already hold modulo c/2. The transposed pencil (±1,m) also misses by the first-door divisibility test. On the three named parameters a=10,20,60, vertical closed leaves have primitive-direction holonomy multiplicities 2 or 10, whereas a primitive closed leaf in the witness direction has multiplicity 414, 814 or 2414, respectively. Integral unimodular derivatives preserve this multiplicity. Thus no affine image of vertical supplies the known witness direction or its shear translates on those inputs. D156 gives the same obstruction for all 45 saved hits (p,1) with |p|≤150 at a=10. These statements concern a method for producing a first shot.
