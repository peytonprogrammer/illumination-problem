# Transport between mirror fibers of a putative dark pair

**Partial result for BL.** Once a pair of affine point blocks is recognized as fibers, darkness constrains the **permutation induced by each individual straight segment**. For degree three the two quotient images must themselves be dark. For geometric fibers of a dark cubic cube every legal segment downstairs must induce the same bijection of the four sheets. These conclusions use darkness, whereas the cubic graph classification holds for lit orbitals as well. There is no assumption here that darkness produces a cubic orbital or that a general affine block is geometric. BL remains open.

## One matching per legal segment

Let f:Q→N be an inner map in a factorization over the pillowcase. Take distinct regular points u,v of N with respective fibers A,B of size k. Suppose the two lifts of physical S lie in A, and the two lifts of physical T in B. Call these two-element subsets I and J. Neither descent of the physical mirror to N nor invariance of this factor under the affine group is needed.

For any straight segment γ from u to v avoiding singularities of N, lifting gives a bijection Lγ:A→B. In this setting this needs no extra branch-value avoidance: at a regular point of N the local degree over a base pole is two, so any further ramification would give degree at least four upstairs, impossible. Away from base poles both maps are unbranched. Thus all lifted segments are legal on Q, and reversing γ gives the inverse permutation. If Lγ(I) intersects J the physical pair is lit. More generally the entire matching of Lγ is disjoint from any known dark relation in A×B. [Local degrees and path projection](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d61-fold-converse/README.md)

* For k=2 or 3, every such matching joins I to J, since 2+2>k. Therefore a dark physical pair with such fibers forces an ordinary dark regular pair u,v on N. This argument requires only the four dark seed edges, with no hypotheses on affine orbit degrees.
* For k=4, seed darkness forces Lγ(I)=B∖J. If a legal γ₀ exists, every relative permutation Lγ₀⁻¹ Lγ preserves I, so it lies in Sym(I)×Sym(A∖I). This is already a restriction without any cubic hypothesis.
* Suppose the dark orbital is the d93 cube and the two parts of its component are precisely these degree-four fibers. Each vertex has a unique nonneighbor on the other side. Its four nonneighbor edges form a single perfect matching M. Every legal γ must have Lγ=M. Consequently Lη⁻¹ Lγ is the identity for every pair of legal segments γ,η. Conversely, two such segments giving different bijections exclude this proposed **dark orbital**: one of their lifted edges is in the orbital, and the inverse affine map that takes it back to the seed preserves legality. This argument would fail for a cubic graph outside one edge orbit. For a dark geometric K₃,₃ the complement has no matching at all, in agreement with the degree-three conclusion.

These are necessary conditions: an upstairs path may project through a downstairs pole, so even a dark pair u,v need not give a dark pair upstairs. On R22 the reviewed seed darkness and degree-three recognition imply ordinary darkness of the two regular staircase images by the pigeonhole proof. The recognized factor equals the canonical factor for two other classes in this particular example. ## Two geometric ways the cube condition can fail

For fixed u,v joined by a legal γ₀ the subgroup generated by the relative permutations above is the monodromy of the subgroup of loops generated by γ followed by the reverse of γ₀. Products here only compare matchings of individually legal paths; we never interpret their concatenation as a light path. Any nonidentity relative permutation excludes the dark geometric cube. Two sufficient tests are:

1. If two legal segments from u to v bound an embedded disc containing exactly one branch value of f, their relative permutation is conjugate to its nonidentity local monodromy (or its inverse). Other, unbranched singularities in the disc do not affect this calculation. Thus this configuration excludes the cube.
2. Suppose u and v lie in the open part of a common Euclidean cylinder of N at distinct transverse heights. In strip coordinates the chords from u to the translates v+jC (C the cylinder circumference) stay in that strip and descend to legal straight paths for all integers j. Consecutive chords differ by one turn of the core. A nontrivial core permutation excludes the cube. More generally in degree four an orbit of the core based at u that moves I as a set excludes seed darkness, since the relative permutation must preserve I. In degree three one chord already suffices.

The tests use a specified quotient, and concern the relative permutations actually swept out by straight segments. Connectedness of the full covering only supplies transitivity with unrestricted paths in the punctured surface. It gives no lower bound on this smaller subgroup.

## Where this leaves the canonical bridge

For r≤8 any degree-three or degree-four factor N here has at most two zeros: each zero has k distinct reflex preimages. This reduces the geometric cubic branch to a problem on a sphere with at most two zeros. Existing d15 illuminates distinct *interior physical points* of a strictly simple small-reflex polygon. Its hypotheses do not automatically apply to u,v: when the quotient is mirror-equivariant, each fiber contains conjugate lifts, so u and v are fixed by the descended mirror. Even for a polygon double these are seam points. A quotient path that passes a forbidden pole would also fail the lifting test above. [Small-reflex theorem and hypotheses](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d15-review/review.md)

The first missing implication in the primitive BBBB case still comes **before** this reduction: two common hidden targets have not been shown to continue into proper sheet fibers. In the imprimitive BBBB case, geometric fibers and small mirror suborbits already coexist in the lit cross. One would have to use darkness to obtain one of the obstructions above or force a parity at which *every* regular transposition collapses. Also, BL as stated concerns every room without a proper canonical factor; its contrapositive includes a discrete B_x as well as a universal one. Restricting to BBBB addresses the assigned open core; other cases need their separate closures. No new family or discovery search was used.
