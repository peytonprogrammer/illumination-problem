# Which route would yield a finite check?

**Decision.** For an *effective* finite check of the same-parity lattice core, the direct area estimate has the shorter implication. Neither route currently gives a cutoff or a defensible compute budget. I would prioritize the direct route only if there is a concrete way to compare the two legs of a trajectory simultaneously. Counting more candidates or periodic points alone does not advance either route.

| | Orbit closures and periodic markings | Direct area estimate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is already proved or under review | Unbounded counterexamples force a whole dark physical component. Periodicity and quotient descent leave a degree-one or degree-two minimal quotient, conditional on the reviewed project inputs and the full-family census. In rank two the inherited mark has the proposed annihilator 4D(r), with D(r) ineffective. | At n≥120 a nondirect dark pair has d≥n−8, hence at least 2(n−8)−16−⌊n/2⌋ unreached regular off-class coincidences on its ordered-pair cover. This lower bound is 148 at n=120. |
| Single hardest missing lemma | A persistent, physically admissible tuple of periodic marks on every surviving minimal locus has a lit member. For rank two the specific obstruction is a non-Weierstrass torsion branch that can remain dark; ranks three through five are not settled by an abelian-surface argument. | An **effective deficit in the number of unreached synchronized coincidences**. For instance it suffices to prove H_sync≤(3/2−ε)n+K with explicit ε>0 and K uniform for r≤8, for the marked pairs in scope. One need not prove a constant shadow budget. |
| What a proof buys | Excluding every persistent component gives qualitative boundedness by contradiction. An additional effective argument is still needed to output the maximum area to check. | Combining the displayed inequalities gives n≤(K+32)/ε for n≥120. The finite census could then be scoped through max(119,⌊(K+32)/ε⌋). |

Here H_sync counts off-class regular coincidences on the marked ordered-pair cover that no legal synchronized trajectory reaches. The known shadow bound on the room double counts different objects. No transfer between those two counts is proved. A proof of the proposed upper estimate must get information about *actual joint paths*, not infer that every algebraic coincidence is reached. The existing census exclusions could be applied once a numerical cutoff existed; the feasibility of that census would depend on its value. Arbitrary real marks and other lattice classes require their own reductions before claiming the full 20-wall goal.
