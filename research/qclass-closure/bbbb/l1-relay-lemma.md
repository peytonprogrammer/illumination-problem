2. **Some off-class site is regular upstairs, and a separating parity factor exists.** For the separated-fold count this means one proper Q→N→B with the endpoint image sets disjoint and regular over a and **all three off-classes corner-only in N**. 3. **Some off-class site is regular upstairs, and there is no such separating factor.** The corrected Relay conjecture says the specified pair is lit. ### Attack on (iii) in the eight-reflex BBBB core

For normalized rooms with r≤8, n≥21 and marked projection degree d≥2n/3, the intersection count gives at least 2⌈2n/3⌉−16−⌊n/2⌋>0 regular off-class branches **on U**. This repairs the first implication under those hypotheses. To realize one branch, both legs must have the same travel time, reach the same lift, and have opposite incoming velocities there in one developed chart. Counting lift choices and states gives an upper bound on options and no lower bound on compatible matches. Even existence of a leg from each endpoint separately would leave this synchronization to prove.

There is a reviewed control for precisely this distinction below 22 walls. In cov4181 (r=8, n=28, d=21), the marked component contains seven regular coincidences over relative class d in the notation of the control table. No compatible pair of legs reaches any of the seven as a midpoint, for any lift choices. The pair is directly lit in another class. Thus a proof even with the degree hypothesis must be able to choose the class and candidate jointly. This control does not settle the existential claim across all classes. [Exact control and remaining reachability lemma](midpoint-necessity.md)

**Exact test for a single state.** Form the bipartite graph over the primitive arc from a to x in Q. Retain only regular vertices and edges between them. Every remaining vertex has degree at most two, since a regular site has two incident sheets. A legal walk continues on the other incidence whenever it passes an interior vertex. It cannot reverse on an edge. Conversely such a nonbacktracking walk is the straight continuation by FC. Thus each component is an isolated vertex, a path or an even cycle (parallel edges can form a two-edge cycle). Fix distinct compatible endpoint lifts over a, with both initial and final incidences available.

* On a path component the unique distance D between the lifts is even. An off-class relay exists there exactly when D≡2 mod 4.
* On a cycle of length C, choose an orientation and let D be the oriented distance. The possible positive lengths are D+jC or C−D+jC for j≥0. Consequently the test is gcd(C,4) dividing 2−D: D is even, so both signs of D agree modulo four. In particular a common cycle of length 2 mod 4 always suffices; an extra turn switches the midpoint parity.

This yields a concrete first failed step. One must put a compatible pair of lifts on a passing path or cycle in **one** state. Universality of B_x gives connectivity only after taking monodromy translates and transitive closure across states. It supplies neither common-component membership in a state nor the required distance on that component. A bound counting branches on U likewise records no distances.
