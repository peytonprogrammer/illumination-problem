### The reduction and its scope

Fix two distinct interior lattice marks of class a. If every lattice site over the other three classes is a corner, the local star argument forces at least nine reflexes: the four axial neighbors of each mark are reflexes, distinct across the two stars, and a third cell at the east reflex of one star forces a ninth reflex diagonally adjacent to that mark. Consequently r≤8 forces a regular off-class site **somewhere on the room double Q** for this pair. A theorem that such a site suffices for lighting *under r≤8* would settle same-parity lighting. Since its premise then holds automatically, the implication still carries the entire same-parity burden. Removing the reflex hypothesis, as in the proposed Relay lemma, gives the counterexamples below. [Local count](l1-dark-implies-fold.md)

For different parities put e=T−S mod 2 ≠ 0. A primitive projected arc goes from the source pole a to a+e, with an odd number of traversals. The geometric midpoint lies over a regular fixed point of the pillowcase translation τₑ. In torus coordinates it satisfies 2u=e mod 2 before taking the sign quotient. There are two such base points. Use the marked components of f(p)=τₑ f(q), with the local branches incident to the chosen mark lifts. Their 2dₑ diagonal intersections are regular candidates. A compatible straight approach to one of them suffices. An eight-reflex theorem for these twisted candidates, together with the same-parity assertion above, would light every interior lattice pair in the orthogonal target. The local star count concerns only equal parities. [Twisted count and midpoint development](l1-bbbb-area-bound.md)

Lighting and an off-class midpoint have different logical strengths. For equal parities a legal walk of length 2j has an off-class midpoint exactly when j is odd. Length divisible by four can also light the pair. For instance in the rectangle [0,8] × [0,4] take S=(2,2), T=(6,2). Every unfolded target has coordinates (±6+16i, ±2+8j), hence agrees with S modulo four. Every developed midpoint therefore has the source parity, although the horizontal chord lights the pair. This example addresses the unrestricted formulation; it requires no claim about normalized BBBB charts. [FC length criterion](l1-dark-implies-fold.md)

### The first failed implication, checked on the dark rooms

A regular site y on Q supplies a *possible local continuation*. For y to relay this pair, its diagonal germ must belong to U, the union selected by the incident sheets of S and T. R22 already fails this first implication. In the manuscript coordinates y=(1,0) is a smooth seam endpoint upstairs, of parity (1,0), while S=(0,0) and T=(2,2) are even-even. S reaches y along the unit horizontal segment of the seam. At y a legal upstairs reflection folds to a reversal at a convex corner of the five-square staircase. The marked-cover table gives zero off-class regular branches. Thus no choice of lifts and matched straight approaches through y can connect this pair. This is a failure before any estimate of the number of directions. ### What survives for eight reflexes

For normalized rooms with r≤8, n≥21 and marked projection degree d≥2n/3, the intersection count gives at least 2⌈2n/3⌉−16−⌊n/2⌋>0 regular off-class branches **on U**. This repairs the first implication under those hypotheses. To realize one branch, both legs must have the same travel time, reach the same lift, and have opposite incoming velocities there in one developed chart. Counting lift choices and states gives an upper bound on options and no lower bound on compatible matches. Even existence of a leg from each endpoint separately would leave this synchronization to prove.

There is a reviewed control for precisely this distinction below 22 walls. In cov4181 (r=8, n=28, d=21), the marked component contains seven regular coincidences over relative class d in the notation of the control table. No compatible pair of legs reaches any of the seven as a midpoint, for any lift choices. The pair is directly lit in another class. Thus a proof even with the degree hypothesis must be able to choose the class and candidate jointly. This control does not settle the existential claim across all classes.
