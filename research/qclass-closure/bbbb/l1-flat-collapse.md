Advances: L1 — a regular chord on a boundary face excludes the entire candidate dark flat if the collapse retains the open passages, the sides of the mirrors, and the endpoint sectors used by the chord. Merged corner points alone do not record this information. The eight-reflex two-arm example has such a chord at zero gap. This gives a qualitative exclusion test, not an effective height bound.

### The collapsed billiard and the lift

Keep the chart's ordered vertical slabs. At the face, identify x levels separated by zero total width. Let F be the images of points where two or more distinct physical vertices coalesce. Continue to forbid ordinary corners, including those with just one precursor. At each identified line **keep the ordered list of vanished slabs** and their occupied transverse intervals. Between consecutive vertex ordinates this gives a simple local rule: a passage from the left cell to the right cell exists if both cells and every intermediate layer are occupied there. If an empty layer intervenes, retain separate banks even if taking the interior of the planar closed union would join them. From an occupied side the first transition to empty is an exposed mirror. Two such transitions on opposite banks need not represent the same physical wall. A chain of occupied layers with no surviving cell on either side can disappear entirely. Record from which cell or layer each mark approaches the line.

**Collapse lemma.** Suppose the limiting marks are distinct. Assume there is a finite chord joining them with these properties:

* Its open legs are inside surviving cells or cross identified lines transversely, strictly within the common open passages just defined. It avoids F, physical corners and slit tips. Crossings away from identified lines have the usual billiard legality.
* Each reflection is transverse and strictly inside a surviving smooth wall, or on a labeled bank with a vertical mirror accessible through an occupied prefix of the vanished layers. At a smooth junction represented by a merged corner we impose avoidance of F as a sufficient condition.
* An interior mark off the collapse lines needs no further test. For a mark on a line, the specified forward germ at S or reversed germ at T must enter a surviving cell from its actual marked layer through an occupied prefix, strictly inside its transverse intervals. A stable mark on the boundary of that cell may use the germ pointing directly into the cell. Coincident marks, marks stranded in a vanished appendage, or departures at a corner do not automatically supply this certificate.

Then every sufficiently nearby **positive** realization of the chart lights its corresponding pair, at arbitrary relative rates of reopening the gaps. In particular such a boundary point excludes a flat dark throughout its positive cone. In a strictly simple collapsed room where these traces of vanished walls cause no extra obstruction, a corner- and F-avoiding transverse chord between stable interior marks suffices.

**Proof.** Select the actual mirror for each prescribed reflection, using the first exposed mirror on the chosen bank. In the unfolded word the developed image of T and the source depend continuously on the wall levels; their nonzero displacement specifies the perturbed direction. Enclose the finitely many transverse crossings and reflections in disjoint local neighborhoods along the parametrized trajectory. At a stack crossing take a small ordinate interval around the hit lying strictly in every required layer. At a stack reflection take such an interval for the occupied prefix and the interior of the selected mirror. When the layers reopen, the transverse displacement across their total width tends to zero: the limiting longitudinal velocity is nonzero there. Thus a crossing traverses the layers in order, and a reflection goes to the mirror and back through its prefix. The same argument on a half-neighborhood gives the endpoint assertion. Outside these neighborhoods compact subsegments have positive clearance from unused physical boundaries. This proves legality with one neighborhood of the parameter point for the entire word. A chord parallel to a collapsed line and uniformly separated from it needs no crossing test there; a chord running along a collapsed bank is not justified by this argument. [Reviewed transparent-layer perturbation](l1-ratio-compactness.md)

### What shrinks

For a maximal horizontal wall whose full length tends to zero, read its two incident turns in oriented boundary order. Mixed convex–reflex or reflex–convex turns cancel: the adjacent vertical edges have the same oriented direction and locally continue on one supporting line. Two convex turns reverse the vertical boundary direction and terminate an occupied finger of vanishing width. Its collapsed trace can be a spike or can disappear; a longer narrow connecting passage can pinch off a whole component, so incidence at the far end matters. Two reflex turns also reverse the boundary direction but terminate an *exterior* finger: its two banks can become a zero-width slit inside the planar silhouette. A horizontal wall only partially shortened survives and retains its remaining length. With several simultaneous contractions classify the **whole stack** by occupancy: the turn pair of one edge cannot decide connectivity after the other edges vanish. The flush terminal-cap argument already covers a single occupied prefix ending at a parallel solid mirror, with both marks retained and the hits clear of the mouth endpoints. It supplies neither transmission across an empty layer nor a starting sector for a stranded mark. [Terminal-cap argument](l1-minimal-ray.md)

### Exact calibrations

**Eight-reflex two-arm chart.** At L=0 the positive bands of the previous note end with (0,1) carrying (1,4) and (7,10), followed by (1,9) carrying (0,10). At x=1 keep the vanished intervals (0,4),(6,10); F={(1,6)}. The marks remain (0,2),(0,8). Reflect T across x=9. The physical legs have ordinates 2+x/3 and 8−x/3. They cross the stack at 7/3 and 23/3, strictly in the respective common passages, and meet the wall at (9,5). This is a boundary certificate. In fact the corresponding legs for L≥0 have slopes of magnitude 3/(9+L); at x=1+L they stay strictly in the arm openings while 3(1+L)/(9+L)<2, i.e. L<15. This includes the recorded L=1 chord. [Two-arm coordinates](l1-minimal-ray.md)

**Six-reflex 1:2 lock of the minimal-ray note.** Set the source interface at x=0. As a→0 the surviving rectangles are (−1,0)×(0,22), (0,B)×(7,17), and (B,B+1)×(16,25). At x=0 the vanished interval is (0,8): the actual passage is (7,8), and (8,17) is a slit between occupied banks. At x=B the vanished interval is (16,24), leaving passage (16,17). The merged vertices give F={(0,8),(B,24)}. S=(0,7) is now a physical corner even though it is outside F; T=(B,22) is on the right rectangle's left wall. Consequently there is no regular chord **between two interior marks** in this collapsed room under the usual mark convention. An endpoint-sector repair can sometimes succeed. For example take B=7/2, launch toward increasing x from S, and reflect on x=B+1. The limiting slope is 15/(B+2)=30/11. At x=B the ordinate is 182/11 in (16,17); the reflection occurs at ordinate 212/11, strictly inside the far wall. On reopening, the same reflected target gives slope 15/(B+3a+2). The first gate ordinate 7+15a/(B+3a+2) is strictly between 7 and 8 for all sufficiently small positive a. All later contacts have strict limiting clearance, and the final leg reaches T from the right. This is a one-sided launch proof for this specialization; the strict boundary lemma does not supply it for every B. [Six-reflex 1:2 coordinates](l1-minimal-ray.md)

For clarity, the earlier **equal-end-travel** six-reflex example also scales the outer cap depths with a. In that version the only positive-area survivor is [0,B]×[7,17], S limits to its corner and T at ordinate 20 lies outside it. The merged-corner images are at ordinates 0,8,20 on x=0 and 16,24,25 on x=B. There is no collapsed interior-pair certificate there either. [Original cap convention](l1-two-gate-boundary.md)

**R22 axis stretch.** The horizontal factor of this family is rank one. Normalizing the horizontal span gives a point; its only cone boundary before normalization is the zero vector, which erases the entire room. There is no nonzero proper face to test.

### Extreme rays and the stopping rule

In the normalized closed cone of a dimension-d linear factor in m gap coordinates, an extreme ray has at least d−1 independent active zero constraints. It therefore has at most m−d+1 positive gaps. Every nonzero boundary point of a dark positive cone, on an extreme ray or any intervening face, must lack the liftable witness above. This means actual darkness for F-avoiding regular chords when the limiting configuration is a room with admissible marks and transparent seams. For a slit, pinch, lost component or degenerate endpoint the precise conclusion is absence of a **certified** chord on the labeled limit. Darkness of the unmarked planar silhouette does not follow.

It would suffice to produce **one** such boundary certificate on each hypothetical dimension-at-least-two dark flat at r≤8. Under the finite-flat and primitive-normalization hypotheses this would exclude positive-dimensional normalized dark families and leave finitely many primitive candidates per chart, non-effectively. D232 bounds a detecting relation for a **supplied signed itinerary**, through its gate box, heights and event count. At a collapsed room the itinerary can disappear, a gate can close, or a mark can cease to be interior. Even at a regular boundary witness there is no uniform chartwise bound on which itineraries suffice or on their transverse data. Nor does exclusion of higher-rank factors bound the primitive generators of the remaining rational rank-one factors. An effective list or height bound for those factors (or an independent effective area reduction) is still needed. **No numerical N₀.**

**First open case.** A marked endpoint on a collapsing line can become a corner even with two positive-area end pieces and a nonempty open gate. The six-reflex 1:2 example exhibits exactly this issue. In general one needs a legal one-sided departure through the reopening layers, or a different boundary face with stable marks. For stable marks the first remaining obstruction is a collapsed passage whose banks or pinched components prevent a regular connecting chord.
