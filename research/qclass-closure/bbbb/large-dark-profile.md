# What a large BBBB dark pair would require

Fix a strictly simple normalized orthogonal lattice room with r≤8 reflex corners, area n≥120, and all four canonical factors universal (BBBB). Suppose S,T are distinct same-parity interior lattice points that are dark. Write Q for the room double, ι for its mirror, s,t for chosen lifts, and U for the marked union of off-diagonal ordered-sheet components.

| Where the condition lives | What darkness requires |
|---|---|
| Affine action on points of Q | Both s and ιs hide both t and ιt, and conversely. Each of the four opposite-endpoint stabilizer suborbits, in either orientation, has size ≤B(r). For the mirror suborbit e(S)=\|Aff⁺(Q)_s·ιs\|, counting the two shared neighbors gives 2e(S)≤B(r)(B(r)−1), likewise for T. B(r) is finite but unevaluated. Cubic orbitals are not forced. [d92–93](small-orbit-blocks.md) |
| Sheet pairs and midpoints on U | Darkness rules out direct visibility, so the marked valency d≥n−8. Already at n≥49, d≥⌈2n/3⌉ and at least 2⌈2n/3⌉−16−⌊n/2⌋ regular off-class coincidences are unreached (26 at n=49). At n≥120 the stronger valency gives at least 2(n−8)−16−⌊n/2⌋, or 148 at n=120, with at least 28 in each off-class there. [d78–79](eight-sheet.md) · [d81–82](midpoint-necessity.md) |
| Primitive sheet action | Already at n≥49, G contains A_n by the Burness–Guralnick argument applied to g_x², whose support is ≤24. Then d=n−1. At n≥73 there is also a lit pair of lifts in the marked parity, but its affine orbit misses all four dark edges. [d99](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d99-review-split/review.md) |
| Imprimitive sheet action | For **every** proper quotient f:Q→N of inner degree k, the full affine stabilizer of f(s) has orbit on f(t) of size ≤k I B(r), in both orientations and for all lift choices. Here k≤r and I≤(k!)^(z+3), where N has z≤⌊r/k⌋ zeros and z+4≤8 poles. For f(s),f(ιs) the bound is k I e(S). Identifying these as quotient mirror partners for the unique maximal partition at n≥120 uses d80. In degree two kI≤56 and at least one endpoint has distinct regular mirror images. Coincident images can make an orbit bound automatic; projected targets need not be dark. [d99 descent](bbbb-split.md) |
| Coordinates and unbounded families | Conditional on d80 and its inputs, all dark interior coordinates at n≥120 have denominators dividing D(r); the lattice pair already has denominator one. Under the same d80 assumptions, an unbounded sequence would have a fixed-r subsequence with all four lifts periodically marked on one affine invariant locus of rank h with 2≤h<(r+1)/2+1. This latter condition concerns a sequence, not an individual room. [d89](dark-locus.md) |

**R22 mechanism check (r=9, n=15).** R22 has a corner-only class and lies outside BBBB. Among regular lattice lifts, the hidden set from s is exactly the stabilizer orbit {t,ιt,(2,−2)}. From t it is {s,ιs,(4,0)}. Both mirror suborbits have size two. Each of the nine reflex corners is independently indispensable *in this room*: for each corner there is a route whose only forbidden contact is straight passage through that corner. All six regular coincidences are in the source class. This establishes no universal nine-corner lower bound. [d92](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d92-review-mirror/review.md) · [Corner certificates](corner-support.md) · [Coincidence profile](midpoint-necessity.md)

**Joint verdict.** No contradiction follows from the reviewed results. The quantity growing with area counts regular coincidences on U; B(r) bounds hidden targets on Q. A coincidence can project to a visible point while the two marked trajectories cannot reach it simultaneously. Likewise A_n acts by continuation on sheets, whereas bounded mirror orbits concern actual affine maps returning the marked cover. Quotient descent preserves an upper bound and supplies no opposing growth estimate. Existence of a room satisfying the whole profile remains open.

**Equivalent formulation of the core for n≥120.** For each nondirect pair in the stated range, some compatible marked start on U reaches a regular coincidence by a straight segment whose two projections avoid room corners.
