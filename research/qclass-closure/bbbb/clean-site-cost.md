# The price of a corner-only site, before and after a fold

## First charge: the degree of a clean torus fiber

For a translation unfolding X with M physical copies and a torus projection of degree d, let Z be a set of distinct torus sites whose **entire fibers** consist of forbidden physical corner lifts. Each site costs exactly d in local covering degree. A physical corner C of angle θ contributes at most Mθ/(2π) across *all* of Z, because that is the sum of the cone angles at its lifts divided by 2π. Thus

    d |Z| = Σ_C Σ_{u over C, π(u) in Z} deg_u π ≤ M(W−2)/2.

This follows by counting a branched-cover fiber with multiplicity and summing polygon angles. Wall counts and lift/displacement test counts are different: reuse of a site by another lift pair costs nothing extra. Here normalized means that the gcd of the horizontal edge lengths and the gcd of the vertical edge lengths are both one. For a normalized orthogonal room the absolute lattice is 2ℤ²: developed wall reflections have even translation terms, and following each wall and the reverse of its physical half-turn image supplies twice its wall vector as an absolute period. The two axis gcd conditions give both generators. Hence d equals the physical area n. At a clean parity x the equality specializes to n=fₓ+3rₓ, with fₓ convex and rₓ reflex corners. All corners project to integer sites; fibers at noninteger sites contain no corners. In BBBB all four integer sites have regular points, so **no torus quotient has a clean whole fiber**. Passing to a coarser torus merely unites preexisting fibers. This conclusion does not exclude a test that restricts to the fixed subset of a nontorus quotient fiber. ## Second charge: what a developed-path parity fold spends

Let Q→N→B be a factorization of an orthogonal room's pillowcase double through a sphere, with inner degree k. For a set J of parity sites assume **every** point of N over J is a pole or a zero. For x∈J write qₓ for the number of regular Q-points over x, rₓ for the reflex count, and zₓ for the number of zeros of N there. Then a necessary test depending only on upstairs counts and the proposed degree is

    Σ_{x∈J} [ qₓ + 2 (rₓ mod k) ] ≤ 2k−2.

Indeed a zero of N has k unramified reflex preimages since the local degrees over the pillowcase are at most three. Over poles of N let bⱼ,ₓ count preimages of local degree j at x. Then qₓ=b₂,ₓ and rₓ=kzₓ+b₃,ₓ. Hence b₃,ₓ≥rₓ mod k. Each b₂ point costs one unit of ramification and each b₃ point costs two. Riemann–Hurwitz on the two spheres supplies exactly 2k−2 units altogether. For distinct proposed folds the inequality applies separately; their ramification budgets cannot be added. This is a necessary cost for an actual fold. The counting argument does not construct one from an orbit exclusion. [Underlying fold count and hypotheses](fold-converse.md)

In R22 the two axial off-source classes have (qₓ,rₓ)=(2,3) each. An inner degree must divide 15, excluding degree two immediately. The inequality also excludes k=2 if J contains either axial class; J={(1,1)} alone passes. On the actual degree-three staircase fold both axial classes have only poles and zeros downstairs, as required. These two classes consume the entire budget of four; the odd-odd class has (0,3). There is no ramification left for a degree-three preimage of a pole. Each of the three off-source classes consequently contains one staircase zero with three upstairs reflexes. The four degree-two preimages are exactly the smooth seam endpoints. Their folded reversals retrace to the source on the truncated path. The nonzero dyadic point is forced into one of the three off-source classes. [Staircase proof](../../illumination-publication/repository/preprints/twenty-two-wall/manuscript.tex)

**Exact incremental stop cost in the fixed R22 polygon.** Keep its walls fixed, convex corners forbidden, and the three odd-odd reflexes permanently stopped. Every stop set that blocks all twelve residual cells jointly must also contain all six axial reflexes: for each there is an archived route on which it is the only forbidden contact. All six routes start on sheet I. Their cells, given as (target sheet, displacement mod 4ℤ²), are (I,(0,0)) once, (diag(−1,1),(0,2)) twice, (diag(1,−1),(2,0)) once, and (−I,(2,0)) twice. Thus eight residual cells have no singleton witness from this list. In the first route the midpoint is the smooth point (2,−2). Stopping all six restores R22's ordinary rules and suffices by the manuscript. The equality 3+6=9 recounts R22's own reflexes and 22 walls; this fixed-polygon calculation bounds no other room. **Limit of the result.** Direct clean torus fibers and folds onto a base with clean parity classes now have separate, checkable prices. The reviewed R22-type fold theorem already forces at least nine reflexes under its separated-mark and three-clean-class hypotheses. For BBBB the direct charge is unavailable, and a proper corner-only pillowcase factor is excluded by the canonical-block condition. The missing step is a cost for a developed-path obstruction without such a factor. Cov4181 shows why merely counting corners on relaxed trajectories cannot supply that cost in all normalized rooms.
