# What survives the four-site constraint

The implication tested here was proposed in the coordinator brief “d175 verdict and your next L1 step,” immediately after the subset-action note went to d176: the four-site product and the r≤8 branch data were to force a corner-only site or a proper block system. It was a proposed route, not an earlier established lemma. [D178 review of the formulation](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d178-review-naturalaction/review.md)

**Verdict.** The proposed implication is false for actual normalized strictly simple rooms. The first degree beyond L2 at which the unmarked implication fails is 21. Both natural group types also survive at arbitrarily large prime degrees in the reviewed DB55 stretch family, where the chambers have distinct same-parity interior lattice points. This is an obstruction to the proposed proof of L1, not a dark example.

## Arbitrarily large degree, including the marked setting

Use the existing DB55 stretch: two 5-by-5 squares [0,5]×[0,5] and [5+ℓ,10+ℓ]×[0,5], joined across [5,5+ℓ]×[2,3]. Choose any prime p≥53 and put ℓ=p−50. Area is p, wall count 12, reflex count four. The unit-height corridor gives a unit vertical edge-length gcd (the vertical boundary lengths include 2 and 5); the horizontal gcd is gcd(5,ℓ)=1. The room is connected and strictly simple. Each chamber contains interior points of all four parities, including pairs in different chambers.

Use the square-sheet pillowcase dictionary from d146. Its four edge involutions L,R,B,T give oriented local permutations LB, BR, RT, TL, respectively at 00,10,11,01 (composition right to left, on front sheets). Their product is the identity by cancellation. Inverting a local permutation to switch conventions preserves its cycle type. Consequently the following are simultaneous cycle data, not independently chosen partitions.

Since ℓ is odd, the four mouth reflexes occur once per parity. The eight convex corners occur twice per parity. Thus at **each of the four sites** the partition is

    1² 2^((p−5)/2) 3¹.

The degree and total ramification are p and 2p−2. Transitivity follows from connectedness of the branched cover, and prime degree implies primitivity. Squaring any site generator leaves a single 3-cycle. Jordan's primitive 3-cycle theorem, as used in d81, gives Aₚ⊆G. The four generators are all even when p≡1 mod 4 and all odd when p≡3 mod 4. Hence G=Aₚ or Sₚ, respectively, in its natural action. Each parity contains 2-cycles, and the invariant equivalence generated by any one of them must be universal by primitivity. All four canonical relations are therefore universal. Primes in both residue classes are unbounded. At p=53 this specializes to four copies of 1² 2²⁴ 3, exactly the independently saved DB55 data. The family refutes a corner-only-or-block implication without an additional hypothesis using the marked pair's darkness. I checked the product on all 21 sheets with the d146 edge model. For each j=1,…,20 I formed the finest generator-invariant equivalence identifying sheets 0 and j. ## First open L1 implication

Can *total darkness of specified interior marks* force a clean site or proper factor in the natural action? Product one, ramification, normalization, the wall budget and the existence of same-parity marks jointly do not force that conclusion. DB55 realizes the symmetric cycle portrait above at unbounded area. Any advance by this route must use how continuing a single legal straight arc transports the marked lifts. The first outstanding structural case is a primitive BBBB room in the natural action with a putatively dark pair. The imprimitive follow-on was conditioned on closing the natural branch; that condition has not been met.
