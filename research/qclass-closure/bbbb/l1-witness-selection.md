Advances: L1 — the specified pinned pair in the five-reflex parabola family is lit for **every** allowed (m,u) by a bounded-shape tier walk. One simple cycle suffices, however many side reflections the narrow tier needs. The seven-reflex window family needs no cycle. For general charts I obtain polynomial tests per bounded template and an exact one-gap tail test for fixed counts. General witness selection remains open.

### The parabola family, including the region below the direct threshold

Use the five tiers of the preceding note, S=(0,0), T=(m,u), m≥3 and U=u−m≥2. The narrow tier 0<y<m has sides x=−1,1 and opens at y=m over (0,1). Above it the left wall is x=0 throughout. The right boundary is at least m, and strictly to the right of m around T and at the outer roof y=u+1. The direct template works exactly for u>m². [Tier coordinates](l1-excursion-normal-form.md)

Choose effective terminal travel D=u or u+2. The latter represents a single reflection from the outer roof. Put V=D−m. For j≥0 integral set p=(m+2j)/D, the absolute transverse travel per unit vertical travel. The signed unfolded crossing relative to 2j is

    δ=pm−2j=(m²−2jV)/D.

**Gate lemma.** If 0<|δ|<1 there is a choice of the initial horizontal sign for which the ray enters at x=|δ| and, after entry, follows x=|ps−2j|, where s is accumulated vertical travel. Indeed the triangle wave obtained by folding the interval (−1,1) has value zero at each unfolded coordinate 2j and derivative (−1)^j there for an initially rightward ray. Negating the initial sign negates both the position and derivative. Select the sign giving position |δ| and derivative sign(δ) with respect to ps. This matches the displayed absolute value, including its possible single reflection at the smooth left wall. Between entry and s=D that absolute value stays below m except at the terminal point: it is the absolute value of a linear function from δ to m. Thus it clears the intervening right walls. The vertical wall x=0 continues smoothly across the later internal interfaces. In the roof case require additionally m−p>0, so the roof hit at s=D−1 lies at x=m−p in its open face. The final point is T in either case.

**Choosing the winding.** For D=u start with the nearest nonnegative multiple 2jU to m². A nonzero error is at most U<u, and the gate lemma applies. If the error is zero and U<m, increase j by one: the new error has magnitude 2U<u. Only U≥m and 2U dividing m² remain. In that case 2(U+2) cannot also divide m². Otherwise m²/2 would be an integer divisible by lcm(U,U+2)≥U(U+2)/2>m²/2. Use D=u+2 and the nearest multiple of 2(U+2); the nonzero error is at most U+2<D. Moreover pV=m−δ<m+1 and V=U+2≥m+2, so p<1 and the roof condition holds. This proves lighting for all m≥3 and all integer u≥m+2.

**Shape and threshold.** In the narrow tier the only internal vertical support is x=0. With upward velocity the four (half-tier, horizontal-sign) states repeat in one simple cycle. The lower walk is a power of this cycle with a bounded prefix and suffix. Above the gate the abscissa turns at most once, and the height turns at most once, so the remaining cell crossings have bounded length independent of m,u. Subdivision vertices at a smooth wall or in the interior use the corresponding tier-graph event. Thus the family threshold is its first admissible u=m+2; the quadratic threshold belongs only to the direct template. In the seven-reflex family the one reflection at (−c,1/5) gives a bounded walk for every integer c≥1; scaling all abscissas by c preserves its cell sequence. These are results for the specified pairs. [Seven-reflex construction](l1-excursion-normal-form.md)

### What holds on an arbitrary bounded template

Allow bounded connecting paths between cycle powers, including an initial and a terminal path. Pure powers alone are closed walks and cannot in general join distinct graph states. Fix such a template on the oriented rectangular cell graph, split into cases for zero exponents, and fix the endpoint sectors. In a cycle returning to the same cell and velocity signs the developing map from one visit to the next is a translation: each coordinate reflection count is even, so its linear part is the identity. Its translation vector v is linear in the wall coordinates. Accordingly, the j-th occurrence of any fixed face in that cycle is its first developed face translated by jv. The final developed target contains the term kv for exponent k. Connecting words have fixed diagonal sign matrices and translations linear in wall coordinates.

For a nonaxial candidate line from S to this target image with displacement Δ, require each designated exit to lie strictly inside its developed face. For a vertical face at x=a with endpoint ordinates b,c, test that the intersection ordinate lies between b and c by multiplying by the known sign and magnitude of Δx; use the transposed test on horizontal faces. Require event times strictly increasing for consecutive distinct events and between the two endpoints. For an allowed simultaneous grid crossing use an equality and the specified incident sectors. Developed event times have denominators Δx or Δy, with fixed signs on each direction case. Clearing them gives polynomial comparisons in lengths and integer counts. For a fixed phase in a repeated cycle these cleared tests are affine in the occurrence index j, with Δ held at its value for the complete word. The same is true of the time order for a neighboring pair, including last face to next first face. Hence test just the first and last applicable occurrence of each phase or neighboring pair. Test transitions between blocks once. This yields finitely many polynomial comparisons independent of the exponent sizes. Conversely the extrema tests imply all intermediate tests; consecutive intersections join inside their developed rectangular cell by convexity. This proves template membership. Simultaneous contacts on artificial grid subdivisions can require equalities even for legal rays.

**One-gap tails.** For a fixed word or fixed counts, vary one gap g on one axis with the remaining gaps fixed and the pinned incidences held fixed. All coordinates on that axis are affine in g; those on the other axis are constant. Each cleared face determinant or cross-axis time comparison is affine in g (a product always uses opposite axes). Same-axis time comparisons only compare coordinates. Consequently on a specified direction branch each strict test has the form ag+b>0. Its tail is feasible exactly when a>0 or a=0 and b>0, and every required equality must vanish identically. When all tests pass, take g beyond every root −b/a with a>0, as well as the chart and direction bounds. This is conditional on finding a witness template with these signs. Witness selection at already-lit points cannot supply lighting at an untested point. Moreover even cofiniteness on *every* coordinate fiber leaves possible unbounded joint regimes (the diagonal in a two-gap cone has finite fibers). A chart-wide area bound needs control there too. ### First open shape

After deleting acyclic context, a strongly connected component with just one directed simple cycle has only that cycle to repeat. More generally if every visited strongly connected component is a singleton without a loop or a directed cycle, the condensation graph is acyclic and the number of powered blocks is bounded by the number of states. The first graph obstruction is a recurrent component with a branch: two different simple first-return cycles based at a common state (distinguishing edges as well as states). In an abstract figure-eight graph the words (AB)^k require 2k simple-cycle powers up to bounded connecting pieces; AB itself repeats the base state and is not simple. A single example requiring A,B,A would still have bounded shape. To refute a universal N(r) one needs a family at fixed r for which the *minimum* number of blocks over every legal witness tends to infinity. No such pinned room family follows from the figure-eight language. Neither test family requires interleaving. The open selection step is to replace an interleaved witness in such a recurrent component by another aimed at the same marked target with boundedly many blocks, or prove that no such replacement exists along a family. No general N(r) or census area bound is obtained.
