### Which projection has which group?

Remove the four pole values and their preimages when computing monodromy. Let G≤Sₙ be the monodromy of Q→B on the generic sheets Ω. An off-diagonal connected component of the normalized pair curve corresponds to an orbital G·(i,j). Its first projection to Q has fiber Δ=Gᵢ·j and monodromy the image of Gᵢ on Δ: exactly the loops fixing i lift to closed loops on the first copy. Its degree is |Δ|. The marked union U can contain more than one orbital. In that event d is a sum of degrees and there is no transitive degree-d action on that union. A block for a connected projection gives U_component→V→Q, whereas the separated-fold count requires Q→N→B with additional parity and endpoint hypotheses. [d208, §4](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d208-review-condition-i-low/review.md) · [Separated-fold hypotheses](l1-dark-implies-fold.md)

**Primitive original G, n=22, r=8.** The imported classification input is d208's CFSG-based primitive degree-22 list. The geometric inputs are that local degrees are 1, 2 or 3 and each reflex contributes exactly one 3-cycle. The list is M₂₂, M₂₂:2, A₂₂, S₂₂. Write gₓ for the branch permutation at a pole and zₓ for its number of 3-cycles. Its other cycles have lengths one or two. Thus gₓ² is either identity or an element of order three moving exactly 3zₓ sheets. In the natural action of M₂₂ its only order-three class fixes four points and has six 3-cycles. In M₂₂:2 every square lies in M₂₂ by the index-two quotient. Hence either Mathieu possibility would force zₓ∈{0,6} at **each** pole. But Σzₓ=r=8. This excludes both groups. The argument uses exactly eight reflexes; at six this divisibility test alone gives no exclusion. Consequently G is A₂₂ or S₂₂, its point stabilizer acts naturally as A₂₁ or S₂₁, and the off-diagonal component is connected and primitive of degree 21. In particular it contains a single 3-cycle. [ATLAS, natural degree-22 cycle types](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/v3/permrep/M22G1-p22B0)

This proof uses the reviewed classification directly. A useful local Jordan test is zₓ=1: gₓ² is then a single 3-cycle. A fixed sheet i of gₓ² gives a closed lift of the squared pole loop to Q and the same 3-cycle on the other sheets. Likewise gₓ³ is a single transposition if gₓ has exactly one 2-cycle. A product of several disjoint triples is not a single 3-cycle. The total r=8 alone does not supply the local singleton criterion (the numerical distribution 2+2+2+2 illustrates the issue). Jordan requires a primitive action and a single prime cycle fixing at least three points. [Jones, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.5169)

**Imprimitive original G.** D208 gives eleven pairs of sheets, block action containing A₁₁, and one external orbital. With i fixed, Δ consists of the twenty sheets outside i's block. Their ten original pairs are a nontrivial invariant partition for Gᵢ on Δ. Thus this marked external projection is genuinely imprimitive. If the marked union also includes the partner of i there is an additional degree-one component. Even the original eleven-block quotient need not separate the marked images or be corner-only in the required classes. Case (iii) cannot be discarded from this group calculation.

### Why giant monodromy leaves a geometric question

For a monodromy tuple, one Hurwitz convention sends adjacent entries (g,h) to (h,h⁻¹gh). Its square sends them to (k⁻¹gk,k⁻¹hk) with k=gh and leaves the other entries fixed. The product gh is unchanged. This gives an explicit action of pure generators on **tuples**; the conjugator is prescribed by that tuple. It does not allow an arbitrary element of Gᵢ to move the target incidence while holding the source, the other pole loops, and the regular FC graph fixed. Simultaneous conjugation of the whole tuple and of its markings merely renames the sheets. Arbitrary loops on Q do realize the marked projection monodromy, but their concatenations can turn, change primitive arc, or pass a corner at which the straight FC walk stops. [FC, §§2–3](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d61-fold-converse/README.md)

**Composite words: geometric coverage and the implementation assumption.** A state can be defined geometrically by a marking of the four-punctured pillowcase. Transport its based arcs, peripheral loops and endpoint incidences together. For the cover representation ρ, the tuple on arcs transported by a homeomorphism F evaluates the loops by ρ∘F∗. The edge over a transported arc starting at a specified sheet is its unique lift, with its endpoint determined by path lifting. Hence transport under F∘H is successive transport under H then F on the base; evaluation by precomposition must respect the corresponding order. The endpoint labels must move with the cycles: under the relabeling g↦kgk⁻¹ a cycle label function m becomes m∘k⁻¹. This proves functoriality for geometric marked states, up to a common relabeling if base paths are changed. It needs no claim that every conjugator in the sheet group is available.

On the torus double, the integral shears U(p,q)=(p+2q,q) and V(p,q)=(p,q+2p) descend to the pillowcase and fix each pole because they are identity modulo two. They and their inverses reach every primitive unoriented slope of each parity from its base slope. For |p|>|q|>0 subtract the nearest even multiple of q from p. The new absolute value is at most |q|, strictly decreasing the old maximum; use the other shear with the coordinates exchanged when |q|>|p|>0. The process ends at an axial unit vector or at |p|=|q|=1, where one shear and overall sign suffice to choose (1,1). Parity is preserved throughout. The affine shear carries a primitive straight base arc to the primitive straight arc of the new slope. Combined with the reviewed single-arc FC criterion, this proves the geometric equivalence: a same-parity physical pair is dark iff all compatible endpoint lifts are separated in every regular graph for every geometric state and off-class. No midpoint residue is needed for this equivalence.

**Scope of d218's calibration.** To infer the physical slope and marked incidences of a *particular composite code word* from d218, one still has to identify its positive half-twists with oriented geometric mapping classes and reconcile the order of tuple pullback with matrix action. The checks compared only the base and four single pure moves; the review code says the signs were indistinguishable up to mirror in the nonvacuous comparisons. Order matters even up to sign: on (1,0), U∘V gives (5,2), whereas V∘U gives (1,2). Thus a single-letter match cannot by itself certify the word-to-slope convention. Any use of that dictionary for a named composite word here is conditional on that identification. The area-22 group and cov4181 group calculations use pole permutations only, and the existential geometric statement ranges over all arcs; neither uses a composite-word numerical calibration. For the stronger off-class midpoint target its walk must also have length 2 modulo 4. There are three possible off-classes. At n=22, r=8, the count gives ≥13 distinct candidate coincidences across them if d≥20, and ≥15 if d=21. It gives no distribution among Hurwitz translates. ### cov4181 by hand from its cells

The room degree is 28. Here is a block and sign calculation giving **both** groups. Label sheets by the lower-left corners of the cells in d66. Define Vᵢ to exchange adjacent cells across vertical grid lines of parity i whenever the neighbor exists, fixing the cell otherwise. Define Hⱼ on horizontal lines in the same way. Use Vᵢ followed by Hⱼ at each of the four parity sites. These four products generate the even edge-word monodromy: same-axis products can be written by inserting an opposite-axis involution twice. Inverses or a different peripheral basis give the same group. [d66 cell rows, §7](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d66-pair-level/README.md)

List each block in lexicographic cell order. For i=0,1,2 put
Bᵢ=({0,3,4}×{i,5−i})∪{(7,i)}.
The fourth block B₃=({1,2,5}×{2,3})∪{(6,2)}. Each has seven cells. The following table gives the destination of each block and the parity bits of the four internal bijections, always in **source** block order. It can be checked by taking the two indicated neighbor steps on each row.

| generator (vertical,horizontal parity) | block permutation | internal bits |
|---|---|---|
| a=(0,1) | (01) | 0010 |
| b=(1,1) | (01)(23) | 0011 |
| c=(1,0) | (123) | 0000 |
| d=(0,0) | (12) | 0001 |

For example a² cycles precisely the three cells (5,2), (5,3), (6,2) and fixes the other 25. All three are in B₃. Since the room action is transitive, the setwise stabilizer acts transitively on B₃. Seven is prime, so this degree-seven action is primitive. Jordan gives at least A₇ on B₃. The normal closure of that single supported triple under the block stabilizer therefore contains A₇ **supported only there**: its restriction contains all A₇-conjugates of the triple, which generate A₇, and all conjugates fix the other blocks pointwise. Block transitivity now gives A₇⁴ in G.

The block action is S₄ by the table. Each generator's total internal sign equals its block sign, so G is contained in the even permutations of the full wreath product S₇ wr S₄ (a block swap on odd-sized blocks is odd). Modulo A₇⁴ the word applying a,b,a,b successively fixes the blocks with sign vector 0011. Its block conjugates give every weight-two vector, spanning all even bit vectors. These are exactly the allowed kernel bits. Together with the surjective S₄ block action this proves equality:
   
    G = (S₇ wr S₄) ∩ A₂₈.

Fix i in one block. On the other three blocks its stabilizer induces the **full S₇ wr S₃**: extend any desired permutation there using an internal permutation of the first block fixing i, whose parity can be chosen either way. This realizes the required overall sign. The remaining six sheets in i's block form the other stabilizer orbit. Thus the reviewed marked orbital of valency 21 is the external one, and its projection monodromy is S₇ wr S₃ on three blocks of seven. It contains a supported 3-cycle, while imprimitivity prevents the Jordan conclusion A₂₁. The relative class d still has no legal joining state for the named pair; class b lights it. This control tests the group computations and the per-class distinction. Its room degree differs from the area-22 target.
