# A half-period into the terminal strip of a lattice staircase

**Result.** In the notation of the [terminal-strip proof](ferrers-terminal-strip.md), let P be a common slope period of the intervening bands. Suppose the reduced denominator of Ph/W is even and the last horizontal width increment satisfies W−v≤w. Every pair of interior marks in the ten-wall Ferrers room is lit. This includes arbitrary real marks in the lattice room. Either axial orientation can be used. The result settles the entire named d17 below-chord room, which the earlier large-denominator inequality missed. The hypotheses do not hold for every lattice realization.

Only the opposite terminal-cell placements remain in d15. The broad-band target therefore has abscissa z strictly between v and W. Its mirror coordinate W−z belongs to (0,w). For direct bottom travel b and floor-return travel 2h−b the rotations are Pb/(2W) and P(2h−b)/(2W). An irrational rotation places a virtual endpoint in (−w,w) by density. Otherwise at least one of these rotations has even reduced denominator: the sum of rationals with odd denominators has odd denominator, and the actual sum is Ph/W. A power of that even-order rotation is exactly a half turn. At that power choose the target image −z modulo 2W; the virtual endpoint is W−z. Choose either source image in [−w,w] after the same shift. The segment between these virtual positions clears all common-side mouths with the explicit margin in the terminal-strip proof. For a floor return its two source images have distinct floor coordinates separated by less than W, so at least one misses every floor corner. This proves illumination simultaneously for all terminal placements without imposing any denominator condition on the marks.

**Named all-pairs application.** For d17's grid (0,3,4,9,12) on both axes, P=18, h=3, W=12 give Ph/W=9/2. The last increment 3 equals w. Thus *all* interior pairs are lit even though the middle reflex is below the outer-reflex chord. This is an all-marks conclusion for a room, not an inference from its saved one-pair direction.

## Where the wider-door argument actually fails

Here is an explicit normalized lattice realization of the same word with coordinates specified on both axes by (0,2,10,23,230). From bottom to top its rows have widths 230,23,10,2. Its axis edge gcds are one. The middle reflex (10,10) lies strictly below the chord from (2,23) to (23,2). Take S=(1,115), T=(115,1).

Allow any integer multiple of the *least* common middle period, both terminal cap choices, and either sweep axis. Enlarge the common strip by allowing the virtual straight line to use the entire signed width of each of the three mouths, with no extra virtual reflection between them. Even this enlarged construction fails.

Indeed, the middle travel ratios are 13/20 and 8/46. Their common real slope periods are exactly 460ℤ: writing M=20i/13=23j/4 gives 80i=299j with coprime coefficients. Bottom travel B is 1 or 3. Thus MB is always zero modulo 460. Any signed virtual terminal image Z is ±115 modulo 460, so |Z|≥115. The last two joint coordinates have absolute values at most 10 and 23, respectively, and are separated by longitudinal distance 8. A virtual straight line therefore has slope magnitude at most 33/8, implying |Z|≤23+3·33/8=283/8. The contradiction is independent of the source image and of any initial cap. Transposition followed by reversing the pair gives the identical calculation. The exclusion concerns routes admitting this straight virtual representative after subtracting common middle periods. General attachment-wall reflections need not admit it.

**What lights this pair.** The diagonal mirror exchanges the two tips. Launch from S along (1,−1). At heights 23, 10 and 2 its abscissas are 1, 6 and 2. To check the folds, the first 92 units of descent return to abscissa 1 in the width-2 tip, moving right. The next 13 units reflect at x=10 and end at x=6 moving left. The last 8 reflect at x=0 and reach (2,2) moving right. This is an interior point of the symmetry diagonal, and the velocity is normal to it. Reflecting the reversed first leg across that diagonal gives the continuation to T with matching velocity. Exact replay with d164's tracer reached (2,2) at time 113 in these velocity units and T at time 226, with 48 and 96 wall reflections respectively. This settles the exhibited pair. It leaves other placements and nonsymmetric rooms in this residue open.

## Follow-on at ten and twelve walls

The half-period proof only needs a contiguous set of bands sharing a common side, every intermediate width at least w, a full-width terminal floor, genuine reflecting side walls, and a target with 0<W−z<w. The number of intermediate bands is arbitrary. Choose P with P times each intermediate travel a multiple of its doubled width. Outside attachments are immaterial if the path never visits their mouths. No initial cap is required.

For the requested next word LLLRLLLRLR, use the ledger realization's four unit-height bands but stretch their x-lines from (0,1,2,3,4) to (0,1,3,4,5). From bottom to top the runs are (3,5), (3,4), (1,4), (0,4). Starting in the second run and moving up, measure transverse coordinates from x=4. The nested widths are 1,3,4, with full roof and P=6. Now Ph/W=3/2. Every mark in the second run lights every mark with 0<x<1 in the top run, irrespective of both ordinates. The overhanging bottom run does not enter this path. This pair-region result does not treat all 52 d15 placement types for the word.

At twelve walls the identical test applies, for example, to the five-band Ferrers word LLLLLRLRLRLR. Take unit intermediate heights and nested widths 2,3,5,9,10 from top to bottom, with bottom height 1. P=90 is a common period but Ph/W=9 has odd denominator, illustrating why increasing the number of bands does not itself guarantee a half turn. Instead choose middle widths 3,5,7 and bottom width 8: P=210, Ph/W=105/4 and 8−7<2. Every opposite terminal-cell pair in that lattice realization is lit. Its other unresolved placement types still require their own certificates. See the [per-word ten-wall](wall-slab-10.md) and [twelve-wall](wall-slab-12.md) tables. No new room or direction search was run.
