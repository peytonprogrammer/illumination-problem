# Low marked valency and blocks of two

**Result (proof in this note, reviewed by d78).** In a normalized strictly simple orthogonal lattice room with at most eight reflex corners and area n > 48, suppose the sheet action has an invariant partition into blocks of size two and the induced action on those blocks is primitive. For every distinct same-parity interior lattice pair, the marked valency d is at least n−2. Thus this unbounded family cannot furnish a low-valency pair. The statement makes no BBBB assumption and gives no straightening or lighting assertion.

## Matching lemma

Let a transitive group act on 2m sheets in m invariant two-element blocks, m ≥ 6. Suppose the induced group contains the natural A_m. Then either the group is transitive on ordered pairs of sheets in different blocks, or it preserves a partition of the sheets into two sets of size m.

Fix an orbit O on ordered pairs from different blocks. For a sheet s in B the sheet stabilizer has index at most two in the block stabilizer. Its induced action therefore contains A_(m−1) on the other blocks: the induced subgroup has index at most two and the alternating group on m−1 letters has no nontrivial quotient of order two. Hence the stabilizer of s is transitive on the other blocks. The analogous fact holds for the second sheet coordinate. Every ordered block pair B,C therefore has a constant positive row sum and constant positive column sum in its 2-by-2 incidence matrix for O. The two sums coincide. If they are two, O contains all external ordered pairs. Otherwise O prescribes a bijection f_BC from B to C for every B ≠ C. Equivariance follows from the definition of an orbit.

Here is why those bijections yield a two-part partition, including when O was initially directed. Label the two sheets in each block arbitrarily by bits. Write f_BC(i)=i+e_BC, with all sums modulo two. Relabeling block B adds a bit at B to every incident edge. Consequently the backtrack sums e_BC+e_CB and the directed triangle sums e_BC+e_CD+e_DB are invariant under relabeling. They are constant, separately, by transitivity on ordered pairs and triples of distinct blocks. Call the constants b and t. Add a triangle and its reversal: 0=t+t=3b, so b=0. All edge labels are symmetric. Replace every e_BC by e_BC+t (replace all matchings by their complements if needed); the resulting equivariant matchings have zero triangle sums. Fix a base block and transport its two labels along these matchings. The triangle identity makes the transported labels consistent across every edge. The two sets of equal labels give the desired partition; the group may interchange the sets. This proves the lemma.

## Application to the room

An invariant two-part partition gives an intermediate degree-two cover of the base pillowcase. Its source is genus zero, since it is dominated by the room double, a sphere. It has no local cycle of length three, so it is itself a pillowcase. Proposition R1 excludes any nontrivial intermediate pillowcase in a normalized room. The matching alternative is therefore impossible. Each interior mark has four distinct incident sheets, which cannot all fit in one size-two block. There is an ordered pair of marked sheets from different blocks, so its orbit alone contributes 2(m−1)=n−2 to the marked valency.

For the threshold, let N be the intermediate cover on the m blocks. R1 implies it has at least one zero (local degree three over a base pole). Every such zero has two distinct reflex preimages by Fact L, so there are at most four zeros on N. Squaring the monodromy at a pole bearing zeros gives an order-three element with z three-cycles, 1 ≤ z ≤ 4. The project's d14 Theorem G(a), applied to the primitive action on the blocks, gives A_m or m ≤ max(6z,z²) ≤ 24. Thus n>48 suffices. The matching lemma also applies at smaller areas whenever its group hypotheses hold. ## What was checked and what remains

The checker reads the nine named D72 BBBB rooms and their 747 marked pairs, computes diagonal orbits, and sums the valencies of precisely the distinct orbits meeting the incident-sheet product. It does not generate any directions or rooms. There are zero low pairs. Four pairs in dumb12 have area 20; the other 743 meet the n ≥ 21 hypothesis in [the midpoint note](midpoint-necessity.md). Of the 747 pairs, 566 satisfy this note’s n > 48 area condition. In st279 (n=56) the external orbit has valency 54. The remaining orbital of valency one gives a pairing: its equivariant permutation commutes with the group and is an involution because its inverse is also an orbital of valency one and there is only one such orbital. Of the 120 marked pairs, 104 have d=54 and 16 have d=55. Thus a room with four canonical factors equal to B can still have blocks. [Checker](low-valency.py) · [Complete counts](low-valency.txt)

The primitive-sheet branch already satisfies d=n−1 at n>64 by d14. For an imprimitive sheet action choose a maximal proper block system (primitive quotient). Its block size k is at most eight: the quotient has a zero by R1 and every zero has k reflex preimages. The result above handles k=2 at n>48. The [spare-block argument](three-sheet.md) extends the reduction to larger blocks at n ≥ 120, leaving the single eight-sheet configuration. Its [direct-visibility proof](eight-sheet.md) was independently reviewed by d79. The finite exceptional degrees were not enumerated.
