# One middle winding handles the seven pyramid placements on a length region

D169 leaves seven position-type pairs among the five pyramid order types. Each has a sweep of the following form, with the full side walls physical throughout each open band. Start in width w, cross one middle band of width u and travel into a final run of width V. Each run is contained transversely in its successor and shares one side with it. The two shared sides can differ. Write a>0 for initial travel, d>0 for the full middle travel, and b>0 for the travel from the final gate to the target. No top or bottom cap is required.

**All-abscissa lemma.** If wb≤ud, all interior placements at those levels are lit. This holds for real lengths and real marks. Put the common side of the first joint at zero, so the middle width is [0,u]. Its common side with the broad run is c=0 or c=u. Let x,z be physical endpoint coordinates in this chart. Set L=d+b and D=a+L. Choose i nearest to (dz−cL)/(2ub) and set M=(z+2ui)/L. Then R=dM−2ui satisfies |R−c|≤ub/L. Choose X in [−w,w] congruent to x+Ma modulo 2w. Start at developed coordinate X−Ma with slope M−X/D. The first joint coordinate is XL/D, strictly within w of zero. The next joint, after removing 2ui, is R+Xb/D, within strictly less than ub/L+wb/L≤u of c. The two physical folding maps agree throughout this window: they both fold about zero when c=0, and about the common right wall u when c=u. A zero displacement from a common wall is a legal smooth side contact. At the final mark the correction vanishes and the coordinate is z. The whole path stays in the slab between the endpoint levels, regardless of missing mirrors above or below. An interior target on the far boundary line of its run is allowed, provided a>0 and b>0.

**Per-column application.** In the saved masks 761, 763, 6131 and 5111 use the descending runs starting from the top mark. In 8051 start at the bottom and go up. The other mark is in the third run. In each mask it suffices that w times the **full depth of the target row** be at most u times the middle height. All five unit-gap realizations satisfy this condition strictly. Since d169 covers the other 1,266 position-type pairs, this gives full real-pair illumination in these five rooms and a length region in *each* order type. After the broad-band extension below, unresolved realizations for this test must satisfy both w·h_T>u·d and wV>u² in the canonical rank-grid frame. The proof does not assert that violation implies darkness.

**Broad-band windings extend the inequality.** Replace z by z+2Vj in the one-middle proof. The residual numerator at the second gate is dz−cL+2dVj−2ubi. Rounding i with j fixed gives absolute error at most ub as before. Alternatively fix i and round j, giving error at most dV. Thus min(ub,dV)+wb≤u(d+b) suffices, using D>L for strict clearance. In particular either b≤ud/w or b≥d(V−u)/(u−w) suffices. These ranges overlap for *every depth* whenever wV≤u². This test is independent of all longitudinal lengths. It applies separately to all seven pyramid placements and to those three-corner placements admitting the same nested slab. For example in mask 8052 the bottom-to-middle-to-upper runs have widths 1,3,4 at unit horizontal gaps, with opposite shared sides. The test lights the bottom-tip to upper broad-run placements for arbitrary positive vertical gaps, including the interior mark at the upper edge of that broad run. A mark strictly in the additional top tip requires a different argument. For the five pyramid masks in their d169 canonical frames one can use horizontal gaps all equal to one: the (w,u,V) triples are (1,2,3), (1,2,3), (2,3,4), (1,3,4), (1,2,4) in mask order 761,763,8051,6131,5111. All satisfy the criterion. Consequently all pyramid rooms in all five order types with equal horizontal rank gaps and arbitrary vertical gaps are fully lit, in the displayed canonical orientation. This does not cover arbitrary horizontal gaps.
