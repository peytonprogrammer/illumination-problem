Advances: L1 — the converse from darkness to a costly obstruction. A dark pair need not give any proper pillowcase factor. There is a proof of an equal-cost obstruction when all three off-classes are corner-only upstairs, and a sharp count for a single separated parity fold. The missing case is darkness with regular off-class sites and no separating fold.

### (a) What closure of the legal moves actually proves

Fix two regular lifts over the same pillowcase pole a, another pole x, and initially take N=B so that the endpoint images agree. In state α let Eα mean connection by a regular-interior walk over that one arc. FC identifies it with a cyclic orbit on a live cycle or the orbit of the two partial end involutions on a live path. If the common image u is a pole, work on incident **sheets** in its block, allowing either incidence of each marked 2-cycle, as corrected in d62. Then x-light is the union of Eα over the states for x. Physical darkness requires failure for every compatible lift choice and every direction class. The cited equal-image, same-pole criterion alone does not cover different-parity endpoints. [FC §§1–3](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d61-fold-converse/README.md) · [d62, domain correction](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d62-review-d61/review.md)

Here is a precise way to test the proposed closure. On a generic sheet fiber Ω let G be full monodromy. It acts transitively because Q is connected. For a collection A of pairs of sheets one wants identified, draw edges {gi,gj} for every {i,j} in A and every g in G. Its connected components are the finest G-invariant equivalence identifying A. Indeed G permutes the edges, and any invariant equivalence containing A must identify the vertices of each edge and hence of each component. A nontrivial proper component partition gives an intermediate cover by ordinary covering theory on the punctured base and completion at the poles.

For the canonical parity partition B_x, take A to be the pairs in the 2-cycles at x. This construction always gives *genuine* blocks, possibly singletons or one universal block. For a proposed reachability partition one must additionally prove **separation survives closure**: some target incidence stays outside the appropriate component for the claimed obstruction (and a physical certificate must account for all lift choices). Darkness says it stays out of each relevant single-state orbit. It says nothing about a chain alternating between states or about arbitrary G-translates of legal moves. Even the union of genuine blocks is an informative assertion only with a specified partition and a separation conclusion: every set is a union of singleton blocks. The d14 degree-ten abstract cover cited in d61 has two composable x-lit pairs whose endpoints are x-dark. This refutes an FC-only transitivity proof for one class. It does not assert a physical room dark in all classes.

**What a block would have to realize on U.** Fix a primitive tail arc α from the source parity a to an off-class x. Each traversal has the same developed length. Here a midpoint witness means a realized midpoint of a legal path, and the direction class for this x is x−a. A legal FC walk joining the two a-lifts has 2j traversals for some positive integer j. At half-time its base vertex is x precisely when j is odd, since the base alternates a,x. Hence an off-class midpoint witness is equivalent to a legal walk of length **2 modulo 4** for a single state, a single arc and compatible endpoint incidences. Indeed, running such a walk inward from both ends traces the marked pair cover U up to its regular diagonal coincidence above x. Conversely the two compatible projections of such an approach concatenate to that walk. Incidences and straight continuation at every interior regular vertex are part of legality.

Thus for the pinned, nondirect, same-parity pair in a positive eight-reflex BBBB chart, the block argument would have to produce a single-state walk with that length residue under the candidate-count hypotheses (normalized room, area n≥21, projection degree d≥2n/3). A walk of length 0 modulo 4 would already light the pair but would have its midpoint over a. Equality in the canonical block records neither a common state nor this residue. The two finite pinned controls in d216 are lit throughout M=7 through 12 and furnish no dark instance. ### (b) Where corner avoidance helps, and a physical limit on the converse

At a live pole end, retaining exactly the 2-cycles keeps the reversals that pass through regular upstairs points. A fixed sheet adds only a loop. A 3-cycle would continue through a reflex corner. Deleting those edges corrects the orbit **in the chosen state**. It does not allow a change of developed direction at the junction of two legal words. Full monodromy is the group needed to construct a cover even though its paths are unsuitable as lighting witnesses. Restricting its generators to partial maps changes the path problem and supplies no covering-space correspondence for the resulting orbits. The canonical comparison is d61's open realization lemma: if the lifts have one image in Q/B_x and neither Cut(x) nor Rig(x) accounts for failure, find one state joining them. Even a certificate separately at each x would require further work to produce one common factor.

There is a physical obstruction to any unconditional proof of proper-factor necessity. The strictly simple R34-base double has degree 19. Its only regular lattice sites have even-even parity, including the two interior marks. A nonzero developed displacement between the marks is even-even. Its first primitive step is strictly intermediate and folds to a site of different parity, necessarily a corner. Thus the pair is dark. Primality forbids 19=km with k,m>1; the outer pillowcase itself has z=0. No choice of restricted transitions can recover the requested proper factor here. This refutes the fold-only assertion and leaves the equal-cost alternative intact. [R34 data and proof](fold-converse.md)

For this alternative there is a local count. Suppose all three off-classes are corner-only upstairs. Each of the four axial neighbors of each interior mark touches two cells of its star and must therefore be reflex. If a neighbor is shared, equal parity forces axial separation two (the other shared-neighbor displacement, diagonal (±1,±1), changes parity). Two opposite interior stars would require all four cells at the shared site, contradicting its being reflex. Thus shared neighbors are impossible under the hypothesis. Otherwise there are eight distinct axial reflexes. The east neighbor of one mark has a third incident cell. Together with a cell of the mark's star it supplies two cells at one eastern diagonal neighbor. That site is off-class, hence another reflex, of parity different from all eight axial neighbors. This proves r≥9 in this branch, including prime-degree examples. [Local count](fold-converse.md)

### (c) What can be excluded numerically

Write k=deg(Q→N), m=deg(N→B), and z for the number of quotient zeros. Every quotient zero has k unramified reflex preimages by the angle bound 3π upstairs. In fact r=kz+c₃, where c₃ counts cubic points over quotient poles. For a **single** proper factor with all three off-classes corner-only in N and disjoint, regular marked image sets over a, let q count smooth sites over the base poles. Riemann–Hurwitz gives q=2m−2−2z. Separation and occupancy give 2≤q≤⌊m/2⌋. With kz≤8 and k≥2 the possibilities (k,z,m) are (2,2,4), (3,2,4), (4,2,4), (2,3,5), (2,4,6). Their upstairs areas are at most 16, already covered by the census. The separated-fold argument also eliminates these rows geometrically. Thus there is no surviving small (k,z) in this branch. [Count and geometric eliminations](fold-converse.md)

Without separation or the three corner-only classes the q inequalities are unavailable. Even the trivial quotient N=B, with z=0, can leave the pair in one block for every x. In the normalized BBBB core each B_x is universal, so **any** quotient corner-only over x has outer degree one. The first open case is therefore prior to assigning any proper (k,z): a specified nondirect same-class pair in a positive eight-reflex BBBB chart, with no available separating parity factor. If one merely posits an unrelated proper factor with a zero, the smallest numerical case (k,z)=(2,1) costs only two reflexes by the fiber count. FC gives no reason for such a factor to explain darkness. Neither this numerical possibility nor the open BBBB case is a dark-room example. [BBBB scope](fold-converse.md)

### Hand check on the two d61 dark rooms

Both rooms have the named N5 parity quotient with distinct regular marked images and all three off-classes corner-only in N5. Upstairs R22 and P6 have four and six off-class smooth sites respectively. The following argument applies to every slope since each primitive arc joins a to its off-class x. Thus 2≤q≤⌊5/2⌋ gives q=2 and z=(2·5−2−2)/2=3. The inner degrees follow from the respective areas. At each x a lifted regular-interior walk projects to a live walk in N5. Each live x-vertex is a pole of degree one, so it can only send the walk back to the same a-vertex. No sequence of excursions takes it to the distinct target a-image. This proves separation for all states by hand **given the quotient**.

These examples verify the forward certificate and its cost. R34 explains why reversing that verification requires a separate geometric alternative.
