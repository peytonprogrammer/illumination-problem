**Local visibility lemma.** In a strictly simple orthogonal lattice room, two interior lattice points with coordinate differences (2,0), up to square symmetry, see each other directly. All four unit cells incident to an interior lattice point belong to the room. For endpoints (0,0),(2,0), the squares immediately above and below the first half of the segment are supplied by the first endpoint, and the squares above and below the second half by the other endpoint. This includes the full star of the midpoint. The complete segment is interior. In cov4181 the four intervening cells have lower-left corners (2,2),(2,3),(3,2),(3,3). The proof applies at every wall count, in every factor type.

**Diagonal comparison.** For displacements (2,2), the two endpoints force the southwest and northeast cells at the midpoint. The other two cells decide direct visibility. Both present means interior, one absent means a reflex corner, both absent would be a forbidden pinch. The two open half-segments lie inside the forced cells. Thus a dark pair at this displacement requires a reflex corner exactly at the midpoint. R22 has one at (1,1); its southeast cell is absent. The manuscript's folding argument excludes the other shots. **Limit of counting by midpoint class.** For a developed path from an integer start with primitive velocity v and parameter length exactly two, an intermediate corner can only occur at time one. Reflected lattice corners have integer coordinates; if t v is integral, Bezout gives integral t. Folding across integer walls preserves both coordinate parities, so this corner has class S+v modulo two. Two such midpoint interruptions in distinct classes would require physically distinct corners. But this counts only actual corner interruptions for that length, and an interrupted path by itself specifies no physical target beyond the interruption. Longer shots can encounter corners at even steps of source parity, shared across direction classes. The D66 negative verdict gives no assignment of distinct reflex corners to failed classes.

**Verdict.** There is no wall to identify on the successful cov4181 path. Even adding walls cannot suppress it while the room stays on the same grid and both marks at these coordinates stay interior. Consequently it cannot model a transition from eight to nine reflex corners. R22 charges one corner for its diagonal shot. No ninth-corner bound follows, and this control should be retired for that particular counting argument. No discovery search was used.
