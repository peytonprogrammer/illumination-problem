      GENERATING THE HOMOLOGY OF COVERS OF SURFACES

                     MARCO BOGGI, ANDREW PUTMAN, AND NICK SALTER


         Abstract. Putman and Wieland conjectured that if Σ        e → Σ is a finite branched cover
         between closed oriented surfaces of sufficiently high genus, then the orbits of all nonzero
         elements of H1 (Σ;
                         e Q) under the action of lifts to Σ e of mapping classes on Σ are infinite.
         We prove that this holds if H1 (Σ; Q) is generated by the homology classes of lifts of simple
                                         e
         closed curves on Σ. We also prove that the subspace of H1 (Σ; e Q) spanned by such lifts is
         a symplectic subspace. Finally, simple closed curves lie on subsurfaces homeomorphic to
         2-holed spheres, and we prove that H1 ( eΣ; Q) is generated by the homology classes of lifts
         of loops on Σ lying on subsurfaces homeomorphic to 3-holed spheres.
