# Exact endpoint-fiber condition for any proposed repair

HAND prerequisite, not a positive coverage theorem. The exact-original-pair one-copy assertion is already refuted. This records the smallest distinct proposed repair and a necessary sheet rule for auditing it.

Let rho:M->P be physical projection, G={1,Rx,Ry,RxRy} the four copy-label involutions, p an exact source lift, and q an exact target lift. Since T is interior, its physical fiber is exactly Gq. A revised sufficient assertion would be:

For every native L pair there are g in G, a genuine Sigma-preserving global affine F, and one identified polygon copy C, such that F(p) and F(gq) lie in closure(C) and their straight segment avoids Sigma throughout.

If true, inverse transport gives a single regular straight unfolded connection from p to gq, hence an actual S-to-T ray. It is strictly different from fixing g=1. The pinned L8 counterexample rejects g=1 and g=RxRy but admits g=Rx through H2; this proves neither universal validity nor universal failure of the revised assertion.

## Exact necessity for choosing a visible physical projection

After selecting F, the permitted target set is F(Gq), equivalently (F G F^-1)F(q). It need not equal G F(q). Therefore projecting F(q) to the room and selecting any visible copy-lift of that projected position is not justified. A seed endpoint z is valid precisely when F^-1(z) belongs to Gq (with the source endpoint likewise restored to p).

This distinction is already nontrivial on the accepted control. H2 has derivative H=[[1,2],[0,1]]. Rx has derivative J=[[-1,0],[0,1]]. The conjugated involution has derivative

H J H^-1 = [[-1,4],[0,1]],

which is not the derivative of any original copy-label involution (their derivatives are diagonal signs). Thus H2 does not normalize the physical deck-label group. A proof cannot interchange F(Gq) and G F(q) by claiming the entire physical fiber is automatically preserved. Even though there are four possible original target lifts, their images must be tracked through the genuine global map, with translations and actual sheets as well as derivatives.

This is a falsifiable new coverage lemma and a necessary endpoint qualification, not an equivalent equation offered as a solution. No existence proof for it follows from the accepted shear criteria, orbit finiteness, or the current countercontrol. Before any ten-wall use, the missing global assertion must be proved or rigorously refuted on the full native L family. The broad r<=8 objective remains open.
