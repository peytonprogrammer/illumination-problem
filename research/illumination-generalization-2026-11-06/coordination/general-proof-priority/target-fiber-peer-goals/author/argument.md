## 2. Complete stabilizer of a coordinate foliation

For an integral L P(A,a,B,H), A>a>0,H>B>0, let Aff_Sigma(M) be the full affine homeomorphism group preserving every physical corner lift. Its vertical maximal cylinders have transverse intervals(-a,a), (a,2A-a) and vertical circumferences2H,2B, respectively. Regular convex corner marks do not change these cylinders as translation-flow cylinders; only the genuine cone bounds them.

Any affine map preserving the unoriented vertical foliation maps maximal vertical cylinders onto maximal vertical cylinders. Write its derivative as [[alpha,0],[k,delta]]. A primitive core goes to a primitive core, so its circumference is scaled by abs(delta), without multiple winding. The set of distinct circumferences {2H,2B} must be permuted after this common scaling. Their product forces abs(delta)=1; because H>B the cylinders cannot be exchanged. Global area preservation gives abs(alpha)=1.

Compose with actual copy involutions to arrange alpha=delta=1. The resulting derivative is [[1,0],[k,1]]. Each cylinder is preserved. Its transverse interval maps by x->x+c onto itself, so c=0. Thus in cylinder coordinates the map is y->y+kx+d_left or y->y+kx+d_right.

On each side of the left cylinder boundary, the inner roof corner(a,H) is the ONLY regular point of Sigma on that boundary. The other marked boundary occurrences are of the unique cone. Preservation of the cylinder, its side germs and Sigma therefore fixes the roof occurrence at y=H on BOTH sides. It follows that

    k*a+d_left = 0 modulo2H,
    -k*a+d_left = 0 modulo2H.

Hence ka/H is integral and d_left=ka modulo2H. The two sides of the right cylinder each contain a unique cone occurrence at y=B modulo2B. Fixing these occurrences gives

    k*a+d_right = 0 modulo2B,
    k*(2A-a)+d_right = 0 modulo2B.

Hence k(A-a)/B is integral and d_right=-ka modulo2B. These are precisely the known anchored maps V_k, with no free vertical translation. Conversely their pointwise boundary shifts and common derivative give genuine global maps preserving Sigma, as previously independently reviewed.

Thus the FULL vertical-foliation stabilizer is G times the V_k family, with these exact parameter conditions. This statement classifies that stabilizer, NOT the full affine group. For integral metrics its real parameter group is

    k in (HB/gcd(aB,H(A-a))) Z.

Whole-room transposition gives the full horizontal-foliation stabilizer G times H_l, with

No mandatory Z-preservation condition was imposed in this classification. These are all real parameters satisfying the resulting stabilizer conditions, not just a selected integral-twist subgroup.

    Rx V_k Rx = V_-k,    Ry V_k Ry = V_-k,
