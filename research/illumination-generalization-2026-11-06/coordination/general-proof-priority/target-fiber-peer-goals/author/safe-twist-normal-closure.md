# All-direction safe twists and their normal closure cannot move the branch fiber

This supplements the separately pinned outside-subgroup.md without editing it. It proves a method obstruction for every primitive rational direction and every conjugate of the specified safe twists. It does not prove a full affine-group obstruction, or resolve complete-target-fiber coverage.

## Exact setting

Use any accepted odd-d obstruction-family L surface M. Its exact absolute period lattice is Lambda=4Z². Let pi:M->R²/Lambda be integration modulo Lambda. The unique cone has image c=(2,2); write U=pi inverse(c). The original source p and all four original target lifts gq belong to U.

Every genuine affine automorphism F with derivative A preserves Lambda. Integration gives pi(Fz)=A pi(z)+b on the torus. Since F fixes the unique cone, b=c-Ac. Thus pi(Fz)-c=A(pi(z)-c). In particular F permutes U exactly. This uses the EXACT period torus, not an arbitrarily chosen coarser torus; arbitrary coarser lattices need not be preserved by F.

## Safe twist in an arbitrary primitive rational direction

Let (u,v) be any primitive nonzero integer vector, and s=u²+v². The base torus cut along its closed circle through c is an annulus with circumference ell=4 sqrt(s) and transverse width w=4/sqrt(s). Thus ell=s w.

As in the pinned diagonal construction, lifting and merging strips gives finitely many maximal cylinders with circumference m_j ell and width v_j w for positive integers m_j,v_j. Choose L=lcm_j m_j. The simultaneous twist with common shear tau=s L is identity on every cylinder boundary, since

    tau v_j w/(m_j ell)=L v_j/m_j in Z.

It is a genuine affine map of M, with common xy derivative

    I + L [[-u v,u²],[-v²,u v]].

The matrix is a nontrivial unipotent fixing (u,v). The gluing argument and inverse apply just as in the diagonal case. The already accepted L symmetry lemma ensures preservation of every physical corner lift.

If z in U lies on a cylinder boundary, the twist fixes it. Otherwise its transverse offset is r w for an integer r, because z and the boundary project onto the same base circle through c. Its displacement divided by circumference is

    tau r w/(m_j ell)=L r/m_j in Z.

So the safe twist fixes EVERY point of U individually. This argument is quantified over all primitive (u,v); it does not enumerate any of them or follow a billiard orbit.

## Global conjugates and arbitrary words

Let Q be the subgroup generated by every such safe twist and all of their conjugates by the FULL affine group. Every affine F permutes U. If T fixes U pointwise, then for z in U,

    F T F inverse(z)=F(F inverse(z))=z.

Therefore every generator of Q, every inverse and every finite product fixes U pointwise. Q is normal by construction.

This conclusion is stronger than failure of two coordinate directions. It excludes every word using these safe twists in arbitrary rational directions and their full-group conjugates as a way to move p or the complete original target fiber. It does not exclude smaller compatible cylinder twists, or other affine maps.

Let N be the original copy/diagonal symmetry group of this self-dual room. Since Q is normal, <Q,N>=QN. For F=n k with k in Q, Fp=n p and F(gq)=n(gq). The accepted original same-copy segment hits the reflex, and for g!=1 the points occupy distinct copy interiors after n. Thus the entire group <Q,N> fails ALL four target-lift seed choices on the infinite odd-d family.

## What a successful map must do

The action rho:Aff(M)->Sym(U) is well-defined and finite, but its image has not been determined here. If rho(F)=rho(n) for some n in N, the same endpoint obstruction applies. A successful map must act on the actual endpoint fibers differently from the original copy/diagonal symmetry action.

This is NOT a claim that such an action is impossible or that rho(Aff)=rho(N). No full-group orbit is computed or classified. It identifies the missing ingredient more sharply: existence of many parabolics, including maps outside the failed coordinate subgroup, does not imply useful movement in this finite physical fiber. Arbitrarily increasing twist powers until gluing is automatic systematically erases precisely that movement.
