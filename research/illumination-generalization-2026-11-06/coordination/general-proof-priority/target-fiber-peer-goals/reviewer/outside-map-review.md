# Independent audit of the diagonal outside-subgroup map

Decision: ACCEPT both scoped claims. The map is a genuine global affine automorphism outside the previously excluded subgroup. The safe version fixes all relevant original endpoint lifts, so it does not furnish a seed. The full affine gate remains OPEN. The author source pin matched before and after review.

## 1. Cylinder existence and global affine gluing: ACCEPT

An integral L unfolding maps by signed translation coordinates to an integral square torus (a unit square torus always suffices). The unique cone has one branch value c; regular marked corners need not be additional branch values. In the specified family the exact primitive square period is 4, as independently reviewed.

Cutting that torus along its primitive diagonal through c removes every branch value from the complement. The complement is an open annulus of circumference ell=b*sqrt(2) and transverse width w=b/sqrt(2), so ell=2w. A connected finite unbranched annulus cover is again an annulus, with circumference m*ell and unchanged width w. There are only finitely many such components.

If the whole boundary orbit between two strips is regular, their identification is a homeomorphism of closed core circles. The core periods must agree, not merely divide one another. Such strips merge to a cylinder with the same m and an integer multiple v*w of the transverse width. If a boundary orbit contains a cone, even though its remaining arcs are regular, it is a genuine saddle-connection boundary and cannot be crossed by extending a whole open cylinder. A closed chain consisting entirely of regular strip boundaries would form a torus component without a cone, impossible in connected genus-two M. Hence the maximal diagonal cylinders indeed have circumferences m_j*ell and widths v_j*w with positive integers m_j,v_j.

For L=lcm(m_j), the map (xi,eta)->(xi+2L*eta,eta) has upper-boundary displacement 2L*v_j*w=(L*v_j/m_j)*(m_j*ell), an integral full circumference. Both boundaries are fixed pointwise. Each cylinder has the same flat-frame derivative I+2L e n^t. The maps and their inverses agree across each regular saddle-connection arc; their common derivative makes the pasted map affine there. They extend continuously and bijectively at the unique cone. This supplies an actual global automorphism, not an unrelated collection of cylinder twists.

With e=(1,1)/sqrt(2), n=(-1,1)/sqrt(2), the derivative is [[1-L,L],[-L,1+L]], determinant 1 and nontrivial unipotent. It fixes the diagonal direction. Preservation of all regular physical corners follows from the accepted L centrality/automatic-Sigma result, not simply from fixing the true cone. This distinction is correctly maintained.

This rational-cylinder lifting principle is a known accepted type of construction; the new comparison with the precise failed coordinate subgroup must not be interpreted as a new universal endpoint theorem.

## 2. Outside the entire declared subgroup: ACCEPT

Write lambda=d(2d+1)>2. In projective coordinate s=x/y, H^a(s)=s+a*lambda and V^a(s)=1/(a*lambda+1/s). For |s|<1, |H^a(s)|>=lambda-|s|>1. For |s|>1, |V^a(s)|<=1/(lambda-1/|s|)<1. The projective infinity case gives 1/(a*lambda), also strictly inside. The first application to s=1 satisfies the same strict inequalities for either generator, for every nonzero signed exponent.

After merging adjacent same-generator powers and cancelling inverse blocks, any nonempty alternating word therefore sends 1 strictly into |s|>1 or |s|<1, never to +1 or -1. This simultaneously justifies the absence of hidden relations used in the word description. Every signed permutation n preserves the two-point set {+1,-1} and its complement. Thus n*k cannot fix slope 1 unless k is empty. Such an element belongs to the finite symmetry group and cannot have a nontrivial unipotent derivative.

The constructed map has precisely that derivative and fixed slope, hence is outside the ENTIRE subgroup generated by both complete coordinate stabilizers and the stated symmetries. No finite-index theorem, derivative enumeration or orbit census is needed. This is stronger than merely observing that its matrix is not one individual shear.

## 3. Exact endpoint action: ACCEPT

For every z over c, either z is on a maximal-cylinder boundary, where the map is pointwise identity, or its transverse cylinder coordinate is r*w with integral 0<r<v_j. This follows from projection to the cut circle, not from an assumed physical copy. Its displacement is 2L*r*w=(L*r/m_j)*(m_j*ell), an integral full circumference. Therefore the exact surface point is fixed, including its covering-sheet label.

All four original target lifts and all source lifts in the odd-d family project to c. The safe map consequently fixes them individually. For nonidentity g the nominated endpoints stay in distinct copy interiors. For g=1 the original corner-hitting segment is unchanged. Nontrivial powers of the map retain these endpoint failures. Conjugating by the declared copy/diagonal symmetries still fixes the union of both physical fibers pointwise, because that union is invariant under those symmetries. The claim is not made for arbitrary full-group conjugators.

Thus genuine outside-subgroup existence has no demonstrated endpoint-coverage force. This exact action calculation is essential; the nontrivial matrix alone does not establish endpoint motion.

## 4. Smaller compatible twist conditions: ACCEPT as prerequisites only

A common shear tau glues pointwise on each upper boundary exactly when tau*v_j/(2*m_j) is integral. For a specified branch-fiber point at transverse coordinate r*w, it is fixed exactly when tau*r/(2*m_j) is integral. These statements retain full longitudinal periods m_j*ell rather than reducing only modulo the base period ell; they are therefore exact actual-sheet conditions.

The first condition does not entail failure of the second for any prescribed mark. A boundary mark never moves under such boundary-fixing twists. No claim of minimum tau, favorable cylinder occupancy, nonzero endpoint displacement, same-copy placement or seed visibility is proved. Actual target attainment cannot be inferred from the common direction, finite cover or outside-subgroup status.
