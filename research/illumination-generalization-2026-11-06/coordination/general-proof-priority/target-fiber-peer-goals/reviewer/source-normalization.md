# Source-fiber normalization without assuming F normalizes G

HAND exact equivalence for the assigned gate. This does not prove coverage.

One may temporarily allow selection of a source lift hp as well as a target lift gq, with h,g in G, while keeping a SINGLE common affine map F and a SINGLE polygon-copy seed. This is equivalent to the assigned criterion with nominated source p.

Given a regular seed from F(hp) to F(gq), set F'=Fh and g'=h^-1 g. Each copy-label involution h is itself a genuine global Sigma-preserving affine automorphism. Because G is a group, g' belongs to G. Then

F'(p)=F(hp), and F'(g'q)=F(hh^-1 gq)=F(gq).

Thus exactly the same seed works, and its inverse under F' restores the nominated source p and the actual target lift g'q. Conversely, the assigned criterion is the special case h=1.

For a fixed F this means that searching for a seed between F(Gp) and F(Gq), with one source and one target from those exact finite sets, does not enlarge the global quantified criterion. Precomposing by h leaves the entire allowed target set unchanged:

Fh(Gq)=F(Gq),

because hG=G. This is a group identity before applying F. It does not assert Fh=hF or replace F(Gq) by GF(q).

Combined with the central-J lemma, both exact sets split into two J-antipodal pairs. Their members need not fill all four physical polygon copies, and even a common-copy pair need not be visible in a nonconvex L. No existence conclusion follows from pair counts, pairing or this normalization alone. Nevertheless, a constructor may choose an arbitrary source-fiber member first and then normalize by precomposition; it need not prove that F itself fixes a nominated source sheet in advance.
