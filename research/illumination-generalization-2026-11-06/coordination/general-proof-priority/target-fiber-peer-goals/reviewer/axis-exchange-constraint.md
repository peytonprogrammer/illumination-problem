# Full-group axis-exchange and derivative constraints

HAND consequences for the whole L family, jointly using the independently accepted derivative-kernel and coordinate-stabilizer proofs. These are exact necessary conditions and a conditional global automorphism construction; they are not endpoint seed availability.

## A derivative cannot be repaired by an independent translation

On the actual L unfolding, the derivative homomorphism is injective. If genuine affine maps F1,F2 have the same derivative, F2^-1 F1 has derivative I, hence is identity. Thus F1=F2 as actual maps, including all translations, cone prongs and regular sheets. A matrix known to be realized by one affine map cannot be supplemented with another convenient sheet permutation or translation at the same derivative. This does not prove an arbitrary matrix is realized.

In particular, if a derivative is a word in the known coordinate-stabilizer/symmetry derivatives, the corresponding word of actual maps realizes it. Injectivity makes this the only realization. An additional hypothetical map with that same derivative cannot evade the accepted odd-d fixed-pair obstruction by choosing different offsets.

## Exact criterion for interchanging vertical and horizontal foliations

For P=[0,A]x[0,B] union [0,a]x[B,H], assume 0<a<A and 0<B<H. There is an affine automorphism sending the vertical foliation to the horizontal foliation if and only if

aH=AB.

Necessity: the two maximal vertical circumferences are 2H>2B. The two horizontal circumferences are 2A>2a. A single affine derivative scales every vertical primitive core by the same positive factor when mapping it horizontally. Primitive cores map with degree +1 or -1, not multiple winding. Multiplication by that factor must send the ordered pair (2H,2B) to (2A,2a), giving A/H=a/B. Hence aH=AB. The same argument applies to a map sending horizontal to vertical.

Sufficiency: when aH=AB, set c=A/H=a/B and define the physical linear map

S(x,y)=(c*y,x/c).

This is an involution with determinant -1. The lower rectangle [0,A]x[0,B] maps exactly onto [0,a]x[0,H]; the latter maps exactly onto the lower rectangle. Their union is P. The lower rectangular part and the full left rectangular part overlap, but their union is precisely the original L. Thus S maps the entire actual polygon onto itself, permuting its finite walls and convex/reflex corners.

On the four-copy unfolding, swap the two reflection labels as well as the physical coordinates. In signed translation coordinates the single common derivative is [[0,c],[1/c,0]]. The image of each edge gluing is the corresponding swapped-edge gluing, so this descends to a genuine global affine involution of M preserving Sigma. Its inverse is itself. It need not preserve the integer-lift set; no such preservation is required here. The construction uses the metric equality, not merely an arbitrary room transpose identifying two different surfaces.

## What a zero matrix entry implies

Let DF=[[alpha,beta],[gamma,delta]] be any genuine affine derivative.

- beta=0 means the vertical foliation is preserved, so F is already in the classified full vertical stabilizer.
- gamma=0 means the horizontal foliation is preserved, so F is already in the classified full horizontal stabilizer.
- alpha=0 sends the horizontal direction to vertical. This requires aH=AB; composing with S turns F into a horizontal stabilizer.
- delta=0 sends the vertical direction to horizontal. This also requires aH=AB; composing with S turns F into a vertical stabilizer.

Consequently every genuine affine map with at least one zero derivative entry belongs to the group generated by the two full coordinate stabilizers and, when it exists, the weighted axis interchange S. When aH is not AB, no affine map can exchange the coordinate foliations at all; zero diagonal entries are impossible.

## Application to the accepted all-four subgroup countercontrol

The author's infinite family has A=H=2(2d+1), a=B=2d, so S is its actual diagonal isometry. Its group of coordinate stabilizers and copy/diagonal symmetries is proved insufficient for all four target lifts. Hence on that family any affine map furnishing an admitted one-copy seed must have all FOUR derivative entries nonzero. More strongly, its derivative must lie outside the derivative subgroup generated by the already excluded moves, because derivative injectivity forbids another realization of the same word.

These conditions are necessary only. Products of known stabilizer derivatives can also have all four entries nonzero, and such products are already excluded. A matrix with four nonzero entries therefore supplies neither a genuine affine map nor seed availability. Global edge gluing and the exact F(Gq) images still have to be proved.

This narrows the required outside-subgroup construction without expanding the target class, changing the nominated physical endpoints, or proposing a new example. It provides no all-native-L coverage theorem or full-group counterexample. No numerical check, discovery search, orbit census, engine, worker, git action or effort-clock renewal was used.
