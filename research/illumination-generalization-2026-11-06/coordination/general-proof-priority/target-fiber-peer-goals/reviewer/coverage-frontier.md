# Full-group period-content guard and the remaining coverage quantifier

Hand derivation; no new search, census, checker, worker or discovery clock. This note is an independently justified necessary condition, followed by an exact demonstration that it does not obstruct the current residual family. It is not a new illumination theorem or a claim that the full affine action has been determined.

## 1. An intrinsic lattice for every integral L

Write P=[0,A]x[0,B] union [0,a]x[B,H], with integral 0<a<A and 0<B<H. The absolute period lattice of its actual four-copy translation unfolding is EXACTLY

Lambda = lambda_x Z x lambda_y Z,
lambda_x=2 gcd(A,a), lambda_y=2 gcd(H,B).

Indeed the signed-cross presentation has only horizontal gluing translations of lengths 2A or 2a and vertical translations of lengths 2H or 2B. Developing a closed curve gives the negative sum of its seam translations, so every absolute period lies in the displayed lattice. Conversely, primitive horizontal cylinder cores have periods (2A,0) and (2a,0), and vertical cores have periods (0,2H) and (0,2B). Integer linear combinations of their homology classes give both displayed generators. This proves equality, without presuming that each period generator is itself a legal optical ray.

Every global affine homeomorphism F induces an automorphism D of Lambda. In the displayed lattice basis its matrix U is in GL(2,Z), with determinant +1 or -1. The one genuine cone is fixed. Using its torus image as origin, the induced period-torus map is linear. Other regular corner marks need not be individually fixed. No free translation/sheet choice has been added to a realized affine map.

## 2. A full-group invariant for a specified actual connection

Fix actual lifts p and gq BEFORE applying F. Let gamma be a finite regular straight unfolded connection between them, and h its developed displacement, including every actual seam translation. Its torus class delta=[h] in R^2/Lambda depends only on the actual endpoints.

For these integral native marks delta is torsion. Let n be its exact order (n=1 when delta=0), and write

n h = lambda_x z_x e_x + lambda_y z_y e_y,
c(gamma)=gcd(|z_x|,|z_y|)>0.

Both n and c(gamma) are invariant under EVERY genuine global affine map: endpoint torus differences transform by U, and the integer vector (z_x,z_y) transforms by the same unimodular U. gcd is unchanged. Reversal and a lattice-basis change also preserve it. This concerns the developed holonomy of the actual path, not an arbitrarily chosen difference of projected coordinates.

The collection of these invariants over all regular connections p->Gq is transported to the collection for F(p)->F(Gq). It is NOT transported to the collection for F(p)->G F(q) unless normalization of G has separately been proved.

## 3. A necessary one-copy bound

Suppose a connection becomes a seed in closure of ONE actual polygon copy. Its developed displacement in that copy satisfies |h_x|<=A and |h_y|<=H, also for smooth-wall endpoints. Write (z_x,z_y)=c(v_x,v_y), with v primitive. At least one |v_i|>=1; hence

c/n <= max(A/lambda_x, H/lambda_y).

Equivalently c <= floor(n max(A/lambda_x,H/lambda_y)). Thus a full-group seed obstruction could be proved by excluding EVERY regular original connection to ALL FOUR original target lifts with this permitted content. It would be insufficient to check a single path or reflection word. The bound alone supplies no existence assertion and does not assert sufficiency.

A zero displacement cannot be an actual seed with distinct endpoints: a nonconstant regular straight segment has nonzero developed vector, and a homeomorphism preserves endpoint distinctness.

## 4. Why this invariant does not exclude the odd-d residual

Use the already assigned normalized family A=H=2d+1, a=B=d, odd d>=3, p=(2d-1,1), q=(1,2d-1). Here Lambda=2Z^2, and every original gq has the same period-torus class as p, so n=1. Any one-copy seed must have c<=d. This covers d=3 mod4 as well as d=1 mod4.

But the existing one-left-reflection physical witness develops to a regular straight connection p->Rxq with displacement

h=(-2d,2d-2), h/2=(-d,d-1), c=1.

Its true reflection point is (0,2d-2+1/d). The incoming line crosses x=d at y=d-1+1/d<d, and crosses y=d at x=d-1 strictly between 0 and d. It therefore leaves the right arm through the internal mouth, enters the upper arm internally, and first hits the actual left wall at a strictly smooth point between y=d and y=2d+1. The reflected final leg remains in the upper rectangle and first reaches T. All inequalities are strict for d>=3. This is the already known physical witness, used here only to compute the invariant; it is not a new sampled room or direction.

Consequently even the strongest possible rejection based ONLY on this period-content bound fails on the whole current odd-d family: a regular primitive-content connection already exists to one original target lift. Its endpoints are in different copy interiors, so that witness is not itself a one-copy seed. The unresolved question is whether some FULL affine map puts that connection, or another allowed connection, into one polygon copy while preserving the exact endpoint images.

## 5. Exact current frontier

- The old exact-original-q obstruction remains valid at its original scope; it does not exclude Rxq/Ryq.
- The new diagonal quarter-phase construction gives an exact Ryq seed for the specified d=1 mod4 pairs, subject to pending independent review.
- The conditional complete-diagonal result excludes all words in four controlled-foliation stabilizers plus room symmetries for d=3 mod4. That subgroup is not proved to exhaust Aff(M,Sigma).
- Full-group period content is a valid necessary guard, but the explicit primitive connection proves it cannot establish the desired negative on these residual pairs.
- The missing universal assertion remains simultaneous ordered-pair availability: for EVERY native L pair, some genuine F and some ORIGINAL g give a regular seed in one copy. No accepted argument yet proves this or a full-group all-four-lift obstruction.

Finite decidability, existence of an outside-subgroup map, known physical L illumination, and failure of the tested constructors do not close that assertion. There is no automatic ten-wall extension. Full r<=8 remains open.
