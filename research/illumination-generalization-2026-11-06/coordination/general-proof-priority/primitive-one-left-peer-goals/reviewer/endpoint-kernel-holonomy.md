# Same full endpoint-fiber action, unbounded actual-path holonomy

HAND consequence for the prescribed one-left gamma. The coordinate twists and their pointwise action on U are ACCEPTED dependencies from the previous phase, not new map constructions or availability mechanisms. The new point here is their effect on the present exact-path question after ANY genuine affine map.

Use normalized odd d>=3, A=2d+1, the actual four-copy unfolding M, its intrinsic cone fiber U, and gamma:p->ORIGINAL Rxq with h=2v0, v0=(-d,d-1). The assigned d=3 mod4 family is included. Every genuine affine F preserves U.

## Claim

For EVERY genuine global Sigma-preserving affine F, infinitely many genuine global maps have the SAME full permutation of U as F, hence the same exact images of p and ORIGINAL Rxq, but carry the prescribed gamma to a regular straight path that cannot lie in closure of ANY one polygon copy. Their actual developed holonomies are unbounded.

Consequently endpoint-fiber action alone does not control the image of the fixed path. If a compressor exists, its full U-permutation class also contains noncompressors. This does NOT assert that some class contains a compressor, or that no class does. It supplies neither a full-group negative for gamma nor a positive family theorem.

## Actual endpoint-kernel maps

Put lambda=dA. The accepted anchored vertical and horizontal twists with coefficients n lambda are genuine global affine maps preserving all physical corners, with derivatives

V_n: [[1,0],[n lambda,1]],
H_n: [[1,n lambda],[0,1]], n in Z.

They fix the ENTIRE U pointwise. Here is the relevant exact verification rather than a new search: U points have odd coordinates. In the left vertical cylinder, the anchored displacement divided by circumference 2A is

n lambda(x+d)/(2A)=n d(x+d)/2,

an integer for odd x,d. In the right vertical cylinder its representative remains odd under even seam translations, and the ratio is

n lambda(x-d)/(2d)=n A(x-d)/2,

also an integer. Actual cut boundaries are fixed pointwise by the admitted anchored gluing condition. The horizontal statement is the exact transposed construction. In particular it includes U points on ordinary physical walls; there is no assumption that they remain in polygon interiors.

## Same endpoint action after every F

Let F be ANY genuine global affine map and write w=DF v0. Its derivative acts unimodularly on the exact lattice 2Z², so w is a nonzero primitive integer vector. Both named endpoint images and every other F(U) point lie in U.

If w_x is nonzero, use F_n=V_n composed with F. These maps are genuine, Sigma-preserving, and act on U exactly as F because V_n fixes all of U. Their exact path holonomies are

h(F_n gamma)=2(w_x,w_y+n lambda w_x).

For every integer n with |n|lambda|w_x|>|w_y|+d, the second half-component has absolute value greater than d.

If w_x=0, primitivity gives w_y=+1 or -1. Use F_n=H_n composed with F instead. The holonomies are

h(F_n gamma)=2(n lambda w_y,w_y),

and every |n|>=1 makes the first half-component have absolute value lambda>d.

Thus in either case infinitely many maps have exactly the same full U permutation and exact endpoint images as F, but have unbounded developed displacement for THIS gamma.

## Why these are actual noncompressors

A straight segment in closure of ANY copy has coordinate displacement bounded by A=2d+1 in both directions. The paths above have an even displacement component with absolute value at least 2d+2. They therefore cannot be such a segment, even with smooth-wall endpoints. No choice of another copy representative can change the developed holonomy of the actual path: doing so would replace it with another winding class, forbidden in this assignment.

All image paths remain regular because each F_n is a genuine Sigma-preserving homeomorphism and the original gamma is regular. Their failure is not a singular continuation, failed endpoint match or incorrect target sheet. It is precisely the excess winding of the same prescribed path under genuine maps with identical endpoint-fiber action.
