# Winding assessment, 22 November 2026

## L1 decision

**Target.** In a strictly simple normalized orthogonal lattice room with W≤20, a dark pair of distinct interior lattice points implies area n≤20. This is the lattice-mark, axial part of L1 to which the completed L2 census applies. Non-lattice marks and mixed wall directions retain their L3 and L4 obligations. A descent would suffice if it took a hypothetical counterexample to a smaller counterexample in the same class, all the way into L2.

I rank the routes by their chance of delivering this target within one month at the present effort. My subjective estimates are roughly 5% for a structural classification of darkness certificates, 1% for one-way descent, and below 1% for exhaustive lighting tails. These are separate judgments, not additive probabilities. The first route can eliminate a whole monodromy action at every area. Descent has no general invariant known to survive shortening. Tails lack a covering argument when several dimensions grow together. The 22-wall counterexample prevents treating a cutoff independent of the other dimensions as automatic.

**First case.** Take the primitive action on unordered subsets of a finite set, including d81's unresolved pair-action portraits at areas 21 and 28. Its local permutations force a corner-only site. The input specific to the wall budget is r≤8 together with the reviewed all-area corner-only closure through W=20. This gives L1 for that entire branch. The proof below the link also specifies why the natural alternating action remains open. [First L1 branch proof](l1-primitive-subset-action.md)

**Decision.** Park common-period winding as the main proof strategy. Keep it for producing witnesses. The d175 notes await independent review. Strict failure on lit rooms is established for specified charts. Illumination of the exact irrational pair in the strict per-gate example was not proved by those notes.

**Odds.** My subjective chance that another month at the current scale produces a complete proof for every real interior pair in every strictly simple *orthogonal lattice* room with W≤20: about 1% via further winding arguments, about 5% via darkness-certificate completeness. These are judgments about finishing by a deadline, not probabilities that the mathematical assertion is true. I see no credible way to add up local winding witnesses to a theorem across all itineraries. Completeness at least confronts all directions in one statement. D154 and d64 already eliminate the known clean mechanisms for lattice pairs below 22 walls, but proving that every dark lattice pair has such a mechanism is itself the open core. Arbitrary real marks require additional work. I would give the certificate route one targeted attempt and reassess before funding a longer run.
