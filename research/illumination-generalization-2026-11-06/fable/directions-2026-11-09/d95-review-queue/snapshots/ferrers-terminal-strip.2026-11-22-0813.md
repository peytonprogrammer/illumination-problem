# All interior pairs in a ten-wall staircase under one condition on the lengths

Orient the Ferrers word LLLLLRLRLR with all four horizontal runs attached to the left wall. From top to bottom let their widths be w<u<v<W. Let the middle two heights be d,e and the bottom height h. The top height can be arbitrary. For every pair of distinct real interior marks, illumination holds if

    h/W ∉ span_Q {d/(2u), e/(2v)}.                                 (1)

The same conclusion holds under the transposed condition. In particular (1) settles almost every real length assignment of this word **for all marks simultaneously**. With the other parameters fixed, only countably many bottom heights fail (1). For rational rooms the following denominator test is useful. Choose P>0 with Pd/(2u) and Pe/(2v) integral. Put Q=floor(W/w). If Ph/W in lowest terms has denominator greater than Q², all pairs are lit. A sharper test excludes all sums mod 1 of two rationals with denominators at most Q. These conclusions are for review.

D15 leaves only the pair of opposite terminal cells, so take the source in the narrow top band and the target in the broad bottom band. Write a>0 for the source distance to its exit and 0<b<h for the target depth below the entrance to its band. Let x and z denote the two physical endpoint abscissas, with 0<x<w and 0<z<W. We may descend directly through distance b in the bottom band or descend to its floor and return, with developed travel B=2h−b. Write D=a+d+e+B and measure longitudinal travel t downward in this developed frame.

**A common strip of explicit size.** Use a virtual straight line from transverse coordinate X at t=0 to Z at t=D, where |X|≤w and |Z|<w. At each of the three joints t=a,a+d,a+d+e the line has absolute coordinate at most w−(t/D)(w−|Z|). Thus a signed-coordinate error strictly smaller than a(w−|Z|)/D at every joint is safe. Negative coordinates fold about the common smooth left wall. On either side of a joint the folding law is the same absolute value throughout this strip, including a smooth simultaneous side reflection at zero. This constructs a crossing for every choice of positive lengths. The clearance scales explicitly with the lengths and marks and can tend to zero at their boundary.

**Insert a large slope M.** First suppose the middle travels Md and Me are exact multiples of 2u and 2v. Choose X as the representative in [−w,w] of either +x+Ma or −x+Ma modulo 2w. Give the virtual line the extra slope M, starting its developed abscissa at X−Ma. It launches at the physical source. At the first joint its signed abscissa is the original virtual line's. At each later joint remove the corresponding full middle periods. The two sides of each seam have compatible folded positions and velocities by the strip bound. The terminal developed abscissa after these removals is Z+MB. Hence it suffices to choose |Z|<w with Z+MB ≡ z or −z modulo 2W.

For the floor route there is one further constraint. At the floor the unfolded abscissa, after period removals, is the virtual line's value at t=a+d+e+h, plus Mh. The two choices of X are distinct modulo 2w since the source is interior. Keeping M,Z fixed and switching X changes the floor abscissa by (h−b)ΔX/D. Its absolute value is strictly between zero and W, since |ΔX|≤2w and (h−b)/D<1/2. Both choices cannot be floor corners (multiples of W). Use the regular one. The floor is the full physical bottom side. This is the step that allows the second travel without a new exceptional class of marks.

**Rational test.** Take M=kP, k integral. If the rotation PB/(2W) is irrational, its translates are dense. If it has reduced denominator q>W/w, their spacing 2W/q is smaller than the open interval of width 2w. In both cases one can place Z strictly inside the strip for every target z. For both the direct and the floor construction to fail this test, Pb/(2W) and P(2h−b)/(2W) must both have denominators ≤Q. Their sum is Ph/W. This proves the denominator claim. It does not assert that a small denominator blocks a particular pair.

**Arbitrary real lengths.** Put α=d/(2u), β=e/(2v), γ=B/(2W). If γ is outside the rational span of α,β, there are real M with the middle phases tending to zero modulo 1 and the last phase tending to z/(2W). Indeed, take a rational basis of the span of α,β, divide it by a common denominator so that both are integer combinations of the resulting basis, and append γ. The continuous Kronecker theorem applied to this rationally independent list gives the prescribed approximation. Under (1) at least one of the two choices of γ lies outside that span since their sum is h/W.

For precision about approximate periods, take a sequence of such M. Pass to a subsequence for which Ma modulo 2w and Mh modulo 2W converge. In the limiting phase configuration set Z=0 and take the two source representatives as above, choosing a noncorner floor contact when needed. For the actual sequence choose representatives tending to the selected X, allowing an arbitrarily small overshoot of w at an endpoint of the representative interval. This is harmless because the strictly positive clearance is needed at intermediate joints. Subtract nearest full periods at the two intermediate exits. The resulting signed errors at the joints and the unfolded target tend to zero, as do errors in the floor coordinate modulo 2W. Correct the terminal signed error by adding its negative divided by D to the slope while fixing the developed source. The joint corrections tend to zero. For all sufficiently late terms, the explicit strip margin preserves every seam, the chosen floor point remains regular, and the terminal coordinate is exact modulo 2W. Within an open band all side contacts are on physical vertical walls. This proves (1).

**Named resonant control.** On d17's below-chord staircase the widths are 3,4,9,12 and d=4,e=2,h=3. Take P=18. The room fails (1) and the large-denominator test. Nevertheless for its named S=(1,10), T=(10,1), a=1,b=2, choose M=18, X=1, Z=2. Then Z+Mb=38 folds to 10. The total transverse travel is 163 over a drop of 9. The exact tracer replays direction (163,−9) with 31 vertical-wall reflections, reaching (10,1) without a floor bounce. Thus joint resonance and failure of d17's chord test do not preclude a monotone witness. The remaining task for the whole word includes below-chord rational rooms where both terminal rotations have small denominator in both orientations.
