# Two axial twists: exact phase locking and a primitive BBBB control

**Class-wide result.** In every strictly simple BBBB room with r≤8, a dark pair must fail the explicit two-band congruence below at every axial image of all four lift choices, in both coordinate orientations. The geometry of the actual walls determines the windows. The DB55 computation gives a positive witness even with a nontrivial phase lock. Uniform positivity for every BBBB pair is open.

Independent phase adjustment by words in the horizontal and vertical multitwists fails in DB55, a primitive BBBB room with four reflex corners and 53 cells. Every lift choice of the named pair has a joint orbit of size five inside a product of size 25, yet the physical pair is lit. The lock comes from identical local cylinder rotations, and the resulting equivariant matching does not extend to a commuting affine symmetry of the room double.

## The all-words criterion

Fix H,V as actual affine maps, including their actions on sheets. Use the canonical least positive shears fixing all cylinder rims pointwise in the computations below. Put L=⟨H,V⟩. For a chosen pair s,t let X=L·s and Y=L·t. These finite sets retain the full endpoint labels. Let A,B be the permutation images on X,Y, and let J≤A×B be generated by the two simultaneous permutations. Both projections are surjective. Write A₀={a:(a,1)∈J} and B₀={b:(1,b)∈J}. These are normal, and J identifies A/A₀ and B/B₀ with the same finite quotient D: the B-coordinate of a lift of a is unique modulo B₀, and conversely. Therefore J consists exactly of pairs with the same image in D.

Write P and R for the images in D of the point stabilizers A_s and B_t. For x∈X choose a_x s=x; for y∈Y choose b_y t=y. The possible quotient labels taking s to x are the coset ā_x P, and analogously b̄_y R. For any independently certified visible window I×K, where every point of I sees every point of K, a successful word exists **if and only if**

    (union over x in I∩X of ā_x P) intersects (union over y in K∩Y of b̄_y R).

Indeed a common label allows one to choose a permissible permutation in each coordinate, and the resulting pair belongs to J, so one word realizes both. Its inverse transports the legal segment to the original pair. For four lift choices one takes the disjunction of their four tests. Failure for a specified window family means disjoint label sets for every window and lift choice, including possibly an empty marginal. If a marginal is empty, even completely independent motion could not help.

The joint orbit is the whole product exactly when D=P R: every two such cosets intersect precisely under that condition. For a single twist of periods u,v this recovers the common residue modulo gcd(u,v). For two twists D need not be cyclic; noncommuting derivatives give no independence on the finite labels. This is a condition on the selected twists and windows. If all possible mutually visible neighborhoods are allowed as windows, a legal segment between distinct regular endpoints already persists on such a product neighborhood by straightening in flow boxes. Thus allowing arbitrary visibility witnesses as input largely puts the original illumination question back into the window hypothesis.

## A wall-defined forbidden configuration for every dark BBBB pair

Work with any word sending the chosen lifts to s′,t′. Suppose their heights are h−a and h+b, with a,b positive integers. On the open height range up to h let the source run be (L₀,R₀) bounded by actual vertical walls and of constant width W₀. On the range from h to the target let its run be (L₁,R₁), of width W₁, likewise constant and bounded by vertical walls. Take the open overlap I on the seam through which passage is interior, splitting it at any vertex if necessary. Require that the selected endpoint representatives lie in the horizontal interiors of the runs and are regular, with the endpoint-side limits belonging to their specified lifts. The endpoints may lie at the next height where another band starts. The entire statement also holds with axes exchanged or the path reversed.

In doubled horizontal row coordinates let X_i=x_i on the front sheet, X_i=2L_i−x_i on the back, read modulo P_i=2W_i. Look for a crossing of the seam on the front sheet, at abscissa c. A single path progressing upwards and reflecting only at vertical walls exists through this window **if and only if**

    ((b X₀+a X₁ + g ℤ)/(a+b)) ∩ I ≠ ∅,    g=gcd(b P₀,a P₁).

Indeed a signed horizontal slope p per unit vertical rise must satisfy c≡X₀+a p modulo P₀ and c≡X₁−b p modulo P₁. Eliminating p gives the displayed lattice, and Bézout gives a common p for every point of that lattice. Straightening in the two runs gives legal segments: collisions with their vertical walls occur strictly between seam/end levels, every such contact is smooth, and the common front-sheet slope agrees at the seam. Reflect the entire construction in the physical mirror for the back seam choice. Applying the inverse word gives one legal chain for the original endpoint lifts. This is the two-run placement calculation applied to an actual *joint* twist image; no independently movable phases or previously known visible arcs are assumed.

**Necessary positional bound.** For any physical dark pair, every such configuration at every joint image has

    (a+b) length(I) ≤ gcd(2b W₀, 2a W₁)

and its actual lattice above misses the open interval. When the strict reverse inequality holds, all choices of longitudinal sheet phases pass. At equality one must check position. This statement applies at all areas, to primitive and imprimitive sheets alike. The corresponding single-band case is immediate from a strip chord. It does not assert that the joint orbit must reach two adjacent runs with a wide enough opening.

## DB55: nontrivial lock despite alternating sheet monodromy

Use the two 5×5 chambers [0,5]×[0,5], [8,13]×[0,5] and the corridor [5,8]×[2,3]. The marks are S=(1,1), T=(9,1). D69 records BBBB and r=4. The area 53 is prime, hence its transitive sheet monodromy is primitive; d81 gives G⊇A₅₃. Each parity contains regular sites already in a chamber. [Named cells and marks](reversal.md) · [D69 room classification](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d69-bbbb/README.md) · [D81 Proposition P](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d81-lower-threshold/README.md)

In the horizontal decomposition the doubled row intervals have (circumference, height) (10,2) in the upper/lower chamber bands and (26,1) across the corridor. A pointwise rim-fixing shear λ must satisfy 10|2λ and 26|λ (equivalently the two ratios are integers). The least is 130. In each band the circle shift is λ times height above its lower rim. Every integral-height point is therefore fixed, as are the rims: **H acts identically on all lattice lifts.**

Vertically the chamber cylinders have (10,5), and the corridor has (2,3), so the least rim-fixing shear is 2. Each mark is exactly one unit from its chamber's left rim, so V shifts its doubled vertical circle by two modulo ten, with a consistent cylinder orientation. Each lift has period five. On any of the four starting pairs, every word now acts as (V^j s,V^j t) for the same j modulo five. Hence A=B=D=C₅, P=R=1. A vertical step changes both phases together, and interleaving horizontal twists cannot change their difference. This uses the least rim-fixing twists, not artificially chosen high powers. Their derivatives nevertheless have distinct parabolic fixed directions and generate a non-elementary subgroup.

There is a direct geometric check on the failed overlap. Every marginal image still has x=1 or 9 respectively and physical y in {1,3,5}, by folding the vertical circle. Even if the heights were chosen independently, a direct chord has integer ordinate at x=5, its horizontal midpoint. To pass legally through that mouth it must have ordinate strictly between 2 and 3: the two ends are reflex corners. So **no word exposes a direct chord for any lift choice**. Nevertheless the reviewed right-wall-reversal witness lights S,T. This example refutes compulsory phase independence and compulsory overlap with direct-chord windows. It does not refute overlap with a window certified by a suitable reflected route. [Saved trajectory](reversal.md)

The period-five equivariant matching here is not induced by a global commuting affine symmetry taking S to T. Such a symmetry would have to centralize both shear derivatives projectively. Commuting with the horizontal unipotent fixes its invariant line; commuting with the vertical one fixes the other line, and a diagonal matrix commuting projectively with either shear is scalar. An affine automorphism of this compact surface has determinant of absolute value one, giving derivative ±I. By d91 it then descends to a physical coordinate-sign symmetry about the centroid (13/2,5/2). Such a symmetry sends physical x=1 only to 1 or 12, never 9. Thus the matching records a shared action on two circles that are eight units apart, without requiring an automorphism realizing it globally. This claim concerns an intertwiner commuting with the maps, not an arbitrary affine map that happens to exchange two points. [D91 kernel classification](stabilizer-orbits.md)

## A positive overlap witness with locked phases

For DB55 take the source on the back and target on the front. Use vertical doubled-circle coordinates Y=y in front and Y=−y modulo ten in back. Choose the sign of V to add two at both marks (the inverse is available). V sends the two coordinates −1 and 1 to 1 and 3, so both transformed endpoints are in front, at (1,1) and (9,3). The seam is y=2, the two runs are (0,5) and (0,13), and I=(0,5). Here a=b=1, g=gcd(10,26)=2. Choose c=3. The equations for p are p≡2 modulo 10 and p≡6 modulo 26. The choice p=32 gives total developed displacement (64,2). The lower leg makes six vertical-wall reflections and the upper two, keeping the front-sheet endpoint at the end of each leg. The project's rational wall tracer, run only at this derived displacement, returned the sole intermediate lattice site (3,2), arrived at (9,3), and found no forbidden contact. Small endpoint perturbations preserve the regular itinerary and give a genuinely visible product of arcs containing the common-time V images, as in the d106 certificate. The original pair has equal heights and no direct chord; it cannot itself use such a strictly upward route. Thus this is a nonidentity twist exposing an independently constructed reflected window. [Exact tracer](../../illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/directions-2026-11-09/d8-perturbation-test/tracer.py)

**Whole-room corollary for the existing dumbbell stretches.** Keep the two 5×5 chambers and the unit-height mouth at 2<y<3, with any positive integral corridor length ℓ coprime to five. All same-parity interior lattice pairs, including those with locked joint phases, are lit by a direct chord or by a common power of V followed by the two-band test. This corollary illustrates the class-wide criterion; other room geometries need not have these bands.

All interior lattice marks lie in the chambers. Within one chamber use a direct chord. For opposite chambers, vertical rim-fixing shear 2 works at every length, since 2·5 is divisible by 10 and 2ℓ by 2. At a mark at horizontal offset d∈{1,2,3,4} from its chamber's left side, V adds 2d modulo ten. By height reflection it suffices to treat odd endpoint heights, 1 and 3. If they already differ, no twist is needed. If they coincide at y, choose a nonzero j modulo five allowing the first mark to retain its physical height: dj≡y modulo five does this by starting on its back lift and landing on its front. For the second mark at any offset d′, d′j is nonzero modulo five. It can reach the other height by choosing its initial lift: the four possible shifts ±3±1 modulo ten are all four nonzero even residues. The two choices of initial half are independent, while the exponent j is the same. For even heights 2 and 4 the identical calculation uses ±4±2, again all four nonzero even residues modulo ten.

For heights 1 and 3 the seam at 2 joins a width-five chamber run to the width-(10+ℓ) middle run; overlap width is five and the spacing is gcd(5,10+ℓ)=1. Thus the window works for whichever final sheets the common exponent produced. At heights 2 and 4 use the reflected geometry. This proof uses neither the long-corridor limit nor orbit growth. In particular it applies to unbounded primitive BBBB instances by taking 50+ℓ prime greater than 53; the wall gcds are one, r=4 and the area exceeds both the proper-canonical-factor bound and the corner-only bound. ## Controls and scope

**R22.** The named cell list gives horizontal row intervals of widths 1,2,2,(2 and 2),4,2 from bottom to top; the width-two left run continues across the middle two rows. Vertically the interval lengths by column are 2,4,(2 and 2),2,2,1. Each orientation has a one-unit-thick band of length four, so the rim-fixing shear must be a multiple of eight; eight suffices since every circumference divides eight. Consequently both canonical twists fix *every lattice lift*. Each joint orbit is a singleton. Since the pair is dark, it misses every genuinely visible window. Here D is trivial: darkness does not even require a nontrivial shared phase quotient. D92's smaller-shear affine maps need not fix the rims pointwise. **Allowing more axial generators.** In the reviewed odd cross, even the group generated by the *full* horizontal and vertical stabilizers preserves each coordinate-sign orbit on lattice points. For (6,0),(0,6), all independent images have chord midpoint (±3,±3) outside the cross. Thus adopting another convention for the axial generators cannot give a universal direct-visibility theorem either. [D91 axial result](axial-stabilizers.md)

**What survives.** The common-quotient condition characterizes synchronized transport to specified windows. Neither BBBB, the d68 star count, nor alternating sheet monodromy prevents that lock, and the tests distinguish two independent failures: a target window missing already from a marginal, and incompatible labels for two nonempty marginals. The new core is not a symmetry-classification problem. A two-twist illumination proof would have to supply a geometrically established window with a common label for at least one lift choice **even in a locked action**, or light that pair by another path. The DB55 witness above establishes common-label overlap in a reflected window despite the lock. The remaining gap is whether arbitrary BBBB layouts force such an overlap in some wall itinerary; the two-band proof only establishes it when its geometric hypotheses are met. No room, ray, direction or reflection-word discovery search was used.
