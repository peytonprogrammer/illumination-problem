Advances: L1 — In the natural branch, primitive straight-arc averaging on the unordered-pair cover fixes the total number of successful sheet pairs but gives no positive lower bound at specified marked starts; a lattice determinant gives a sufficient scale for converting a near visit into a clean hit.

# What averaging on Y actually counts

Let Y be the unordered-pair cover of a primitive natural-action room of degree n. Its generic fiber has N=n(n−1)/2 unordered pairs. All projected room corners, ordinary forbidden points on Y, and swap poles lie over lattice vertices in the developed unit grid. Fix source site a. At a regular physical interior source and target of that parity, select incident sheet germs, one from each position, with the compatible launch half-plane. In a common arc marking these give a small specified set M of unordered pairs, not a uniform sample of the fiber. The statements below can be applied separately to each compatible marking.

**Counting lemma.** For a fixed simple primitive segment from a to a different site b on the pillowcase, exactly q_b of the N initial unordered sheet labels end at a swap pole. Their interiors are clean. In the arc-adapted labeling these successes form a *matching* on the n individual sheets. Indeed parallel transport in the open arc identifies the sheet labels bijectively. The two labels must be the two entries of the same transposition of local monodromy at b. Pulling those transpositions back gives q_b disjoint edges. The primitive segment has no lattice site in its open interior, so neither projection can meet a physical corner. The label count includes all launch germs at a, even ones unavailable to the marked pair. A hit from M gives a legal shot by midpoint reversal. Longer clean paths may visit intermediate lattice sites, so this is a sufficient first-step test.

For any probability distribution on primitive arcs in one parity class, express their pulled-back matchings in common source labels (or choose the compatible identification for each launch sector). The edge-hit probabilities sum to q_b. For a uniform unordered label the probability is q_b/N≤1/(n−1). The marked success requires mass on the *specified* edges. Even with a perfect matching, two disjoint four-letter sets can have zero cross edges, by pairing within each set. Averages of such matchings preserve the zero. This example is about the marginal constraint, not a claim that these matchings are all realizable as straight arcs on a given Y. Natural monodromy permits arbitrary transport along general paths; to apply it here one would need a distribution theorem for simple primitive arcs with the endpoint labels held fixed. The mass identity alone gives no such theorem. In DB55, each site gives 24/1378 for a uniformly chosen pair of labels, despite the global pole count of 96 swap and 4 false poles. Thus the pole-majority ratio uses the wrong denominator for a marked launch.

**Continuous flow has a second limitation.** From a marked lattice start, every exact visit to any pole or projected corner has rational developed direction. The union of these directions has angular measure zero for each target, even a directly visible one. Equidistribution of flow in sets of positive area can instead give visits to neighborhoods. The following lemma quantifies the additional information needed for straightening one.

Develop a clean straight segment from a compatible germ at the origin to y. Suppose its endpoint is within ε<1/2 of a particular swap pole in the same local cone chart, and develop the short closing radius to that pole, ending at v∈ℤ². If v is primitive and |v|ε<1, then the straight segment to v is a clean shot to that same swap pole. Indeed, for every point u of the triangle with vertices 0,y,v, |det(v,u)|≤|det(v,y−v)|<1. An integral point of the triangle must therefore be on the line through 0 and v. The primitive chord has no interior lattice point; the triangle intersects that line only in the chord in the nondegenerate case. The triangle minus its endpoint vertices has no branch value or ordinary forbidden vertex. Deform the broken path across it while retaining the launch sector at 0 and terminal sector at v. Covering-path lifting gives the same terminal pole, and both projections of the straightened open chord are clean. In the collinear case a clean segment that has already passed through the pole can be truncated at its first hit; otherwise continue the short closing radius. This proof works on any such pair cover, without an ergodicity hypothesis.

For a hidden marked pair, every such near approach to a swap pole must therefore violate the reciprocal-length bound, have nonprimitive developed address, or fail the specified clean approach and local closing hypotheses. At nonprimitive addresses the chord contains intermediate grid sites whose actual sheet labels can permit or forbid continuation. Ordinary flow equidistribution neither enforces the reciprocal scale nor specifies the lift at primitive approximants. This identifies a missing dynamical statement; it does not disprove the possibility that stronger marked shrinking-target estimates could give one. A theorem for almost every surface would also require a separate argument for these fixed arithmetic surfaces. [Marchese on Diophantine approximation for translation surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.5887)

**Source check on the approximation step.** Marchese Theorem 1.1(2) supplies prescribed endpoint bundles for almost every framed surface in the stratum. It cannot specialize to our fixed arithmetic surface. Theorem 1.6 applies to every flat torus with punctures, but guarantees some endpoint pairs; it does not specify the pair, and passing to our branched cover adds sheet labels. Neither theorem supplies the marked return above. [Theorems 1.1 and 1.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1003.5887)

**Area cutoff.** No N₀ follows. The counting obstruction persists as q_b/N tends to zero with growing n. A sufficient next statement is that a compatible launch admits a clean reciprocal-scale near return with primitive address *to a swap pole*, or a clean hit with controlled intermediate labels. The first open L1 case remains the marked primitive natural-action BBBB branch beyond the area-20 census. No room, ray, direction or reflection-word search was performed.
