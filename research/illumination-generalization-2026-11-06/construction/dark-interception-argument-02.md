# DI02: a two-period cone turn forces a different mediant cone

## Exact conditional necessity

Let P be an original finite strictly simple orthogonal integral room, with distinct actual interior even-even S,T. Let M be its connected four-copy reflection unfolding, X,Y the COMPLETE actual endpoint fibers, and Z its genuine cone set. Assume the original pair is dark. The accepted cone-only full-fiber equivalence then says there is NO finite Z-avoiding straight segment between ANY x in X and ANY y in Y, even if such a segment passes through regular physical-corner lifts.

Fix primitive u,v in Z^2 with det(u,v)=+/-1. Suppose there are actual x in X, y in Y and z in Z with:

1. The straight u segment from x first encounters Z at time2, at z.
2. The straight -v segment from y first encounters Z at time2, at the SAME surface cone z. Equivalently a specified outgoing v prong at z reaches y after time2 with no genuine cone in between.
3. At z these two actual germs bound the NARROW sector of angle strictly between0 andpi corresponding to the oriented developed triangle Delta=conv{0,2u,2u+2v}. This is the triangle-adjacent slot, not either of the alternate long sectors in the6pi cone.

Then the primitive mediant w=u+v from x has its FIRST genuine-cone encounter at time1. Its cone z' projects to a DIFFERENT original physical reflex corner from the corner underlying z. In particular these hypotheses plus darkness imply at least TWO DISTINCT original reflex corners.

This is not an assertion that conditions1--3 are available in every dark room. It is not an r>=9 theorem, a classification, or a direction/reflection cutoff. All other positive integer return weights remain uncontrolled.

## 1. The developed triangle has only boundary branch values

Apply the unimodular linear coordinate change taking u,v to e1,e2. The triangle becomes conv{(0,0),(2,0),(2,2)}. Its integer points are exactly

    (0,0), (1,0), (2,0), (2,1), (2,2), (1,1).

There are no interior integer points. Transforming back, the only nonvertex lattice points of Delta are the side midpoints

    u, 2u+v, u+v.

The natural unit torus cover is branched only over integer points. Thus the OPEN developed triangle is unbranched. This is a geometric statement about an entire fundamental triangle, valid for arbitrarily large determinant-one u,v; no directional size bound is used.

## 2. Actual triangle sheet and exact target completion

Select the lift of the open triangle adjoining the actual initial u germ from regular x. Its first side follows the specified x-to-z segment. The intermediate lattice point u on that side lifts to a REGULAR point: otherwise assumption1 would have a genuine cone earlier than time2. A regular physical convex-corner mark is allowed geometrically at that midpoint; we are working in the justified zero-only convention.

The triangle sheet extends to z on its first side. Condition3 specifies exactly its narrow cone wedge adjacent to the second side. Thus its continuation along the second side is the PARTICULAR outgoing v prong specified in condition2, and not a free choice of a cone continuation. The midpoint 2u+v on that side also lifts to a regular point by condition2. The second-side endpoint is consequently the actual y, with its original sheet identity.

Extensions of this sheet to boundary wedges are justified by finite branched-cover completion: away from the listed lattice points the cover is ordinary; approaching a listed vertex within a fixed triangle wedge is Cauchy in its flat metric and has a unique limit. At regular intermediate points the two adjacent boundary germs coincide in a single local chart. At z, the fixed narrow wedge supplies the required prong. At regular x and y the sheet completion is unique. No bent trajectory is called an optical path.

Consider now the third side, from0 to2u+2v, with the straight velocity w=u+v and total time2. Its only possible intermediate cone location is its lattice midpoint u+v at time1. The initial lifted half-side from x is an actual straight segment whose open part projects away from0 and so has no genuine cone before time1.

If this midpoint lifted to a REGULAR point, the full third side would continue regularly through it and end at the SAME y: the selected open triangle sheet and its regular completions at the third-side midpoint and final vertex identify the entire side. There are no other lattice points to cause an unrecorded singular event. We would have a Z-avoiding straight x-to-y segment. The accepted cone-only full-fiber transfer would then produce a finite legal original S-to-T billiard path. It may change endpoint lifts when deleting removable-corner retracing; retaining the complete actual fibers is essential. This contradicts the assumed actual darkness.

Therefore the midpoint is a GENUINE cone z'. It is reached by the actual mediant germ from x at time1 and is its FIRST genuine cone, since the preceding open segment avoids the torus branch value. No continuation through z' is declared legal. The triangle boundary beyond z' is used only to identify the alternative regular-midpoint case in the contradiction.

## 3. The two cones are different physical corners

In an orthogonal integral room, every developed physical reflection is a diagonal sign change plus an EVEN integral translation. Thus every developed image of a physical corner has that corner's coordinate parity, independent of repeated developments or source chart. The actual even-even source has developed parity(0,0).

The cone z at developed displacement2u consequently lies above an even-even physical corner. The mediant cone z' at displacement u+v lies above a corner of parity(u+v) mod2. Since u,v form a basis over F2, u+v is nonzero modulo2. Thus z and z' cannot project to the same physical corner. This is an explicit distinctness proof for THIS pair of events, not an assumed injective charge over many directions.

Every genuine cone in the connected four-copy orthogonal unfolding corresponds to a physical reflex corner of angle3pi/2 and has cone angle6pi. Hence two different physical corner labels here imply r>=2 under the stated hypotheses. Regular convex physical corners do not supply the forced z'. No claim that each further Farey step supplies a new corner follows.

## 4. Scope/calibration

- Known dark Q0 has no even-even reflex corner. Thus conditions1--2 with a common physical cone at even/even times cannot hold there. DI02 does not reprove Q0 darkness or explain all its cones.
- Q100 is actually dark and HAS even-even reflex corners. DI01 explicitly verifies its first-cone time2 from S100 in direction(50,-1), so the even-time issue is real. No matching reverse-target neighbor leg or triangle-adjacent prong is asserted for that control without proof. Determinant-one adjacency in a fixed unit lattice is not transported unchanged by diag(100,1).
- The prescribed six-wall lit L control of DI01 has x=(0,0), z=(2,2), y=(0,2), u=(1,1), v=(-1,0), both first times2 and the narrow compatible joint. Its mediant is w=(0,1), with regular midpoint(0,1), and the full straight side reaches actual T legally. This is the exact positive branch of the proof and confirms that DARKNESS is doing work when it forces z'. It is not a counterexample to one-way necessity.
- The six-wall intrinsic x,rho(x) obstruction concerns the same physical point; the distinct S,T premise and accepted complete-fiber cone-only equivalence cannot be dropped. The abstract Heisenberg example lacks the required disk quotient and actual X,Y; no physical corner count is imported from it.
- Weighted-return/Farey word identities do not supply conditions1--3. This proof uses one actual triangle sheet and a single straight mediator, preserving endpoint sheets before invoking full-fiber purification. It neither concatenates directions as a ray nor suppresses extra lattice events of larger triangles.

## Decision and remaining implication

DI02 is a new CONDITIONAL first-cone necessity: in the first physically possible even/even weight case, a compatible corner turn under actual darkness forces a mediant first cone of a different physical parity, hence a second distinct reflex corner. The two-corner lower bound is conditional on the supplied actual chain and slot; its GLOBAL availability is open.

For arbitrary even times a,b, the larger developed triangle may have extra lattice points in its interior and on its sides. Its sheet can contain further genuine cones and repeated images of the same physical corner. No reduction of those data to2, no well-founded Farey induction and no nine-distinct-corner charge has been proved. The full r<=8 theorem remains open.

Method: hand proof with one prescribed lit geometric calibration and existing dark controls; no discovery, new engine, census, coefficient scan, worker or search clock.
