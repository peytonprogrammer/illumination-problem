## What is missing

We need an argument handling arbitrary k. The old identities W=2+6I+Q-2J-s_N and k=J+s_N do not themselves bound k in terms of W. Dilation alone invalidates such a bound. Reducing a hypothetical dark room requires a darkness-preserving map or a direct construction of an actual path in the original room. Mirror additions/deletions are not monotone for illumination. No density or rounding argument is licensed.

The accepted three-band outer-endpoint theorem remains available under its exact geometric hypotheses; it is not a general normal form for <=21-wall polygons.
