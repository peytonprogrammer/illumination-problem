# AV01: shortest-chain global availability candidate

Candidate to prove or refute, published before checks. Let P be a finite strictly simple integral orthogonal disk, S,T distinct actual interior even-even points, M its compact four-copy reflection unfolding, Gamma the physical reflection group, X and Y the complete endpoint fibers, Z its genuine cones. Assume every X-to-Y straight geodesic avoiding Z is absent.

Take a shortest metric path among all pairs X times Y, retaining the exact lifts, cone prongs and lengths. Proposed availability step: this minimization supplies a single cone joint with original-endpoint Z-free arms bounding the strictly narrow angle (less than pi) of a nondegenerate developed triangle, allowing the prior interception implications to apply.

AV01 is W-free; it does not claim use of r<=8. A refutation of AV01 is not a refutation of arbitrary joint availability or the goal theorem. No unit-weight, primitive-direction, finite-state, or straightening assumption is added.

Success criterion: prove the minimizer supplies the joint with all guards, or rigorously prove its sector geometry forbids the proposed selection, with an actual dark calibration. Strongest objection: at a metric geodesic through a cone, both adjacent cone angles are at least pi, so no strictly narrow triangle sector exists. Further issue: a shortest chain may have several cones, and then first/last arms need not meet at one cone.

Hand proof and prescribed control only; no discovery computation or search clock started.
