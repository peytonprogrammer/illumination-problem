# ET01 candidate: one remote-flat-wall transfer of a reflex-degenerate chord

Published before computational checks. HAND local proof first; no search/census/worker/engine.

Let D be an original strictly simple orthogonal lattice room with actual reflex corner C=(A,H), whose missing local quadrant is NE. Suppose the actual points are

    S=(A+a,H-b), T=(A-c,H+d),

with a,b,c,d>0 and ad=bc. The degenerate straight S-C-T segment is thus collinear, with positive incoming/outgoing time, and both endpoint germs lie in the two allowed opposite arms. S,T are the ORIGINAL distinct interior even-even points, not arbitrary unfolded substitutes.

Assume a full physical L-corridor is available: there are R>A>0 and U>H>0 such that

    Q=([0,R]x[0,H]) union ([0,A]x[0,U])

is contained in D, int(Q) is contained in int(D), S is strictly in the lower/right rectangle with0<a<R-A and0<b<H, and T is strictly in the upper/left rectangle with0<c<A and0<d<U-H. The intended floor contact on y0 must lie in the relative interior of an ACTUAL horizontal wall of D; the floor is not declared a new reflector. It is sufficient to require the full open floor(0,R)x{0} is an actual smooth wall segment. C remains an actual reflex corner of D.

Proposed local physical transfer: a legal actual S-to-T path with ONE floor reflection exists in Q, replacing the singular straight passage at C. It can use a different initial direction. Construct it by reflecting actual T across y0, then derive exact floor contact and strict intersection of its outgoing leg with yH. Prove positive times, common signed direction, no corner/earlier-wall hit and actual target first attainment for the complete path. The collinearity ad=bc must enter the strict guard proof; this is not an arbitrary two-image construction.

Distinction from A26: the old conditional theorem assumes an opposite-sector remote CONVEX CORNER return and sufficiently small endpoint coalescence, then splits two contacts. ET01 instead assumes a FULL rectangular corridor to a smooth FLAT wall, addresses finite fixed endpoints with exact ad=bc, and aims for one smooth reflection. Neither corridor existence nor its universality is assumed.

Only AFTER settling this local statement, test the tentative availability claim A8: every actual straight reflex-degenerate pair in an original r<=8 room admits this full-corridor geometry, or its x/y-swapped version at the same corner. A8 is a conjectural test, not a premise of ET01 or an established low-reflex fact. If A8 fails, do not infer arbitrary endpoint transfer or the whole theorem false. No global transition/rank or nine-DISTINCT-corner claim is licensed without proved edges and availability.

Calibration: accepted r2/r4 arithmetic roots are smooth, so they need no endpoint repair. Their excluded guard endpoints are generally not matching roots and must not be relabelled corner-degenerate actual S/T connections. Test r9 dark control by checking whether the stated full L-corridor hypothesis could actually hold at its direct blocked pair; never infer a new low-r bound from that example.
