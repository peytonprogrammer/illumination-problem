## 2. The proposed conservation-to-exact-coverage implication is FALSE

For a finite labelled branch-window family with period p=2w, set

    C(theta)=sum_j #{n: theta+np is STRICTLY inside V_j}.

For each phase theta, let L(theta) and R(theta) count ALL lower and upper endpoints congruent to theta modulo p, including repeated developed images and winding endpoints. A direct one-sided count gives

    C(theta+)-C(theta-)=L(theta)-R(theta),
    C(theta)=C(theta-)-R(theta)=C(theta+)-L(theta).

This also holds when a long interval's two ends have the same residue: its interior winding multiplicity remains, but its excluded endpoint still creates a point deficit. No endpoints are replaced by closed intervals.

Suppose the endpoint currents cancel pointwise: L=R at every phase. Then C has a constant integer value M on the open nonendpoint arcs. Its average satisfies

    M = sum_j length(V_j)/p.

But at an endpoint phase it is

    C(theta)=M-R(theta).

Therefore the missing sharp condition for the ACTUAL phase is M>R(0), and uniform coverage needs M>max_theta R(theta). Neither follows from current cancellation or positive mass.

Example: V1=(0,w), V2=(w,2w). Endpoint currents cancel at both residues, total normalized length is1, and C=1 elsewhere. Yet C(0)=C(w)=0. Repeating both labelled intervals N times preserves exact endpoint cancellation and gives arbitrarily large M=N, but R(0)=N and C(0)=0. All copies and signs are retained. The failure is not caused by forgetting multiplicities.

These are ABSTRACT window counterexamples, not claimed realizable actual-room branches. They rigorously disprove the general analytic inference proposed as the first route to C8.
