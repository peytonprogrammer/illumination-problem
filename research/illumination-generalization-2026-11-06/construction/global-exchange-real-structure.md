# B05: a finite labelled orbit need not retain the full transported real structure

This is a hand obstruction to one averaging premise, NOT a refutation of label-only finite orbit methods, the specialist lower-bound conjecture, or all possible potentials. Use the existing W4 square[0,24]^2 control and its48-by48 unfolding torus with the complete actual source/target fibers.

Let R=diag(1,-1) be its physical horizontal reflection. A shear A_n=[[1,n],[0,1]] acts on the ENTIRE surface/marks, and transports the reflection to

    R_n=A_n R A_n^-1=[[1,-2n],[0,-1]].

All these involutions have different derivatives. A translation isomorphism between labelled origamis has derivativeI and cannot conjugate different derivative matrices. Consequently the orbit enriched with the FULL transported physical reflection is infinite even though the underlying finite labelled origami orbit is finite.

Concretely the unit horizontal permutation h on this torus has order48. Under the anchored move T^48, v becomes v h^-48=v, while h and every vertex label remain unchanged. Hence the finite labelled triple returns EXACTLY. The transported real reflection, however, has matrix[[1,-96],[0,-1]], rather than R. Repeating the same loop gives[[1,-96k],[0,-1]] for every integer k. No census, new room or branch suppression is involved.

The intrinsic HALF-TURN rho is different: derivative-I commutes with shears and does not produce this obstruction. A proof using only rho and label data may descend to the finite orbit, but that has less information than the full physical real structure requested in section2. A potential using the actual additional reflection cannot silently be treated as a single-valued function of(h,v,labels).

There are two legitimate ways forward: prove the proposed potential is invariant under every closed labelled-orbit shear word's action on the extra real structure, so that it descends; or supply an independently justified averaging scheme on the enriched orbit that handles its infinitude. Choosing a new convenient reflection after each move discards the transported physical data and requires its own actual-endpoint/path justification.

This obstruction leaves H(h,v,labels) and any genuinely label-only finite-orbit averaging valid. In particular it does not alter the saved known22 certificate or the arc-chain criterion. It identifies a precise additional burden for a structure-sensitive charge, rather than a smaller dark room or a universal impossibility result.

A finite permutation action of the reflection on selected vertices is weaker than this FULL affine involution. In this torus control, the extra term -96y is itself zero modulo48 for integer y, so the entire integer-vertex permutation can agree while the derivative differs. A vertex-label-only potential could therefore descend; the obstruction does not rule that out. Identifying by an arbitrary affine self-map instead of translation equivalence also changes the horizontal direction used by H and requires an equivariance proof before orbit averaging.
