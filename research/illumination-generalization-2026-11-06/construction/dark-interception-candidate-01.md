# DI01 candidate: cone-slot Farey interception capacity

Predeclared HAND implication before discovery; no computational discovery is planned.

Let M be the connected four-copy orthogonal unfolding of a strictly simple integral room, X,Y its complete distinct actual interior even-even fibers, and suppose EVERY X-times-Y pair lacks a legal connection. Genuine reflex cones have angle6pi and three prongs in any fixed direction. Use the unit torus cover with every corner and endpoint over0.

For primitive u,v with det(u,v)=+/-1 and one cone z, define I_u(z) as the incoming u prongs reached from X in exactly one primitive-vector unit of time, and J_v(z) as the outgoing v prongs reaching Y in one unit. These legs have no earlier cone because their torus interiors avoid0. Each arrival prong specifies one geometrically adjacent outgoing v prong bounding the developed triangle(0,u,u+v). Propose that this gives a bijection b_z on the three slots, and darkness forces

    b_z(I_u(z)) intersect J_v(z) = empty,
    |I_u(z)|+|J_v(z)| <=3.

The exact lift and endpoint identity must be proved even at the cone; a bent two-leg path is not itself an optical path. This is a proposed necessary capacity relation, not a charge bound or an assertion that any I,J must coexist.

Discriminating test: does this inequality have any force in the actual even-even orthogonal class, or is one-period common-cone occupancy already forbidden by physical coordinate parity? Retain arbitrary positive first-cone times a,b separately: test whether a common PHYSICAL corner for Farey neighbors forces a,b even, and hence lies outside this one-period triangle. Do not replace all first times by1 or infer nine distinct corners.

Calibration: Q0 and Q100 are already-dark distinct physical controls; six-wall intrinsic-pair control is not off-orbit darkness. A prescribed lit low-reflex example may show that even-duration sharing is geometrically possible, but cannot refute a darkness-necessary statement. No new census/engine/worker/clock.
