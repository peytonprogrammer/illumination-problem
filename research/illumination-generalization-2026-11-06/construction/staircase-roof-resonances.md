# Two explicit exceptional equations for the weaker roof chart

Candidate exact pairwise refinement for independent review. This does not settle either equation or the full staircase. It is a further deduction from the frozen partial-overlap roof construction, not another numerical search.

Use its notation T0=d-ys, h=H-d, Ht=yt-d, v=2h-Ht. Suppose only v>=T0, rather than v>=2T0. The overlap length ell>a makes the final target-image interval longer than2a. It contains two consecutive distinct actual images Y1,Y2 in {+xt+2ma,-xt+2ma}, with gap either2xt or2(a-xt), strictly between0 and2a.

At the roof the coordinate B(Y) has slope beta=(T0+h)/(T0+v). It is strictly less than1 because v>h. It is strictly greater than1/2 because 2(T0+h)-(T0+v)=T0+Ht>0. If BOTH candidate paths hit genuine roof corners, both roof coordinates belong to aZ and their positive difference is less than2a. Hence their difference is exactly a. Thus either

    2beta*xt=a, or 2beta*(a-xt)=a.

Writing r=(T0+Ht)/(2(T0+h)), these conditions are exactly

    xt/a=r OR xt/a=1-r,
    r=(yt-ys)/(2(H-ys)), 0<r<1/2.             (R)

Therefore, under v>=T0, every actual cross-pair NOT on(R) has a legal connection by the two-image roof chart. Every other actual-corner and seam argument is unchanged from the frozen roof proof, including Ht=0 and actual target truncation. Equations(R) are necessary for this particular two-candidate failure, not sufficient for physical darkness or even for failure of another choice of n,K.

If H>=2d then h>=d>T0, so v>h>T0 for EVERY cross-placement. This proves all cross-pairs except(R), and the earlier orientation-avoiding L cases already cover all other placements. This is a genuine weaker-geometry pairwise statement; it is NOT a whole-room theorem at H>=2d.

## A specific new repair question, still open

For a pair with Ht>=T0, the roof-free partial-overlap chart already connects it. Consider the remaining Ht<T0 with H>=2d. Reflecting the source once in the genuine floor changes T0 to T0'=d+ys. One still has

    v=2h-Ht > 2d-(d-ys)=d+ys=T0'.

The corresponding two-image roof resonance is

    xt/a=r' OR xt/a=1-r',
    r'=(yt+ys)/(2(H+ys)).                     (R')

It is DISJOINT from(R), since

    r'-r=ys*(H-yt)/(H²-ys²)>0,
    0<r<r'<1/2.

This means the reflected-source roof chart cannot have both candidate paths hit roof corners when the original chart is resonant. However it has acquired a genuine floor contact. At most one of its two candidates hits a floor corner, since the floor-coordinate separation is ys*(Y2-Y1)/(T0'+v)<a<A. The current argument DOES NOT exclude the split failure: candidate1 hits a floor corner while candidate2 hits a roof corner. Merely knowing that each obstruction affects at most one candidate is insufficient to guarantee a good candidate.

The next bounded proof question is to repair or disprove that split failure, retaining actual images, both portals, and all true corner guards. A third candidate is not automatically available: the proven interval length is only>2a. A change of n/K or another explicit chart needs a proof, not genericity or an unchecked claim that the two obstructions are independent. No discovery or worker is running.

If a repair were proved, it would establish a stronger staircase subclass H>=2d (and its transpose), not the mainW21 objective by itself. No full staircase, nonarithmetic or universal minimum claim follows here.
