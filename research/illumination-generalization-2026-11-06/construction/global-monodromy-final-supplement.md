## 4. All-primitive group compatibility: exact stopping point

The accepted direction-skeleton theorem already gives the same-direction first-return equivalence for EACH primitive integer vector. Packaging all these maps as a group-generated graph adds no valid concatenation rule. R60's independently reviewed27-square Heisenberg surface makes this failure concrete: r,u generate a transitive cover group, yet every regular x is genuinely zero-only dark from a central order3 translate. Primitive straight loops have nonzero mod3 abelianization; their powers never give that central translation. Thus transitivity of the full monodromy generators cannot force a one-direction connection.

This abstract control has NO canonical45 reflection quotient: the explicit primitive torus map has9 distinct zero images, whereas a physical intrinsic-I involution must fix every zero and forces corner images into a translated four-point half-period set. See global-monodromy-physical-obstruction.md. That exclusion is an application of the already accepted torus-cover bridge, not a new darkness-derived constraint. It explains precisely why the physical hypothesis cannot be dropped, but supplies no proof that physical hypotheses FORCE an X-Y direction.

The missing step is not a finite or computational detail. One needs a theorem linking simultaneously realizable primitive return maps of the SAME physical reflection quotient, its full original endpoint fibers and its maximal-wall budget. Neither the unbounded permutation orders/return weights, the few half-period branch values, nor geometric graph transitivity currently supplies that implication. No bounded primitive-word inventory or unrestricted graph connectivity can substitute for it.
