# Assessment of the proposed folding-necessity route

The accepted shared-seam W>=22 theorem has a precise, narrower scope: whole congruent copies of an ORTHOGONAL polyomino parity base, pairwise disjoint interiors, a strictly simple disk union, pointwise agreeing inverse-copy maps, and interior preimages of two distinct smooth boundary marks. It is a geometric cost theorem conditional on this decomposition. It never assumes darkness, and proves no necessity of the decomposition.

A candidate necessity theorem must retain every one of those features or supply a replacement wall bound. In particular it must distinguish a nontrivial endpoint-preserving parity factor from the automatic folding of a canonical triangulation onto one fundamental triangle. The latter sends both A marks to the same base vertex and supplies no dark-pair proof.

The universal intrinsic half-turn result is also insufficient: its dark graph x,rho(x) exists in EVERY canonical45 unfolding but projects to the SAME physical point. Local midpoint retracing therefore cannot by itself distinguish an illuminated room from an actual dark pair. One must use two disjoint full physical endpoint fibers, not just one selected lift pair or a local corner condition. This is an already accepted limitation, not a new no-go theorem for every possible converse.

The hand frame lemma in fold-necessity-beveled-candidate.md supplies one exact necessary condition for the narrow class: connected pointwise whole-copy folds onto an orthogonal base force a single global orthogonal wall frame. The same document proposes a43-wall genuinely octilinear dark room, made by beveling a convex corner of a nine-square parity base before the standard three-copy gluing. Its new225-degree vertices remain genuine corners, and the four smooth seam endpoints still fold to90-degree reversals. If independently accepted, it disproves the narrow orthogonal converse at unrestricted wall count. It does NOT disprove a W<=21-only converse or a converse admitting octilinear parity bases; indeed it retains such a broader fold.

Recommended next hand question: state explicitly whether the desired factor base MUST be orthogonal. If yes, a low-wall necessity result must first exclude actual dark pairs in genuinely octilinear rooms; the proposed43-wall control prevents an unrestricted symmetry argument. If no, first establish the wall-count bound for the enlarged base/angle class. The orthogonal m(w-4)+4+2t3 formula cannot be transferred without its angle-count hypotheses.

No new local-to-global necessity proof has been obtained. Two attempted shortcuts are retired: treating the known seam rigidity as an existence theorem for seams, and treating universal half-turn surface darkness as an off-orbit physical dark pair. Both reverse an accepted implication or discard required endpoint fibers. No new room/ray/direction/word/cylinder/orbit scan or fixed-room computation was performed.
