# OR01: guarded shooting through a rectangular ear

Status: candidate published before checking. This is a sufficient actual-room routing lemma; global low-reflex availability is a separate proposed implication, not assumed or proved.

Let D be a finite strictly simple orthogonal room. In a Euclidean coordinate frame let E=[0,w]x[0,h], w,h>0, be an attached rectangular chamber: its open interior is in D, its top and two vertical sides are physical boundary, its top corners are physical convex right-angle corners, and M=(0,w)x{0} is an interior section. Neither actual interior endpoint S,T is in E. Side/mouth endpoints must be treated using the actual D boundary, never as new mirrors.

Choose a nonempty bounded connected open slope interval J=(l,u), constants alpha,beta in R and a,b>0, and epsilon in {+1,-1}. Require for EVERY m in J:

1. A specified finite source reflection branch from actual S first reaches M at x_S(m)=alpha+a*m, with velocity proportional to (m,1).
2. A specified finite reversed branch from actual T first reaches M at x_T(m)=beta-epsilon*b*m, with velocity proportional to (-epsilon*m,1). Thus its forward reversal leaves M at velocity (epsilon*m,-1) and reaches actual T.
3. Both abscissae lie strictly in (0,w); both branches stay outside E before their terminal section crossing, have positive flights, obey the physical specular law, and avoid every true polygon corner. All sheet/endpoint data belong to the actual specified S,T. The branch guard domain is the WHOLE J, not disconnected pieces with a sign comparison across a gap.

Proposed sufficient condition:

    (a+b+2h)*(u-l) > 2w.                         (C)

Claim: D illuminates actual S,T. The proposed construction chooses m and an integer winding n together, rather than retuning a selected reduced-room ray at its old mouth point. It permits all legal horizontal-side reflections within E. A top convex-corner contact is handled by the existing retracing-removal mechanism; a mouth endpoint or reflex-corner contact is not permitted.

Required positive argument: the exact signed exit relation must produce a matching m in J under (C), followed by an actual endpoint path with corner guards. It is insufficient to present the shooting equation alone.

Global candidate G8: every original target room r<=8 that is not already directly lit admits SOME such actual chamber and actual branch data satisfying (C), possibly after switching source and target or rotating the coordinate frame. This is an unproved geometric/dynamic hypothesis. No inference from r<=8 to (C) is currently available. A failed prescribed chart only rejects that chart; it does not refute G8 or the illumination theorem. A proof of the local statement alone will not complete the goal.

Bounded success criterion: prove the local positive implication with complete guards, then either derive a noncircular G8 argument or identify the precise quantitative information missing. Strongest objection: first-hit guard intervals can shrink as reflection words lengthen; large a or b alone does not force (C). Irrational-direction recurrence does not imply the required interval width.

Method: hand proof and exact prescribed algebra checks only. No discovery search, worker, census, or renewed search clock.
