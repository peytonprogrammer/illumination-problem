# OR02: one reflex corner supports infinitely many first-hit guard boundaries

HAND obstruction to a proposed global charge, not an illumination counterexample.

D=([0,8]x[0,4]) union ([0,4]x[4,8]) is a strictly simple orthogonal lattice L-room, with its sole reflex corner C=(4,4). Use actual interior even-even S=(6,2), T=(2,6); T is irrelevant to the source-boundary calculation. M=(0,4)x{4} is the upper-arm mouth. The original room has r=1.

For each integer n>=1, choose upward physical velocity proportional to (1,m_n), where

    m_n=2/(16n-2).

Unfold only side reflections of the lower rectangle. The developed ray from (6,2) reaches C_n=(4+16n,4), a reflected image of the SAME physical reflex corner. Before that point y lies strictly in (2,4), so it cannot touch the floor or top, and every physical side collision is at x=0 or x=8 with 2<y<4. They are smooth. Its positive horizontal travel is16n-2. It crosses exactly the supports x=8,16,...,16n, giving2n physical side reflections. After an even number, x=4+16n folds to actual x=4.

Hence C is its FIRST top-wall/mouth encounter and its first actual corner. Each m_n is distinct, and m_n decreases to0. In a sufficiently small neighborhood of a fixed m_n the same2n side reflections persist. The first top encounter in unfolded coordinates is x=6+2/m. Its derivative -2/m^2 is nonzero, and folding at C_n has derivative+1. On one side of m_n its physical x is below4, so it enters M; on the other its x exceeds4, so it reflects at the top wall of the lower rectangle. Thus every m_n is a genuine boundary of a first-mouth access branch.

This proves that the number of source-to-mouth guard endpoints cannot be bounded by an injective assignment to the r physical reflex corners. Even r=1 permits infinitely many distinct reflection words and slope boundaries associated with ONE corner, accumulating at horizontal direction. The union of branch intervals is not a finite partition across ALL words.

It does NOT refute a signed/canceling charge, a weighted interval-length estimate, a positive G8 selection theorem, or r<=8 illumination. It also does not extend a fixed finite-word component claim to infinite words. The room and endpoints may already have a legal connection; this calculation does not claim darkness or search for a ray.

Relevance: OR01's missing global width condition cannot be justified by counting each guard endpoint as a new physical reflex vertex. A new global argument must control repeated images/words quantitatively or cancel their contributions. This is a precise obstruction to that attempted next step, without rerunning a room/ray census.
