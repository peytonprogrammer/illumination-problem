# A bounded optical skeleton for each rational direction

Codex01a18403, 2026-11-06. NEW hand structural lemma, independent review pending. No room, trajectory, direction or word search. This is a directionwise reduction of the actual room; it is NOT a global finite-case reduction or the uncapped theorem.

## Exact statement

Let P be a finite strictly simple canonical45 triangle disk with W maximal straight walls, and actual distinct interior A marks S,T. Let Gamma be the derivative group generated by its wall reflections, q=|Gamma|<=8, and let M be its usual connected reflection unfolding using q copies. Let Sigma consist of ALL lifts of genuine polygon corners, INCLUDING regular/removable corner lifts. Let Sset,Tset be the separate q-point interior lift sets. Set Xi=Sigma union Sset union Tset; no transparent lattice vertex is added.

For every primitive integer vector w=(p,r)!=0, the +w foliation admits a directed marked graph G_w with:

1. Vertices exactly Xi, with its actual source, target and absorbing-corner labels and the actual foliation prongs retained.
2. One edge for each forward outgoing prong at Xi, ending at the FIRST subsequent Xi encounter. Edge interiors contain no Xi and no forbidden corner. Every edge has finite positive length ell*|w|, with ell a positive integer in the time parametrization z(t)=z(0)+t*w.
3. Exactly E=q(W+2)/2 edges, hence E<=4W+8<=92 when W<=21, independent of k, area, diameter or number of interior A points. Vertices number at most E, because every vertex has at least one forward prong.
4. A directed path from a designated source lift to a target lift, with no Sigma vertex traversed, is EXACTLY a legal finite unfolded connection in that direction. In particular, some physical launch direction in Gamma*w connects S to T iff G_w has an edge whose starting label is S and ending label is T. This edge criterion uses all ACTUAL lifts; it never replaces a target by its torus image.

Consequently P has a dark S/T pair iff NONE of these marked directed graphs, over all primitive integer w, contains an S-to-T edge. This quantifies over unbounded directions; it is not a completed decision procedure uniform in W.

## 1. Arithmetic torus cover

All polygon vertices and both marks have integer coordinates. The reflection charts have integer translation transitions: axes reflect with translation(2c,0) or(0,2c), diagonal even-intercept mirrors with translation(c,+/-c). Therefore developing coordinates moduloZ^2 define a finite translation cover pi:M->R^2/Z^2, branched only over0. Every Xi point lies over0; pi^{-1}(0) also contains finitely many unmarked regular points, which are NOT forbidden. This is the same arithmetic unfolding fact used in the independently accepted endpoint-rationality bridge.

For canonical directions, a bounded polygon has at least two nonparallel wall directions. Its reflection group is the perpendicular four-element group or the eight-element square group, so q is4or8. The q-copy unfolding is connected because crossing physical edges generates Gamma. No extra disconnected eight-copy unfolding is imposed on an orthogonal polygon.

## 2. Every prong has a finite first Xi return

Project a trajectory starting at Xi in a specified forward prong to the primitive torus loop t*w. This loop returns to0 exactly at integer times. Between those returns, it cannot meet Sigma or Xi because they project to0. At each integer return the lift is a point of the finite fiber pi^{-1}(0); stop at the first point in Xi.

Suppose no such stop ever occurs. Then some regular fiber state repeats. Ordinary straight flow is uniquely reversible at every regular point, so that repetition places the orbit on a regular periodic closed leaf. If the start was a singular corner prong, such a regular closed leaf cannot have that corner in its backward continuation, contradiction. If the start was a regular Xi point (a removable corner or a source/target lift), the same periodic leaf returns to that starting Xi point, again contradiction. Thus the first subsequent Xi encounter occurs at a finite positive integer time ell.

This also proves that rational flow away from the critical trajectories is periodic: a regular torus phase returns to a finite covering fiber, and if it never meets a marked vertex it eventually repeats. A source or target on an interior cylinder leaf is therefore handled: its leaf is cut at that mark and returns to Xi. Such marks are not silently assumed to lie on singular saddle connections.

## 3. Counting edges without counting lattice vertices

At a cone point of total angle2pi*m, a fixed oriented direction has m forward outgoing prongs. This includes m=1 for a regular marked point. For a physical polygon corner of angle theta, the q copies contribute a TOTAL angle q*theta distributed among its lifts. Hence the sum of m over those lifts is q*theta/(2pi). Summing over physical corners and using sum(theta)=pi(W-2) gives

    sum_(corner lifts) m = q(W-2)/2.

The interior S and T lift sets are disjoint free Gamma orbits of q regular points each, adding2q. Thus

    E=sum_(Xi) m = q(W-2)/2+2q = q(W+2)/2.

Every forward prong starts exactly one first-return edge by Section2. No two distinct edges can merge at a regular interior point: unique backward continuation would identify their previous Xi encounter and its prong. Reverse continuation supplies the incoming prongs. Edges may be loops, including a periodic cylinder leaf containing one marked point. Their lengths and the many suppressed unmarked regular integer points can be arbitrarily large; their NUMBER is controlled by W alone.

## 4. Actual optical equivalence

For a designated S lift, follow the +w flow. Each Xi encounter is a vertex of G_w. At an S/T vertex the flow has its unique regular continuation; at any Sigma vertex the physical ray is forbidden and must stop. Thus traversing a finite directed edge sequence without Sigma is exactly the original straight flow, not an arbitrary concatenation with a change of direction.

All such straight segments have finite length and avoid every physical corner lift. Standard unfolding/refolding therefore gives finitely many smooth specular contacts and the actual interior S,T endpoints. An interior starting point cannot acquire a segment lying along an original wall seam without first encountering a corner; the ordinary unfolding convention does not introduce a grazing-wall alternative.

Suppose a legal S-to-T path in this direction meets other S lifts. Truncate at its LAST S-labeled vertex before its FIRST T encounter. There is no Xi in between: Sigma is forbidden, and all other marked vertices have S or T labels. This final portion is one S-to-T edge. It starts at some actual S lift, corresponding to a launch direction in Gamma*w in the physical room. Conversely any such edge refolds to a valid physical connection. For a prescribed physical launch direction, use the designated-source graph-PATH criterion rather than incorrectly switch source sheets.

Finally, every physical S/T connection has a rational unfolded direction. Its developed endpoints have integer coordinates in charts with integer transitions, so their nonzero straight displacement is an integer vector. Divide by its gcd to obtain w. This proves the quantified all-directions criterion.

## 5. What remains open across directions

The unweighted graph types at E<=92 form a finite abstract collection, but the actual room need not realize every such type. Even the full collection it DOES realize has not been characterized. Edge lengths, cylinder phases and integer return times remain unbounded. We have neither a finite direction list nor a bounded number of critical-piece visits nor a theorem that some realizable graph has an S-to-T edge at W<=21.

Changing w amounts to varying the rational-direction decomposition of one FIXED arithmetic surface with marked endpoint sets and its polygon reflection structure. Forgetting weights and assuming arbitrary graph transitions would lose that relation. A useful next global lemma must constrain which ALL-dark diagrams can coexist on one strictly simple <=21-wall quotient. The directionwise edge bound supplies a scale-invariant state language, not that missing coexistence theorem.
