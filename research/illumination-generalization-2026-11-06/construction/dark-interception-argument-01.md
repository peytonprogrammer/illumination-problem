# DI01: exact cone-slot relation, but unit-period physical capacity is vacuous

## 1. Conditional cone-turn identity

Use the unit torus cover of the original orthogonal unfolding, with actual endpoint fibers X,Y and all genuine cones over0. A reflex cone z has angle6pi. Fix determinant-one primitive u,v. A specified incoming u prong at z has a unique outgoing v prong bounding the narrow oriented sector of the triangle conv{0,u,u+v}; its angle is strictly between0 andpi. Three incoming prongs, separated by2pi in the6pi cone, give three distinct outgoing prongs separated by2pi. This is a bijection b_z between the two sets of three slots.

Suppose an incoming slot is reached from actual x in X by the primitive u segment of duration1, and its b_z slot reaches actual y in Y by the primitive v segment of duration1. The open developed triangle contains no lattice point, since the determinant is+/-1. The open triangle therefore lifts as an unbranched simply connected sheet. Select its germ along the actual incoming edge. At z the specified NARROW sector, not an arbitrary cone continuation, fixes the sheet adjacent to the outgoing edge. At the regular x and y vertices, the completion is unique. The third side is consequently the actual straight mediant segment from x to the SAME y. Its open part projects away from0, so it avoids every physical corner, genuine or regular. This is a single straight ray, not the broken u-then-v route.

Thus full off-orbit physical darkness implies b_z(I_u(z)) is disjoint from J_v(z), and |I_u(z)|+|J_v(z)|<=3. The specified incoming and outgoing segments reach z at their first genuine-cone time because their open primitive-period portions project away from0. Cone slots outside b_z are not certified by this argument. Both determinant signs use the corresponding oriented triangle side.

## 2. Why this gives NO new capacity in our actual unit-period class

For orthogonal integral mirrors, every affine reflection has a diagonal sign matrix and an EVEN integral translation: reflection in x=c sends x to2c-x, and likewise y. Thus all developed images of a physical integer point retain its coordinate parity. Every actual X and Y lift of an even-even mark has parity(0,0).

If a unit-time u ray from X ends at physical corner C, then C mod2=u mod2. If a unit-time v ray from C ends in Y, then C mod2=v mod2. Determinant-one u,v are a basis modulo2, so their nonzero residues are different. These two conditions are incompatible. In the actual class I and J cannot both be nonempty at the same physical cone, BEFORE invoking darkness or the cone-slot inequality.

Therefore DI01 is a valid local cone-turn relation but its unit-period capacity is already automatic from physical parity. It gives no new physical corner lower bound and is not positive progress on the universal theorem. Do not promote it to independent blocker charges for the three parity classes.

## 3. Retaining arbitrary first-cone times changes the issue

Let the first genuine cone in direction u from some X be the physical C at positive integer time a, and let a reverse-target first cone in direction -v from some Y be that SAME physical C at positive integer time b. The diagonal-sign/even-translation argument gives

    a*u = b*v = C mod2.

Because u,v are independent modulo2, a and b must BOTH be even, and C must be even-even. This is exact for unbounded a,b, every source/target sheet and repeated developed images. It is not an assertion that such shared interceptions exist or that all first times are even. In particular a first-cone time cannot simply be normalized to1 while keeping the same physical lattice, fibers and Farey relation.

The larger triangle conv{0,a*u,a*u+b*v} generally has extra lattice points. Those may lift to genuine cones. The unit-period cone-turn proof cannot discard them or identify its diagonal with an unobstructed connection. This is the missing metric/phase information, not a permitted optical turn.

## 5. Decision

DI01's local cone-slot implication is proved, but its attempted physical unit-capacity force is vacuous. The unbounded-time parity restriction is a prerequisite, not nine distinct corner charges. Q0 has only non-even-even reflex corners, making its finite first-cone times odd; Q100 has even-even reflex corners and the explicit even-time event above. Neither the six-wall same-physical-point involution nor nonphysical Heisenberg control changes the actual off-orbit hypothesis.

No new engine, enumeration, search clock or cutoff was used. The theorem remains open. A useful next step must handle the first physically possible EVEN durations and the extra lattice events in the triangle, rather than reusing the empty unit triangle unchanged.
