## 2. A first-right-exit cap route from the left has NO roof corner

Each triangular fold is even about any boundary phase. Consequently the ENTIRE formal rectangular flow has the exact symmetry

whenever both sides are defined. This is an identity of the two explicit fold functions, including both actual coordinates.

If T<2t_c, then gamma(T)=gamma(2t_c-T) is an earlier right-mouth contact, contradicting that all earlier lower contacts lie in M (or the initial left mouth). Equality T=2t_c would identify the final right-mouth point with the initial left-mouth point, impossible because a<w.

If T>2t_c, then gamma(2t_c)=gamma(0) is an earlier left-mouth contact, also forbidden. Thus every possibility contradicts the first-right-exit guards. NO roof corner occurs in such a programme. This eliminates all top-corner exclusions at once for every finite ell; it is not a bounded reflection check.

The intermediate notch contacts themselves avoid both reflexes by strict membership in(a,w). The final strict right-mouth guard avoids(w,h). Ordinary outer-wall zero residuals remain legal original-wall contacts. Therefore the stopped cap programme is an actual finite corner-free specular path from its original left entry to its original right exit, with one common direction.
