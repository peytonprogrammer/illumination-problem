# CE04: commensurate source arms of arbitrary width

Candidate published before prescribed verification. This extends CE03 by
allowing an independent source width P*e, rather than normalizing it to e.
It is a sufficient family theorem, not the whole spiral or r3 theorem.

Let e>0 rational and P,K,L,N be positive integers satisfying

    2<=K<L, P<N-L.

Put a=P*e, A=N*e, w=(N-K)*e, l=(N-L)*e in the ORIGINAL
ordered spiral polygon from CE03. Integral heights obey0<b<c<h<H.
Actual rational marks satisfy0<s<a,b<u<H,l<t<A,0<v<c,
with integral ordinates u,v. Use CE03 developed heights d0,C,g1,d2,D.
Assume

    delta=gcd(P*C,N)<K,
    Lambda=lcm(P,N,K), gcd(Lambda*d2,L)=1.

Claim: a finite actual S-to-T ordinary corner-free specular path exists.
Upper-cap singular formal prefixes are discarded only at an exact return
to the SAME actual S, retaining the legal descending suffix.

Missing implication: prove two strong narrow-unit endpoint exclusions
remain noncovering, then select the cap orbit at spacing2e*delta;
correct the whole bottom target period by the invariant slope shift2e*Lambda.
The source guard is OMITTED at u=h (it can be identically bad modulo2e
although the actual source is strictly inside the P*e mouth).
Strongest objection: modulo2e information must not replace actual source
period2Pe, right period2Ke or target period2Le, and upper-cap continuations
must not traverse an original roof corner.
Success: full original-room path/velocity/first-target proof plus independent
review. No search, cutoff or new clock is proposed.
