# CE01: bottom width L*a and an L-residue target correction

Positive candidate stated before prescribed verification. No independent
acceptance is claimed. This extends the coordinator's COORD-COPRIME-01
construction; its full independent audit is separately peer-owned.

Let L>=2 and N>=L+2 be integers, a>0 rational,

    A=Na, w=(N-1)a, l=(N-L)a, R=a, B=La.

Let 0<b<c<h<H be integers, G=2(H-h), gcd(G,N)=1.
Take actual rational S=(s,u), 0<s<a, b<u<=h, u integral,
and T=(t,v), l<t<A, 0<v<c, v integral. Put

    d0=h-u, g1=h-c, d2=c-v, D=d0+G+g1+d2.

Assume gcd(N*d2,L)=1. Claim: there is a finite actual
corner-avoiding one-roof S-to-T path, with no reflection/period cutoff.
Unlike the coordinator's terminal lemma, d2 need not be odd.

Key new steps to prove:

1. Initially the hand candidate excluded equal four developed heights.
   A stronger hand argument, added BEFORE checks, removes that exception:
   if the cap-forbidden class exists in that resonance, the source-forbidden
   class is empty because 0<s<a. Hence the dangerous (2,4,4) covering is
   impossible even for even d2. No odd-d2 assumption is needed here.
2. After the cap orbit chooses a strict return, target matching modulo2a
   leaves E=z+2aJ. For bottom width La, actual target matching requires
   J=0 modulo L. A slope shift2aN preserves all three portal residuals
   and increments J by N*d2. Coprimality solves this exactly in <=L-1
   shifts. This is an arithmetic choice, not a sampling bound.
3. All finite original-wall, signed velocity, roof and first-target guards
   survive changing B from2a toLa.

    L>=3 odd, N>=L+2 odd, gcd(N,L)=1,
    c=4, 0<b<4, 4<h, H=h+1 (integers).
