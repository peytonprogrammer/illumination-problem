# Convex completion of the full left-return programme: soundness, not existence

## Scope and prior work

This applies the previously accepted full-physical-fiber/zero-only formulation recorded in global-bridge-impasse.md. It is not a new darkness classification, new nonemptiness theorem. It settles a guard question in LR01 while retaining its frozen literal-path guards.

Fix any finite strictly simple orthogonal room, distinct actual interior S,T, and tau>0. A completed path is a FINITE piecewise-linear path from actual S to actual T, at one fixed nonzero speed, lying in the room. Every ordinary reflection is at an actual open wall and obeys the specular law. At an actual convex right-angle vertex ONLY, allow the formal law v_out=-v_in (the two commuting perpendicular-wall reflections). No reflex-corner contact, grazing boundary segment, artificial mirror or arbitrary sector continuation is allowed. Every other segment/first-hit requirement remains physical. Multiple actual preimages in an unfolding are retained, not identified by a desired endpoint label.

Claim NC1. Such a completed path exists at absolute slope tau IF AND ONLY IF there is an ordinary finite corner-avoiding actual S-to-T path at that same absolute slope. The ordinary path can use a different source germ/sheet. It does not preserve a prescribed initial signed velocity or prescribed individual lift. Every path still has one common unfolded direction in its own physical unfolding.

## Finite last-source proof

The reverse of any ordinary billiard segment is ordinary: at a wall with reflection matrix J, J(-Jv)=-v. Its open segments, finite wall contacts and corner guards are the same in reverse order. At a formal convex vertex the required outgoing vector is precisely the negative incoming vector.

First truncate a completed path at its FIRST actual T visit. There are finitely many actual S visits because there are finitely many nonconstant straight segments. Retain the suffix beginning at the LAST actual S visit before that first T. Suppose this suffix contains a convex reversal C, and take its first such C. Its prefix from S to C has only ordinary contacts. The outgoing ray at C therefore retraces that ENTIRE prefix by deterministic reversibility, including every previous regular cap/left-floor excursion and its finite wall contacts. The prefix has no T visit, by first-T truncation, so its retrace cannot reach T before S. It returns to actual S at a later time before the endpoint, contradicting the chosen last S visit. Thus the retained suffix contains no convex reversal.

All remaining contacts are ordinary by hypothesis; reflex contacts were never admitted. The suffix has positive finite length since S and T are distinct. It reaches the same actual T and contains no earlier T. Hence it is a legal actual connecting path. Conversely every ordinary path is already a completed path.

The proof uses the WHOLE physical prefix, not an assumed identification of an arbitrary mouth coordinate with the source. A generic mouth state alone has no such conclusion.

## NC2: application to LR01's full source-injected union

Take the original ordered spiral and actual terminal S,T. Let L_tau be the union over all physically admitted source germs of LR01 finite left-return programmes followed by first R exit and its direct-or-floor target tail. Keep both target images and both source horizontal signs, as well as upward/downward initial source options. Define its convex completion by allowing the NC1 reversal at convex roof/left-floor/bottom-floor vertices, retaining all REFLEX mouth/shelf/corner exclusions and all original wall guards.

A successful completed programme has an ordinary last-S suffix as above. Before its first R exit, it remains in the cap/left arm. Therefore that suffix begins either in the original upper-source chart, in a lower-source upward chart, or in a lower-source downward/source-floor chart. At a source on y=h the same outgoing germ convention selects the appropriate initial passage. After the last S it consists of genuine cap episodes and genuine left-floor returns until the same first R exit, followed by the retained direct/floor target tail, or an earlier actual-T truncation of that tail. No earlier R excursion is created by taking a suffix. Thus it belongs to the same FULL admitted source/target union at tau.

Consequently convex completion adds no new successful slopes to this full union. It can be used as an existential relaxation ONLY with the entire source history and all initial germs retained. A completed orbit starting from an isolated xi cannot be declared legal. A fixed word cannot be kept through a convex singularity by perturbing its direction; the conclusion selects a suffix of the exact completed trajectory.

At a left-floor corner, LR01 has xi_d+V*tau in aZ. The formal two-wall formula gives xi_u=-xi_d modulo 2a and the horizontal velocity is reversed. The following cap flight retraces the cap prefix. If a symbolic computation instead jumps to an independently selected next cap state, it does not satisfy NC1. Likewise, any reflex encounter remains fatal; its 270-degree sector does not have the prescribed convex continuation.

## Consequence and actual remaining obstruction

LR01 correctly excludes floor corners from each literal legal word. Its warning against an automatic source-suffix argument at a generic mouth state is also correct. With a COMPLETE source-injected completed trajectory, NC1 supplies the missing identification by retracing the full prior history to actual S. This is the precise distinction.

This observation does NOT supply an actual target root. In particular, LR01 already excludes EVERY ordinary member of its punctured near-common-period slope band for all finite left returns. NC2 transfers that exclusion to the convex-completed union: inserting formal convex reversals cannot rescue that band.

Away from that band, no proved source/target intersection or complete all-tau failure follows. Extending through convex punctures does not yield a continuous closed shooting curve: reflex mouth/shelf endpoints still split domains, non-target R exit is a stopped outcome, and a closed cycle or nonzero degree has not been established. A degree proof that assumes these properties would repeat the retired root/current-balance argument. We therefore do NOT launch that purported new approach.

Success criterion attained: existential guard soundness for a full actual-source prefix. General nonemptiness success criterion NOT attained.
