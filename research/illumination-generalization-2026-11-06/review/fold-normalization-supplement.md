# Canonical compatibility is automatic for a normalized literal fold

R21 hand supplement, same original effort/deadline. This removes an EXTRA hypothesis from the preceding corollaries; it does not infer a fold from darkness. No computation or discovery scan.

Let B be the strict canonical45 parity base of fold-base-incidence-supplement.md, with noncorner integer points only in its even-even A class, and two distinct smooth boundary A marks a,b. Let Q be a strictly simple disk assembled from m whole congruent copies g_i(B) with disjoint interiors and literal inverse-copy agreement at every contact. Suppose actual interior marks S,T project to a,b respectively. The placement maps may initially be arbitrary Euclidean isometries, and no grid alignment of Q is assumed.

## 1. Normalize one copy, then use seam words

Normalize the entire room by the rigid isometry g_0^{-1}, and put h_i=g_0^{-1} composed with g_i. Thus h_0 is identity. The positive-contact graph is connected: the copies' open interiors cannot form a connected disk through isolated point contacts.

For adjacent copies i,j, the accepted full-wall rigidity gives a physical supporting-line reflection taking g_i(B) to g_j(B), with the SPECIFIED copy maps. Equivalently

    g_j = g_i composed with r,

where r is reflection in the corresponding maximal wall line of the original B. This follows by conjugating the physical seam reflection by g_i; literal inverse-map agreement fixes that base wall pointwise. Consequently

    h_j = h_i composed with r.

Induction along a graph path expresses EVERY h_i as a finite word in the affine wall reflections of B. Cycles create no ambiguity: the h_i are the given globally defined maps; no path-independence is being inferred from arbitrary labels.

## 2. Every generator preserves the canonical tiling and A

A canonical base axial wall lies on x=c or y=c with integer c. Its reflection is a signed coordinate permutation plus a translation with even integer entries. A diagonal wall lies on x-y=c or x+y=c with EVEN integer c; its reflection swaps signed coordinates and adds(c,-c) or(c,c), up to the chosen sign convention. These translations are even. Thus every generator preserves the integer lattice, the A class2Z², the B class of odd-odd points, and the C class of mixed parity points. It also preserves the canonical triangle tiling: the tiling is uniquely specified by splitting each unit square along its A--B diagonal, and the reflected squares have the same A--B rule.

Every h_i therefore preserves that same tiling and A class. The normalized union Q_hat=g_0^{-1}(Q)=union h_i(B) is a finite strictly simple canonical45 triangle disk. Its actual normalized endpoint images are A: if S belongs to copy i and projects to a, then S=g_i(a), so g_0^{-1}(S)=h_i(a); similarly T maps to h_j(b). Actual distinctness, strict interiority and ordinary specular illumination are preserved by the rigid normalization.

This proves the grid/class hypotheses needed by fold-exception-bound-supplement.md automatically for THIS normalized base class. It is not valid for an arbitrary base with another parity convention, or maps agreeing only modulo a symmetry.

## 3. Full construction-class theorem

Assume W(Q)<=21. Base incidence gives w>=8. The independently reviewed assembly identity gives W>=m(w-4)+4>=4m+4, hence m<=4. In Q_hat every transparent non-A integer point is a smooth/interior seam junction, so k_Qhat<=2m-2<=6. The accepted historical canonical45 theorem supplies a finite corner-avoiding specular path between the ACTUAL normalized S,T. Applying g_0 yields an actual S-to-T path in Q.

Therefore every room in this entire literal whole-congruent-copy class of a strict canonical45 parity base, with actual interior preimages of two distinct smooth boundary base A marks, has W>=22 if those actual marks are dark. This includes diagonal bases, shared seam endpoints, cycles and arbitrary initial rigid placements. No extra grid-preservation assumption on the placement is needed.

## Remaining gap

No claim here says an arbitrary dark room has such a fold, or that a parity factor preserves two distinct smooth marked fibers. Those existence/necessity requirements remain OPEN. The theorem excludes a precisely defined sufficient-construction class below22; it does not settle the unrestricted W<=21 target or arbitrary-polygon minimality. Its optical dependency is the already accepted historical k<=6 theorem, not a new certificate replay.
