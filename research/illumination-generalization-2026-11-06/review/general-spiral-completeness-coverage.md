## Exact symbolic complement, and what is not yet expanded

Let R(D) be the terminal actual-pair set above. Let H_i(D,S,T;theta) be the EXACT full hypotheses of each accepted constructor in blocks 1–6, with theta its permitted chart, actual source/target sign, normalization or stated global isometry. Let

    C_known(D,S,T) = OR_i EXISTS permitted theta H_i(D,S,T;theta).

Then the symbolic set still needing an argument is

    U_known = {(D,S,T): (S,T) in R(D) AND
                       FOR EVERY i,theta, NOT H_i(D,S,T;theta)}.

This complement is of SUFFICIENT HYPOTHESES, not of legal rays. Its membership says only that the recorded constructors have not supplied that pair. The formula is exact relative to a complete receipt registry. A simplified finite list of arithmetic inequalities for U_known is NOT yet independently established because the legacy strict-lattice/upper-route predicates have not all been expanded or proved subsumed. Calling the AP01 divisor failure the complement of ALL accepted results would be false.

For a lower pair, the bare four-selector complement can be written more explicitly: for each permitted source-time X in {h-u,h+u-2b} and target-time d in {c-v,c+v}, compute the corresponding M,g and metric. Failure is the conjunction, over those four choices, of

    (g does not divide K) OR (g>P) OR
    [PF+g(X+C)>K(X+C+F)], F=h-c+d.

This formula describes that particular scalar test union only, and assumes the chosen source/target/period composition has actually been proved. It does not eliminate the legacy blocks, AP07, or full roots of the same itineraries.
