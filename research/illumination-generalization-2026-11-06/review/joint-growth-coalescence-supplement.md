# Interior-coalescence stratum in the same joint-growth effort

R26 supplement under its ORIGINAL deadline. Frozen joint-growth-limit-witness.md is unchanged. This fills a positive case excluded by its distinct limiting endpoint premise.

Use the affine family and full trace K from section1 of that packet. Suppose S0=T0=z and dist(z,K)>0, with actual S(L),T(L) interior and distinct for all stated large L. Then the actual pair illuminates by a DIRECT segment for every sufficiently large L; no limiting S0-to-T0 witness is required.

Indeed choose delta>0 with the closed ball B(z,3delta) disjoint from K. All normalized actual walls converge to K, so for sufficiently small epsilon they avoid B(z,2delta). Both normalized actual endpoints are in B(z,delta), since they equal z+epsilon*S1,z+epsilon*T1. The segment between them lies in that ball and cannot touch a boundary. Because its starting point is inside the actual room, every point of the segment is inside the same interior component. It reaches the actual target, with no reflection or corner contact. Multiplying by L preserves the statement. Distinctness of the actual endpoints is retained even though their normalized limits coincide.

For rational coefficients an explicit rational cutoff follows by bounding the displacement of every indexed segment by epsilon*max_i|v_i^1| and endpoint displacements by epsilon*max(|S1|,|T1|), using any rational square or L-infinity box separated from every limiting segment. This is a finite exact segment-box separation computation, not a direction search.

The condition is open in the leading data. A compact set of coalescent limits with uniform positive trace separation and bounded offset coefficients has a uniform direct-path tail. Such neighborhoods may join the strict-witness neighborhoods in a compact-direction cover and its finite-prefix criterion. Coalescence ON the trace is not covered. Nor is a source on one side of a disappearing internal slit and a target on another. No universal directional cover is asserted.

This supplement uses actual family interiority and the FULL trace, so it introduces no artificial mirror, no endpoint-sheet substitution and no continuity assumption about darkness. It is a positive tail certificate for a particular previously excluded degeneration, not an all-W21 theorem.
