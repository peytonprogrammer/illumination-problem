## F11 — REJECT Proposition 2 for general intermediate torus lattices

File 03 §2 assumes that a lift T' of the base endpoint satisfies T'-S in 2Z² because physical S,T are even-even. In fact

    T'-S belongs to T-S+Lambda = Lambda,

and an intermediate lattice containing 2Z² can have odd vectors. Those vectors are legitimate developed displacements for BASE segments. Their lifts generally end at another member of the original endpoint fiber. That distinction is precisely the unresolved sheet-selection problem; it cannot be discarded while claiming that the base segment set is empty.

Exact torus countercontrol, requiring no room choice: take Lambda=Z² and Bad0={0}. Every integer corner class collapses to 0, so this mark set contains the images of ALL original corners, including all three parity classes. The segment gamma(t)=[(t,0)], 0<=t<=1, is a positive-length straight loop based at 0. Its interior avoids Bad0. Thus the claimed "base is dark for every such Lambda" is false. A regular original source lift has a unique legal lift if the bad-set lifting hypothesis holds, but no conclusion selects actual T. A legal base loop is not an original S-to-T path.

For Lambda=2Z² with all FOUR parity points marked, the previously accepted midpoint self-darkness argument is valid. It does not imply the corresponding assertion after quotienting to Lambda=Z². Self-darkness of the marked base is not preserved under this quotient: a shorter base loop need not lift to a closed loop upstairs.

## F13 — CORRECT the A-state set and finite-arc criterion in Lemma M

A rational native successful path has developed displacement 2n(p,q), with primitive integer (p,q). The reason for the factor 2 is that reflections at integer walls preserve the even-even endpoint parity. Every true corner encountered in the interior occurs at an integer developed lattice point. Keeping the actual sheet and stopping at every original corner therefore gives a valid lattice-sampling necessity/sufficiency, subject to the endpoint convention below.

However, the regular A-fiber in the four-copy unfolding is not in general the set of 4I lifts of strictly interior A points. An even-coordinate smooth wall can carry even-even points. These have regular preimages in the glued surface and may be sampled at time 2n. They must be kept, or a separate first-return operation must skip them with its own proof. Excluding them silently changes sigma_theta. The full regular A-fiber is finite; it includes smooth wall states as well as interior states. Original convex-corner lifts remain forbidden despite being regular as translation-surface points.

For a step of developed length 2(p,q), the only STRICT intermediate lattice point is at +(p,q). The endpoint at +2(p,q) must also be regular and not an original forbidden corner. This is an endpoint guard, not a second strict intermediate point. Retain the starting sheet and both direction signs. Illumination of a physical pair asks for SOME actual target lift; membership of one prescribed target lift is a stronger directional assertion unless the lift is explicitly existentially selected.

The accepted direction-graph-independent-derivation.md and direction-skeleton-review.md already provide the exact all-physical-lift finite-arc formulation without assuming a globally clean leaf. The lattice reformulation is a repair, not a new nonemptiness theorem.
