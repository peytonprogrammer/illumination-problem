# Independent prerequisite for an original left-floor prefix

## Below/mouth sources: the floor guard is additional

For actual S=(s,u) with b<u<=h and initial downward vy, normalize |vy|=1 and horizontal magnitude tau>0. Let sigma=+-1 encode initial horizontal sign by the actual source fold F_a(sigma*s+tau*time), where a=Pe. The actual initial floor y=b is reached at time f=u-b, and ascending h at time d0floor=u+h-2b. A subsequent ordinary roof and descent to T have total time

    Dfloor=u+2H-2b-v
          =d0floor+2(H-h)+(h-c)+(c-v).

The initial horizontal reflection is an actual original wall ONLY IF

    0 < F_a(sigma*s+f*tau) < a,
    equivalently sigma*s+f*tau NOT IN a*Z.

Both zero and a are TRUE floor corners. Unlike a zero centered residual at the h portal, a zero floor coordinate is illegal. Strictness of the later source/cap/right passage residuals alone does not imply this condition.

On an integer seeded slope grid tau=(A+epsilon*z-sigma*s+2eq)/Dfloor, the floor-bad set is empty or one integer residue class of period

    pFloor=P*Dfloor/gcd(2f,P*Dfloor)>P,

because0<2f<=d0floor<Dfloor. The strict source-return endpoint event is separately at time d0floor modulo2Pe and has period P*Dfloor/gcd(d0floor,P*Dfloor)>P. The usual right event is a third condition. It is not justified to import a two-event noncover theorem without accounting for the floor event's exact offset and its interactions.

A final literal-residual-preserving Lambda slope correction also preserves the floor phase modulo2a:

    delta(floor phase)=2eLambda*p*f
                     =2a*(Lambda/P)*p*f in 2a*Z.

Thus it preserves a GOOD actual floor contact, but cannot repair a BAD floor corner. The good floor condition must be obtained before that final correction. This is an original physical guard, not optional symbolic bookkeeping.

At u=h, f=h-b and d0floor=2f. The two source/floor event times are related, but their moduli and offsets differ. For a bad floor phase the return phase is2j*a-sigma*s and hence folds to actual s; it is not a source-return corner. This does not alone prove that the union of all three exclusions leaves a usable cap choice.

## Upper source caveat

For u>h, the initial descent starts in the FULL cap, whose walls are x0,A. Treating the entire descent from S as a billiard in widtha introduces an artificial reflection at x=a aboveh. A valid original floor prefix must first cross the actual left h mouth with x<a and matching signed velocity, using the full-cap fold before that crossing, then the widtha source strip. Endpoint matching at the final ascending h mouth does not establish this initial first-hit/left-mouth guard. A theorem may instead explicitly restrict the floor-prefix construction to u<=h and use another result for upper sources.
