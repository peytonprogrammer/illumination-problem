# Single irrational strip: first-witness applicability screen

R29 bounded primary-source and hand-algebra screen. Outcome: the proposed irrational-gap shortcut does NOT yet supply a witness on an arbitrary strip ray. No imported classification is applied without its missing hypotheses. No claim is made that no useful theorem exists beyond this screen.

## Primary hypotheses actually checked

[Apisa, arXiv1601.07894v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1601.07894), Introduction definition of generic and Theorems1.4/1.5: the finite-blocking classification assumes the surface has dense GL(2,R) orbit in its ambient stratum. Choosing one irrational coordinate gap does not establish that hypothesis. One cannot replace it by irrational wall data or by belonging to a hyperelliptic locus.

[Apisa--Wright, arXiv1708.03411v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1708.03411v3), Theorems1.1,1.3,1.4,3.6,3.16 and Proposition3.11: the non-torus blocking statements still permit periodic endpoint pairs and identifications under a minimal half-translation quotient. Their classification over an entire quadratic stratum requires the relevant orbit-closure premise. No checked statement says that every one-free-gap family contains a member with that orbit closure, that the designated actual endpoint fibers avoid its exceptions, or that their images remain distinct. Finite exceptional sets do not imply the two designated endpoints are outside them.

These are scope findings, not a new no-illumination theorem. Primitivity, minimal covering maps, periodicity and actual full fibers remain obligations before a positive application. The existing real-wall/torus-cover bridge must be used before even asserting non-torus behavior for a particular family; irrationality alone is not substituted for its exact coordinate-ratio criterion.

## Exact algebraic fact available for this family

In a vertical coordinate-gap family, anchor the actual S at x=0. All other fixed coordinates and gap offsets are rational, all x coordinates are affine rational functions of the variable lambda, and y coordinates are fixed rational numbers. Every FINITE specified reflected vertex or target image therefore has coordinates (a*lambda+b,c) with rational coefficients. This follows inductively: a horizontal mirror changes only the sign and rational translation of y; a vertical mirror changes x to minus x plus twice an affine rational wall coordinate.

Subtract the fixed source. For one prescribed corner image V(lambda) and target image U(lambda), their determinant is a rational affine polynomial in lambda. At an irrational parameter its vanishing forces BOTH rational coefficients to vanish. Thus these two prescribed images are either collinear identically in the parameter, or never collinear at an irrational parameter. This also includes the horizontal-image case c=0; no division by a zero component is made.

This separates accidental corner collinearity from identities. It does NOT manufacture an admissible target word. A straight segment toward a formal target image can cross the wrong physical walls, have the wrong collision order, or represent a structurally blocked word. Ordering inequalities can vary with the parameter. There is no proved exclusion of all persistent collinearity identities, and no passage from this algebra to an actual S/T connection. A missing image construction cannot be repaired by counting the nonzero determinant polynomials.

## Disposition and next useful work

Retire the inference "one irrational free gap implies a generic illuminated member." Keep the exact affine determinant fact as a diagnostic only. It is not a new W21 family result or a proof of witness existence. The positive fixed-piece first-witness and effective-prefix results R27/R28 proceed independently of this screen; their actual finite prefixes still need verification. Priority returns to the author's new reflex-coalescence proof and to a concrete finite-prefix or limiting-direction certificate, rather than another unsupported genericity claim.
