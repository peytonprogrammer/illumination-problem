## Actual-source revisit at a bad floor candidate

Let S=(s,u) be in the bottom rectangle [0,A]x[0,c], with 0<u<c. A source-floor chart starts at unfolded height q=-u and has x(q)=F_A(s+tau(q+u)), physical y=|q|. Suppose its floor phase B=s+tau*u belongs to A Z. The triangular-wave identity F_A(B-v)=F_A(B+v) gives

    x(u)=F_A(B+tau*u)=F_A(B-tau*u)=F_A(s)=s.

At q=u the folded path is back at the ACTUAL S, strictly before the first portal c. Discard everything before q=u. The retained portion begins at an interior point, has the inherited actual outgoing direction, and never reaches the suspect floor corner. This is a concrete coordinate instance of the previously accepted full-fiber retracing lemma, not a new permission for corner continuation.

Likewise, if a final-roof chart has a bad convex roof phase, the accepted target-prefix identity reaches actual T on the upward leg. In a nested staircase with S in the bottom band and T in the top band, the two truncations cannot conflict: the source revisit is below the first portal, while the target-prefix arrival is above the last portal. All intermediate portal guards stay strict. If an extremal phase is not a corner, its floor or roof reflection is an ordinary smooth-wall contact. Thus either or BOTH convex corner excursions may be discarded without changing the specified physical endpoints.

## Finite exact exhaustion of the four charts in the fixed rational room

Use widths (10,3,2,1) and seam heights (0,3,4,5,10). For S=(s,u) in the lowest band and T=(t,v) in the highest, the four choices have unfolded start q0=u or -u and end q1=v or 20-v. Let D=q1-q0>0. Vertical travel through the four rectangles has lengths

    d0=3-q0, d1=1, d2=1, d3=q1-5,

all strictly positive for the prescribed marks. In each chart the horizontal coordinate is F_{w_j}(s+tau(q-q0)-C_j). At a portal, subtract an even period of the old width so that its residual lies in (-w_old,w_old]; accept precisely when its absolute value is STRICTLY below the next width. Then retain that signed residual as the initial coordinate in the new fold. This matches positions and velocities at smooth left contacts as well as ordinary portal crossings. Equality at either aperture endpoint is rejected.

Choose a positive rational period P such that P*d_j is an even period of width w_j for every j. Such P exists by rational least common multiple. Replacing tau by tau+P preserves the signed residual at the first portal. Inductively it preserves every later signed residual and the final target fold. Therefore all portal and target conditions are periodic in tau with period P. Any negative slope can be translated into [0,P), or conversely a valid representative can be increased to become positive. Extra vertical-wall folds caused by that translation remain genuine smooth contacts; floor/roof corners are purified as described above.

All C_j in this fixed room are even integers. Because the top width is one, actual target arrival is equivalent to

    s+tau*D = 2n+t  OR  2n-t,

for some integer n, once every portal condition holds. Enumerating these two finite progressions within 0<=tau<P therefore exhausts each prescribed chart. This bound depends on the complete rational input; it is not a bound in W, k, size, diameter, or reflection count. The zero slope is harmless to enumerate, though it cannot connect the prescribed unequal abscissas. No unrestricted billiard path is lost or asserted to be covered: internal horizontal ledge returns and repeated vertical excursions lie OUTSIDE these four charts.
