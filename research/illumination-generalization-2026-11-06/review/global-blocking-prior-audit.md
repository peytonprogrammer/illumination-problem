# Prior-work audit before the finite-blocking continuation

This is a diagnostic, not a universal proof milestone. The audited chain is actual physical darkness of a DISTINCT original pair => zero-blocking of ALL pairs of the two complete physical lift fibers => NEW physical-quotient restriction => W>=22 or an actual path. The last implication is still absent.

Already accepted, and NOT newly proved here:

- dark-endpoint-rationality.md WITH its supplement: arithmetic shear invariance and finite correspondence decomposition give rational coefficients and rational distinct physical dark endpoints. They do not bound slope denominators, correspondence degree or W. Finite projection covers omit finitely many values; whole-surface covering cannot be assumed.
- construction/darkness-specific-reduction.md: nonempty surface dark loci or a full2D dark correspondence do not bound primitive degree at fixed low physical W. Its six-wall intrinsic-rho graph pairs lifts of the SAME physical point, and uses ALL actual corner lifts, including removable regular ones.
- cone-only-fiber-dependency.md: genuine-zero avoidance suffices for a connection between SOME complete-fiber pair, after physical retracing. It does not license retaining one prescribed source/target sheet.
- half-turn-quotient.md, construction/reverser-prong-lemma.md and global-translation-descent.md: the intrinsic clean reverser and marked global automorphism constraints do not classify all zero-blocking correspondences. In the orthogonal case the strong translation descent uses preservation of the FULL corner set Sigma and a regular convex-corner anchor, not only genuine zeros.
- equivariant-correspondence-obstruction.md: one reversing quotient with BOTH inverse-forbidden-set guard and intrinsic-halfturn equivariance cannot cover a whole physical dark rectangle. Neither guard is derived from physical darkness. Finite unions and other mechanisms remain open.
- fold-class-theorem.md/fold-area-invariant.md: supplied strict factors are excluded atW21. Necessity of such a factor is not established; arbitrary torus-cover correspondences are not strict factors.
- global-bridge-independent.md and global-separator-independent.md: automatic chosen-word degeneration lifting fails; a bounded separator and local chord inequalities are necessary conditions only. R59 shows bare full-fiber separation in a litW14 room.

Primary finite-blocking hypotheses independently reread in this continuation: [LMW, journal Theorems1,2,10](https://msp.org/gt/2016/20-3/gt-v20-n3-p11-s.pdf) and [Wolecki v3, Theorem1.2/Lemma2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.09358v3). LMW gives scalar correspondences and finite projections off exceptional sets; it does not state that zero-blocking slopes must be negative or that all components arise from reversing involutions. Generic finite blocking is already automatic for our torus-covered arithmetic class and supplies no threshold21 restriction. Wolecki's finite physical-pair theorem allows isolated rational exceptional pairs; finiteness does not eliminate one specified A pair.
