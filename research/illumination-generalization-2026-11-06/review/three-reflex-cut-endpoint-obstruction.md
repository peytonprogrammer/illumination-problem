# Necessary vertical-mirror use at the remaining inner-bottom placements

This is a reviewer guard on extending TR01, not a darkness verdict and not a counterexample to its correctly restricted statement. It was claimed before considering the author's new TR02 chart, which DOES permit vertical mirrors and is not refuted here.

Use TR01's ordered spiral0<a<l<w<A,0<b<c<h<H. Suppose T has l<x_T<w,0<y_T<c, and the other actual interior mark S is in its uncovered left-or-upper region x_S<a or(y_S>h,x_S<=w).

Any ordinary original-room connection between them must enter the right strip x>w. Indeed after removing that strip, the room's available two-dimensional pieces are the connected upper/left portion and the lowest rectangle, separated because a<l and c<h. The apparent boundary bridge x=w,c<=y<=h is an actual vertical wall. A legal ray cannot use this segment as a boundary glide or pass through either true endpoint corner. A smooth contact with it necessarily has neighboring interior points in x>w. Thus a purported legal path staying in x<=w cannot pass from the upper/left component to the lowest rectangle.

If reflections are restricted to horizontal walls, horizontal velocity is constant. Since both endpoint abscissae are <=w and x_T<w, the entire path's abscissa stays between their endpoint values, so it never enters x>w. This contradicts the necessary access above. Therefore EVERY connection for this placement must use at least one vertical-wall reflection. This is a reflection-orientation necessity; no bound or existence claim for the remaining word is made.

In particular S=(w,y_S),h<y_S<H is an actual interior point of P but an artificial-boundary point of R. It cannot be added to the horizontal-only R theorem by a boundary-limit argument when the target is in the inner part l<x_T<w of the lower chamber. Any successful new construction must change the optical mechanism, not just include an equality endpoint. The prescribed actual A pair(20,24),(14,2) in the doubled known spiral is one instance.

This does not repeat a bottom/left-axis-only assertion: it excludes all horizontal-reflection-only words, on EVERY actual horizontal wall, for this stated placement class. The author's TR02 one-roof-plus-vertical mechanism passes this necessary test. No claim that these pairs are dark follows. No computation/search/clock/worker or geometry expansion was needed.
