## 1. Minimal reflection group and corner lifts

For a bounded strictly simple canonical45 polygon, merge collinear edges first. Write each genuine corner angle as m_i*pi/4, where m_i∈{1,2,3,5,6,7}. Put q_i=gcd(m_i,4), so its reduced angle is a_i*pi/n_i with a_i=m_i/q_i, n_i=4/q_i. The turning identity is

    sum_i(4-m_i)=8, hence sum_i m_i=4W-8.

The finite group generated by linear wall reflections is dihedral of order2N. Here N=2 if all walls belong to just one perpendicular pair of directions, and N=4 otherwise. A bounded polygon cannot have only parallel walls. In the N=2 case every m_i is2or6. Rotating an orthogonal polygon by45degrees still uses only a perpendicular pair and still gives N=2. Thus use the ACTUAL minimal reflection group, not an arbitrarily doubled unfolding.

Take2Ndisjoint polygon copies and perform the standard edge-reflection unfolding. A corner with reduced denominator n_i has N/n_i lifts, each of total cone angle2*pi*a_i. This follows by cycling the2n_iincident copies around the corner. There are2Ncopies total. Count ALL such lifts in Sigma, including cone angle2pi regular points: a billiard is required to avoid actual convex corners even when their lifts are removable.

Let e be the number of even m_i. Then the total number of actual-corner lifts is

    |Sigma|=sum_i N/n_i=(N/4)*sum_i q_i=(N/4)*(W+e).

The cone Gauss–Bonnet formula gives

    2g-2=sum_i (N/n_i)*(a_i-1)
         =(N/4)*sum_i(m_i-q_i)
         =(N/4)*(3W-e-8).

The genus calculation does not remove the regular corner marks from Sigma.

## 2. Bounds for the exact W<=21 target

For N=2 all angles are orthogonal, e=W and W is even. Therefore

    g=(W-2)/2, |Sigma|=W.

Under W<=21 one has W<=20 and g<=9, |Sigma|<=20.

For N=4,

    g=(3W-e-6)/2, |Sigma|=W+e.

There must be at least two odd corners: the parity of sum m_i is even, so their number is even, and no odd corners would mean only perpendicular directions and N=2. Thus e<=W-2. Also W-e is even. For oddW<=21 this gives e>=1 and g<=(3W-7)/2<=28; for evenW<=20 it gives e>=0 and g<=(3W-6)/2<=27. Finally |Sigma|<=2W-2<=40. These are safe upper bounds, not assertions that every extremal angle inventory is realizable.

Hence every target unfolds to an arithmetic translation surface with genus at most28, at most40 forbidden actual-corner lifts, and at most8lifts of either distinct interior endpoint. No dependence on k, triangle count, area or lattice diameter enters these topological bounds.

## 4. Consequence for a bounded critical-skeleton strategy

A proof based on at mostWcorner types may legitimately use the bounded genus/mark count above. It cannot then infer bounded square count, primitive torus-cover degree, regular-fiber cardinality, or a bounded list of endpoint sheet permutations. The L family proves such an inference false even after removing every redundant common period scale.

Neither the topology bound nor one extra finite k layer supplies that optical implication. No new bound on critical visits is asserted or rejected by this degree example.
