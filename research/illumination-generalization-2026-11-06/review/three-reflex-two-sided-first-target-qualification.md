## General exact junction argument

Put y=t-l>0 and let an incoming state coincide for a safe preturn branch(m_-,sigma_-) and postturn branch(m_+,sigma_+). Their cap-to-nominated-target durations are

    D_-=2c*m_-+h+sigma_-*v,
    D_+=2c*m_++h+sigma_+*v.

Equality of their physical cap coordinates gives

    t+epsilon*D_-=2l-t+epsilon*D_+,
    epsilon*(D_+-D_-)=2y>0.

The safe target is therefore reached STRICTLY EARLIER. With r=x_e-l and G=h-c, the slow left-turn time from the cap is G+r/epsilon, while

    D_-=G+(r-y)/epsilon < G+r/epsilon
        < G+(r+y)/epsilon=D_+.

At an opposite-arrival-sign junction the FG01 calculation gives r/(c*epsilon) integer. Thus the literal postturn continuation encounters the true l-floor or l-underside corner at that middle time, but the earlier safe target hit has already occurred. The incoming state remains an actual illuminating state; no singular continuation is required.

Same-arrival-sign junctions have noninteger r/(c*epsilon) under 0<v<c, so that particular corner obstruction is absent. Every branch still needs all its strict guards. This classification concerns only the declared last-bottom branches and does not license a generic corner repair elsewhere.
