# Necessary physical sector and holonomy conditions at a reflex match

Independent HAND lemma and local countercontrol, Codex01a1a00f, 2026-11-07. No new room example, search or trajectory census. These are statements about a specified corner neighborhood and specified exterior reflection words. They do not refute existential global rerouting or the target theorem.

## L1. Actual one-sided scattering at a 270-degree corner

Put the reflex corner at C=(0,0) with the NE quadrant missing. In a sufficiently small neighborhood the true incident walls are the positive horizontal and positive vertical axes; the other quadrants are interior. Take a local source germ represented by P=(-a,-b), a,b>0, aiming at C with limiting velocity(1,k0), k0=b/a>0. Perturb its launch direction through the SAME point P: write k=(b+delta)/a, so the incoming line is

    y=k*x+delta.

For small delta>0 the first incident-wall contact is the vertical wall at(0,delta), followed by velocity(-1,k). For small delta<0 it is the horizontal wall at(-delta/k,0), followed by velocity(1,-k). Both contacts are smooth and occur strictly before entering the missing quadrant. After reflection the respective rays leave into NW and SE. They have no second local incident-wall contact before departing a sufficiently small neighborhood.

As delta tends to0 from opposite sides, the limiting EXIT velocities are

    v_V=(-1,k0), v_H=(1,-k0)=-v_V.

Thus matching a source germ at the corner does not match its forward exit to a fixed target germ after changing sides. The two nearby physical passages differ by an antipodal directed velocity in this configuration. A sector switch alone supplies no legal corner contact at delta0 and no interior mechanism to reverse the velocity of an actual nonzero ray. The claim concerns this specified SW incoming sector; other incoming sectors need their own actual wall/first-hit analysis rather than automatic reuse of these signs.

Proof: solve x0 and y0 intersections of y=kx+delta. If delta>0 the y0 crossing occurs at negative x and is interior, while x0 at positive y is a wall; if delta<0 the x0 crossing is at negative y and interior, while y0 at positive x is the wall. Ordinary specular laws give the displayed signs. The reflected ray moves away from the other positive wall ray. This is local physical geometry, not an arbitrary cone continuation.

## L2. Two-sector holonomy cannot automatically remove the fixed-target corner root

Fix the ordinary exterior source and reversed-target wall words outside this neighborhood, and compare the two local one-wall words V and H using the SAME developed target germ. In the common developed source chart let the transformed corner be C*. Write the two actual-target images as Q_V and Q_H. Reflections in the two perpendicular incident supports obey

    R_H(q)=2C-R_V(q).

Conjugating by the unchanged source-prefix chart therefore gives

    Q_H=2C*-Q_V.

This equality is affine, including corner position; it is not merely a velocity label. Suppose the V-word fixed-target match is degenerate at the corner with positive times. Write

    P*=C*-lambda*v0, Q_V=C*+tau*v0,
    lambda,tau>0.

The H-word candidate displacement is then

    Q_H-P*=(lambda-tau)*v0.

Three cases exhaust this unchanged-exterior two-sector switch:

- lambda>tau: the only H-word unfolded candidate launches in the OLD direction but its developed endpoint occurs BEFORE the would-be corner contact. It cannot realize the declared new local H reflection before arrival.
- lambda=tau: the developed endpoint coincides with the source, giving zero flight, not a positive connection.
- lambda<tau: its only candidate launches in the OPPOSITE direction and does not reach the declared corner neighborhood along the retained incoming source itinerary.

Thus the OTHER local one-wall word does not automatically give a new legal corner-avoiding realization retaining the exterior words and passage structure. Even allowing a different slope does not help this two-word prescription: their fixed-target displacement vectors are collinear. This is a physical-word obstruction, not an actual dark-pair example. If actual T already occurs earlier along a genuine legal prefix, truncate there; that is a separately valid connection, not realization of the invalid switched word.

This lemma explicitly extends the inherited fixed-word rigidity to the TWO neighboring physical sector words. It does not claim that all alternative global words have those same images. A new physical return, another exterior word or a change of earlier source direction can escape it, but its actual target image and joint guards must be proved; they cannot be supplied by relabelling the failed two sectors.

## L3. Exact endpoint and directed-germ compatibility for a nonlocal repair

In a fixed initial chart with actual source S and a proposed NEW physical word w', its developed target is Q'=U_w'(T). A positive exact-target candidate has displacement d'=Q'-S, nonzero, and uniquely determined initial direction d'/|d'|. If the old target image was Q, then

    d'=(Q-S)+(Q'-Q).

Retaining the OLD positive launch direction requires the changed holonomy Q'-Q to be collinear with Q-S and d' a positive multiple of it. But with the same actual source, incoming sheet and ordinary prefix, retaining that launch follows the same deterministic orbit to the SAME forbidden corner. Therefore a genuine avoidance repair must change the actual launch/prefix before that corner, or supply a separately proved valid transformation with different physical contact data. Merely changing target-sheet names at the corner is not sufficient.

If new entry/exit states on a section are used, their positions, signed velocities and actual sheets must agree in the forward orientation; the reversed-target germ must be negated before comparison. A graph edge recording only that two sectors develop to the same Euclidean vector loses this condition. A singular match itself is not such an edge.

The fixed-word uniqueness used here is inherited from the earlier retuning audit. New content is the actual one-sided sector scattering and the two-word target-image identity in L1/L2, which explain exactly why this specific local sector-switch premise does not evade that old obstruction.

## L4. A cone prong is extra information

The four-copy unfolding of one physical270-degree vertex has total cone angle6pi and zero order2. In a local uniformizer z its translation coordinate is proportional to z^3. A fixed developed outgoing direction has THREE distinct prongs at this zero. They are not three regular continuations of the same incoming physical ray. A path through the zero is forbidden; choosing another cube-root branch does not produce a nonsingular straight segment.

Within a simply isolated cone neighborhood, if exterior endpoint positions and their translation development are fixed, the Euclidean straight segment is unique. If it passes through the developed center, inserting a curved detour changes the straight-flow condition; inserting an abstract prong change leaves the forbidden hit. A whole new geodesic/homotopy class can have different holonomy, but proving its global physical realization is precisely additional work. The labels 'same zero' and 'same developed direction' alone do not identify the required outgoing germ or endpoint sheet.
