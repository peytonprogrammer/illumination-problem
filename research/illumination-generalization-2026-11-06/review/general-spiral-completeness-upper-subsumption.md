## Exact common domain

Use an original ordered integral spiral with a=Pe,A=Ne,R=Ke,B=Le, e positive integral, integers N>P+L, 1<=K<L, L=2^ell, ell>=1. Let h be even, c odd, and actual native terminal S=(s,u),T=(t,v) satisfy 0<s<a,b<u<H,l<t<A,0<v<c and u>=h. In particular u,v are even integers. Impose nu2(N)<=nu2(K). No lower-source claim is made. At the mouth u=h, source strictness is retained; a source at a corner is excluded.

Set C=2H-h-u>0, G=h-c>0, d=c-v>0, F=G+d=h-v. Then C,F are even, G,d odd. The GSE03 upper criterion is

    Z0<R*H0,
    H0=gcd(AF,CR,CB),
    Z0=gcd(ARd,ABG,RBC).

Write

    H0=e*Hprime, Hprime=gcd(NF,CK,CL),
    Z0=e^2*Zprime, Zprime=gcd(NKd,NLG,KLC).

I prove the stronger integral divisibility Zprime | K*Hprime and an extra factor of at least2 in K*Hprime/Zprime. It follows that Z0<=R*H0/2<R*H0, uniformly for every actual horizontal offset. GSE03 therefore applies.

## Every odd-prime valuation

Fix any odd prime q. Write n=nu_q(N), k=nu_q(K), c0=nu_q(C), f=nu_q(F), g=nu_q(G), d0=nu_q(d). Since L is a power of two,

    nu_q(Hprime)=min(n+f,c0),
    nu_q(Zprime)=min(n+k+d0,n+g,k+c0).

The second quantity is at most k+c0. It is also at most k+n+f: indeed

    min(k+d0,g)<=k+nu_q(G+d).

To verify the latter at all cancellation cases: if d0<g, use k+d0 and f=d0; if g<d0, use g and f=g; if g=d0, use k+d0 and f>=d0. Thus

    nu_q(Zprime)<=k+min(n+f,c0)=nu_q(K*Hprime).

No odd-prime coprimality assumptions on N,K,C,G,d are used.

## Strict factor at the prime two

Put n=nu2(N), k=nu2(K), c0=nu2(C)>=1, f=nu2(F)>=1. Since K<L=2^ell, k<ell, and the shared-domain hypothesis gives n<=k. Since G,d are odd,

    nu2(Zprime)=min(n+k,n+ell,k+ell+c0)=n+k,
    nu2(Hprime)=min(n+f,c0+k,c0+ell)
                =min(n+f,c0+k)>=n+1.

Consequently nu2(K*Hprime)>=n+k+1>nu2(Zprime). Together with the odd-prime divisibility, K*Hprime/Zprime is a positive even integer. This proves the claimed strict bound, including every nearest-integer equality or resonance in the inherited optical proof.
