    p_q=(2m+1)A+z1, |z1|<R,

Set eta=R-|z1|, mu=R/(1+2A*beta), N=floor(2A/mu)+1 and Q=max(AF,N). I obtain q<=Q.

## Both right-window boundary residuals

If eta<mu, write z1=sigma*(R-eta), sigma=+/-1. Define

The exact target hypothesis gives F*tau_b integer and
