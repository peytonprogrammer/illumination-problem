# Complete actual-target test for one literal source/cap-preserving orbit

This general arithmetic/optical prerequisite is quantified over arbitrary original ordered integer spiral data and rational starting seeds. It gives a NECESSARY AND SUFFICIENT test for repair of ONE nominated state within ONE nominated finite band program, after the accepted exact-source/target regularizations. It is NOT global availability over all starting states or all physical rays. Independent review is required.

## Inputs and the actual target images

Let a=Pe,A=Ne,R=Ke,B=Le with positive integer P,N,K,L and positive rational e, in the original ordered geometry. Let the source be lower u<h, with chosen source duration X=h-u or h+u-2b; cap duration C=2(H-h); right duration G=h-c; target duration d=c-v or c+v; F=G+d. All these are positive integers. Native actual S,T give s,z=A-t rational,0<s<a,0<z<B.

Fix a rational seed tau0 and integer actual source/cap indices i0,m0 with strict literal residuals

z0=sigma*s+X*tau0-2Pe*i0,
z1=z0+C*tau0-(2m0+1)Ne,
|z0|<Pe, |z1|<Ke.

Do not assume a right guard, target root, auxiliary g-grid, sign choice or positivity yet. Define

r0=z1+G*tau0,
y0=r0/(2e),
W0=(r0+d*tau0-epsilon0*z)/(2e),

where epsilon0 is either reference sign. W0 is rational; its integrality is NOT assumed. The two desired actual images are encoded by

eta=0  (reference image epsilon0*z),
eta=epsilon0*z/e  (opposite image -epsilon0*z).

The latter must not be omitted, and need not be integral.

## Exact ALL-real source/cap-preserving slopes

A real increment Delta_tau preserves the two literal residuals, allowing integer source/cap index changes, exactly when

X*Delta_tau/(2eP), C*Delta_tau/(2eN) are integers.

Put Hsc=gcd(NX,PC) and delta0=PN/Hsc. Then

Delta_tau=2e*delta0*n, n integer

is the COMPLETE subgroup. The usual two-term Bezout argument proves both containments. In particular a fractional delta0 is included; restricting to a chosen integer period may lose valid repairs. An irrational seed would stay irrational under these rational shifts and cannot satisfy the rational finite-band target equation; this criterion is stated for rational seeds, including all existing constructors.

At a shifted phase n and right index k, the strict right condition is

    |y0+G*delta0*n-K*k| < K/2.                 (1)

The exact actual target condition for the chosen eta is

    W0+eta+F*delta0*n-K*k in L Z.             (2)

These retain both true target images and every rational residue. No k in gZ restriction is imposed without deriving it from (2).

## Convert (2) to one exact integer affine equation

Choose any positive integer Q clearing the denominators of delta0,W0,z/e. Write

alpha=Q*F*delta0, beta=Q*G*delta0,
kappa=Q*K, lambda=Q*L, Rhs=-Q*(W0+eta).

All are integers. Equation (2) is equivalent to the existence of integer (n,k,j) with

    alpha*n-kappa*k-lambda*j = Rhs.          (3)

Let h0=gcd(alpha,kappa,lambda)>0. There is no target-compatible state on this orbit for this image unless h0 divides Rhs. Conversely h0|Rhs gives an explicit particular solution x*=(n*,k*,j*) by Bezout. This is an exact divisibility condition, not an assumption that a source/cap seed was already on a favorable grid.

## Exact mesh of all target-compatible right states

Set the two integer row vectors

    V=(alpha,-kappa,-lambda),
    W=(beta,-kappa,0).

The allowed homogeneous integer changes are ker_Z(V). The integer right-coordinate changes W(ker_Z(V)) form EXACTLY h Z, where

h=gcd(kappa*(alpha-beta),lambda*beta,lambda*kappa)/h0 >0.

Proof: divide V by h0 to get a primitive integer row V'. Choose an integer u with V' dot u=1. The vectors e_i-V'_i*u generate its integer kernel. Their W values have gcd equal to the gcd of all pair minors V'_i W_j-V'_j W_i. One divisibility follows by expressing each minor through those corrected values. The reverse follows from

W_i-V'_i*(W dot u) = sum_j u_j*(V'_j W_i-V'_i W_j).

Thus the pair-minor gcd is EXACT, giving the displayed h. This proves the projected subgroup, rather than inferring a covering from a determinant alone.

For a particular solution x*, let

    Y*=y0+(beta*n*-kappa*k*)/Q,
    rho=h/Q.

Every target-compatible right state, with no omitted fractional nulls, is in the exact coset

    Y*+rho Z.

Therefore (1) and (2) have a joint integer solution if and only if

    h0 divides Rhs AND dist(Y*,rho Z)<K/2.     (FO)

The condition is independent of the chosen particular solution and denominator Q. Test FO for BOTH eta values. Nearest rounding gives a finite candidate when strictness holds. If rho=K, the exact half-mesh equality fails the open window; if rho>K, the actual offset still matters. A rank, nonzero determinant or mesh claim without this coset check is insufficient.

Let M be a common integer multiple of P,N,K,L. Since X*M/P and C*M/N are integers, n+=M/delta0 is an integer by completeness of the preserving subgroup. Simultaneously set

All are positive integers and preserve (3): F*M-G*M-d*M=0. They also preserve the right coordinate (1), every literal source/cap residual and the exact target image. They increase the actual i,m,k indices and tau. A sufficiently large positive number of these common physical shifts makes all folds and the slope physical. Hence FO's integer solutions cannot be discarded merely because the chosen particular solution had negative indices.
