## Complete one-outer-roof necessity

Its initial vertical component must be positive: a downward component cannot reach that roof before another horizontal reflection. Zero vertical component cannot reach either roof or T. Normalize |vy|=1. Its horizontal magnitude kappa>0; horizontal magnitude zero cannot change actual x8 to24. Both signs of initial vx are allowed.

In the cap represent these signs by F_32(sigma*8+kappa*elapsed), sigma=+1or-1. Since F_32(+-8)=8 and its derivatives there are+-1, this representation preserves actual S and the actual initial signed velocity.

## Consequence

A universal ordered-spiral proof cannot rely solely on one outer-roof plus vertical words, even after arbitrary target/source sign choices. It needs a genuinely additional optical itinerary on at least this pair. The floor-return mechanism provides a concrete next candidate, with an extra strict floor guard that a general proof must retain. This strengthens the earlier positive-seed-only failure control without claiming darkness or the full r3 theorem.
