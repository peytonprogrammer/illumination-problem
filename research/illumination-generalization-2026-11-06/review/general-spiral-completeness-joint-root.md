# Exact joint-root prerequisite for the ordered-spiral milestone

This consolidates the root equations used in the accepted constructions. It is NOT a proof that the required affine lattice intersects the windows for every room or pair. That global existence claim is the missing lemma.

## Original marks and finite band programs

Use the integer ordered domain and residual actual terminal pair of coverage.md. Put R=A-w,B=A-l,z=A-t,G=h-c>0. Choose source sign sigma in {+1,-1} and actual target image epsilon in {+1,-1}.

For lower u<h choose X=h-u (initial vy=+1), or X=h+u-2b (initial vy=-1 followed by the true source floor). Put C=2(H-h). Choose d=c-v (direct final descent), or d=c+v (true bottom-floor reflection). Let D=X+C+G+d. All four band durations are positive integers.

For upper or mouth u>=h use the direct full cap from actual S, with C=2H-h-u>0, choose the same two possible d, and D=C+G+d. Do not use a zero-determinant lower construction at u=h.

## Lower complete actual-target root and open windows

For arbitrary integer indices i,m,k,j define

    tau=((2m+1)A + epsilon*z - sigma*s + 2a*i + 2R*k + 2B*j)/D,
    z0=sigma*s+X*tau-2a*i,
    z1=z0+C*tau-(2m+1)A,
    z2=z1+G*tau-2R*k.

The exact joint open-window condition is

    |z0|<a, |z1|<R, |z2|<R.                    (J_lower)

The root identity gives z2+d*tau=epsilon*z+2B*j. Thus actual-target parity and image are part of the root BEFORE the passage choices. No auxiliary g|K condition appears. i,m,k must be actual fold indices of the final direction; strict windows and the positivity lift below ensure that fact.

Write tau0=(A+epsilon*z-sigma*s)/D. The residual vector belongs to the affine lattice alpha+Z*vi+Z*vm+Z*vk+Z*vj, where

    alpha=(sigma*s+X*tau0,
           sigma*s+(X+C)*tau0-A,
           sigma*s+(X+C+G)*tau0-A),
    vi=(2a/D)*(-(C+G+d),-(G+d),-d),
    vm=(2A/D)*(X,-(G+d),-d),
    vk=(2R/D)*(X,X+C,-d),
    vj=(2B/D)*(X,X+C,X+C+G).

The required box is (-a,a) times (-R,R) times (-R,R). A determinant/covolume, nonzero rank or recurrence statement alone does not show this nominated affine offset meets that box. All quantities here are rational; invoking generic density of an irrational orbit would be inapplicable.

## Upper/mouth complete root and windows

Omit i,z0,X and use

    tau=((2m+1)A+epsilon*z-sigma*s+2R*k+2B*j)/D,
    z1=sigma*s+C*tau-(2m+1)A,
    z2=z1+G*tau-2R*k,
    |z1|<R, |z2|<R.                            (J_upper)

Its exact lattice columns are

    vm=(2A/D)*(-(G+d),-d),
    vk=(2R/D)*(C,-d),
    vj=(2B/D)*(C,C+G),

with offset (sigma*s+C*tau0-A,sigma*s+(C+G)*tau0-A). Again its actual target is built into the root.

## Positive indices are not the remaining global obstruction

Let Lambda=lcm(a,A,R,B) in the ORIGINAL integer widths. The common physical slope shift tau -> tau+2Lambda*q has index increments

    Delta_i=X*Lambda*q/a,
    Delta_m=C*Lambda*q/A,
    Delta_k=G*Lambda*q/R,
    Delta_j=d*Lambda*q/B.

Every relevant increment is a positive integer for q>0; upper simply omits i. Direct substitution preserves every literal residual and the full actual target image. Starting from ANY integer solution of J_lower/J_upper, a sufficiently large positive integer q makes tau positive and i,m,k nonnegative. The whole endpoint index j also becomes positive if desired. Therefore a claim of global impossibility based only on an initial negative index would be invalid. Conversely an existence proof still has to find a strict-window integer solution first.

## All physical qualifications of the root implication

A strict lower source guard is the actual left h mouth; the cap/right guards are the actual right h and c mouths. Source/cap/right/bottom horizontal folds agree at the seams in both position and signed derivative. Zero residuals are single ordinary contacts on the smooth outer wall, while +/-a or +/-R would be actual forbidden reflex corners and are excluded. The bands use actual finite walls only.

For a lower root the unique outer-roof contact cannot be a true corner: its h entry x<a and h exit x>A-R>a would otherwise coincide by fold symmetry. A source-floor chart must be checked at the FINAL tau. If its floor phase is singular, only the proved exact-original-S suffix starting at time 2(u-b) is emitted; X-2(u-b)=h-u>0. Its corner-containing prefix is never traversed. An upper formal roof corner is handled by the exact-original-S descending suffix before the guarded h/c exits, as in AP01/AP05.

For a target-floor chart a FINAL singular bottom floor implies the SAME actual T occurs on its earlier descending height-v leg: F_B(qB-v*tau)=F_B(qB+v*tau). Emit the earlier regular prefix. Source-suffix deletion and target-prefix truncation affect disjoint time portions and retain the same actual S,T and one direction. If a floor is ordinary, retain the full corresponding contact. A numerical proof must test the emitted path, never trace a formal singular piece and call it legal.

Source bands at target height have x<=a<l<t; cap/right bands are above v. The bottom may hit T on descent before its proposed ascent; any first actual T is a legal prefix. This supplies existence without an unsupported final-first-arrival assertion. Finite times and widths give finite ordinary reflections.

Thus a joint-window integer solution gives an actual legal path after these explicit repairs. Within the nominated strictly guarded finite band program, any ordinary realization supplies such signed integer indices by unfolding. This does NOT establish that the nominated finite program union contains a path for every actual pair, or contains every possible ray.

## Universal statement needing proof or a route countercontrol

For EVERY ordered integer room and EVERY residual native pair, is at least one of J_lower (four source/target choices, both sigma/epsilon) or J_upper (two target choices, both signs) nonempty?

A positive answer with the physical qualifications above would close the ordered-spiral milestone. A negative answer would reject only this finite-program universal lemma and require additional genuine horizontal contacts or a different construction. Neither the accepted scalar four-criterion limitation nor a single bad preserving coset disproves this joint-window statement. Conversely merely displaying these equations does not establish it.
