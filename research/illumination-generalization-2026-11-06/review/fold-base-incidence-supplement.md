## 2. Independent incidence derivation: w>=8

Use I,J for interior A and non-A lattice counts, s_A,s_N for smooth boundary counts, and w for genuine corners. Let N be the number of canonical triangles. Triangulated-disk Euler and triangle/edge incidence give

    N = 2(I+J)+w+s_A+s_N-2.

For completeness, with V_int=I+J and V_boundary=w+s_A+s_N, Euler is V-E+N=1 and 3N=2E-V_boundary. Eliminating E gives the displayed identity. Every triangle has EXACTLY ONE A vertex. An interior A contributes8 triangles; a smooth A contributes4; a genuine A corner contributes its positive contiguous fan size m in {1,2,3,5,6,7}. Thus, defining

    Q_A = 3*s_A + sum_{A corners} m,

one has N=8I+Q_A+s_A. Eliminating N gives

    w = 2+6I+Q_A-2J-s_N.

This is a geometric identity independent of the optical computer certificates. Under the stated strict parity-base condition J=s_N=0, and s_A>=2. Hence

    w = 2+6I+Q_A >= 2+3*s_A >=8.             (C)

No unit-square assumption is used. Partial squares, diagonal edges, all genuine45degree angle fans and all smooth boundary marks are included. This is a lower bound8; it does NOT claim the orthogonal lower bound10 for diagonal bases, or existence of an eight-wall base.
