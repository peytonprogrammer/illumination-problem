# Four-chart reduced-period coverage necessarily stops before arbitrary source valuations

This is a limitation of the accepted sufficient criteria and the proposed AP06 source-chart composition. It is NOT darkness, failure of those theorems, or exclusion of other actual rays.

Retain the ordered geometry and all the proposed AP06 parity conditions except replace nu2(P)<=2 by nu2(P)>=3. In particular N,K are odd, P<=K, K<L, L is dyadic, e is a positive integer, b,c are odd, h is even, and 0<b<c<h<H. There is ALWAYS a specified actual interior even-even pair for which none of the four source-up/source-floor times combined with target-direct/target-floor times meets the reduced-period divisor criterion g|K.

Let d=h-b. This is odd and at least three. Choose a native lower source height as follows.

- If d=3 modulo 4, choose u=h-2. Then X_U=2 and X_F=2(d-1)=4 modulo 8. Their two-adic valuations are 1 and 2.
- If d=1 modulo 4, necessarily d>=5. Choose u=h-4. Then X_U=4 and X_F=2(d-2)=6 modulo 8. Their valuations are 2 and 1.

In either case b<u<h, u is even, and BOTH positive source-band times have valuation strictly below 3. Therefore P/gcd(P,X) is even for both source choices whenever nu2(P)>=3. Consequently

M_X=lcm(P/gcd(P,X),N/gcd(N,2(H-h)),K/gcd(K,h-c))

is even for both source charts. For ANY native bottom target height v even, both target durations c-v and c+v are odd. Thus g_X,±=gcd(M_X(c±v),L) is even, because L is even. Since K is odd, g_X,± cannot divide K. All FOUR source/target chart criteria fail at this original pair.

An actual pair exists without a new room example: take S=(2,u),T=(A-2,2). Since P>=8 and e>=1, 0<2<Pe. Odd K>=P forces K>=9, so dyadic L>K gives L>=16 and B=Le>2. Hence A-B<A-2<A. Also c>=3 makes 0<2<c. The strict original geometry separates the terminal rectangles, so the pair is distinct and interior. This construction works for EVERY metric in the stated unbounded parameter class.

The result does not claim these are the only bad source heights, nor that the four physical itinerary classes have no rays. It proves that their specific full-divisor seed criterion cannot close every native pair at higher source valuations, even after independently choosing both real source charts and both actual target charts. A further positive theorem must relax the divisor selection, introduce a genuinely different optical itinerary, or prove a separate construction for these actual pairs. The existing ER02/ER03 width-family repairs illustrate why a sufficient-criterion failure must never be called darkness.

This hand argument uses no discovery effort, numerical sample, new engine or cutoff. It is disjoint from the author's all-outer-width extension. Reciprocal audit may be bundled with AP06; the frozen AP06 proof and request are unchanged.
