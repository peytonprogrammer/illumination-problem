The current FP01 result gives a directly checked physical period for the five declared programmes. I considered extending its integer event-state induction to arbitrary repeated original ledge/floor/roof returns. Before treating that as a new global idea, I read review/weighted-return-wr01-independent.md in full.

WR01 already accepts genuine cylinder-lcm affine maps of the unfolded integral room, fixing each labelled origin-fiber point and the full forbidden set. They transport WHOLE straight paths and their actual endpoint sheets, with all unbounded integer marked-return weights retained. It explicitly distinguishes this from preserving the old physical reflection word. Thus an all-word slope-shift transport is covered in substance by an accepted earlier result; presenting it again as an illumination advance would be misleading.

If the class is enlarged to arbitrarily many horizontal returns, those D are unbounded. A slope period by itself therefore gives no finite exhaustive candidate set, uniform return bound, positive target intersection or r<=8 use for that larger class. No new denominator, degree, direction or return cutoff is justified by this audit.

No new hypothesis, computation, discovery clock or search is opened here. This is a record of a considered route already subsumed by prior work, not a new mathematical result. The global actual-itinerary selection gap remains. The new complete short-word rejection and pending ML01 review must retain their own exact scopes.
