## Related results retained in proof records

The new papers consolidate the current restricted minimum and strict-fold consequence, the uncapped one-sided middle-row comb theorem, the long-neck and conditional tree results, and the main tall-or-wide and coprime-period spiral theorems. The affine note develops the transport mechanism; it cites the separate fixed-foliation noncompression result without presenting its full stabilizer classification.

The two-sided comb subclasses, exceptional network-ratio strips, later arithmetic spiral supplements and remaining affine obstructions stay in the [results inventory](../RESULTS.md), [spiral follow-up](../docs/FOLLOWUP-2026-11-08.md) and [affine update](../docs/UPDATE-2026-11-08.md). They are not implicitly added to the papers' theorem statements.
