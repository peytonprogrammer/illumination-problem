Daniel Wang and Peyton Wang, [Light through comb rooms](../../preprints/middle-row-combs/README.md), working manuscript.

Central finite matching proof, path construction, remaining endpoint cases, and the height-two lattice corollary. Only spine and tooth-middle-row endpoints are covered.

The worked example uses c=2, u=v=1, H=5, Dₛ=9/5, and Dₜ=27/10. The exact matching slope is 14/17, the remainders are 167/170 and 123/170, and the resulting path has four smooth reflections. Off-middle-row tooth endpoints are not covered.
