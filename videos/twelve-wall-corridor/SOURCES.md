Proof map with one-pass/three-pass chart geometry, cap transfer, a five-reflection example and the exact continuous certificate-cover argument. Only lower-to-upper outer-chamber pairs.

The manuscript supplies the portal chart table, cap-transfer lemma, five-reflection fixture, support reduction and certificate dependencies C1–C7. The one-pass diagram evaluates its equal-depth chart at U=V=1/3, s=3/5, t=2/5. The continuous guard plot is an equal-depth illustration, not an extracted C7 cover. Archived certificate counts are reported without claiming a fresh full-corpus replay.
