Tall-or-wide all-real proof and the separate coprime thin-cap even-even theorem, with an exact archived 60-reflection worked path. Protected L/U constructions are cited dependencies.

The all-real proof uses protected L/U endpoint reductions, finite corner exclusions, the safe-interval bound and an exact target-root progression. The coprime theorem is separate and covers native even-even pairs in its stated thin-cap family. The archived fixed example has geometry (4,12,16,20;1,5,7,8), actual S=(2,6), actual T=(14,2), slope 49 and 60 smooth reflections. The provisional slope 9 path reaches (18,2); it illustrates the parity obstruction and is not a connecting path to T. Both paths were checked with exact original-wall geometry. No ray discovery or mathematical source edits.
