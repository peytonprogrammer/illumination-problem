Two-chamber proof using classical directional minimality and exact phase matching; a brief separate summary of the conditional network theorem.

Two-chamber proof: finite entrance beams, virtual marks, the target-image progression and strict folding guards. Classical input: [Masur and Tabachnikov, Rational billiards and flat structures, Theorem 1.8](https://math.uchicago.edu/~masur/handbook1.pdf). Density supplies entrance beams; arithmetic gives exact target attainment. The network extension is conditional on an initial legal witness and commensurate parallel-neck widths. The diagrams use the frozen prescribed L=6 and L=100 controls, not a new search.
