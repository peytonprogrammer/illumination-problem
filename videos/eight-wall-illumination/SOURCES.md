Proof map, detailed L-room mechanism, and selected U, corridor, and staircase constructions. The manuscript contains the full technical proof.

The scene diagrams use the six normal forms and prescribed example rays in the manuscript. All-real quantifiers follow from the analytic argument, not the numerical illustrations. The L mechanism is explained in detail; the other constructions and staircase coverage are summarized.

Narration was generated locally with Kokoro-82M, voice `af_heart`. The project includes the generated audio; no model weights or runtime dependencies are bundled.
