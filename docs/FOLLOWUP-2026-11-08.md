Research note dated November 8, 2026. The initial theorem below has an independent internal AI review. Its proof, checker, and evidence files are included in the current [file manifest](../manifests/README.md).

## Accepted result and exact limits

For positive integer a, odd integer N>=5, integers 0<b<c<h with c odd and H=h+1, define l=(N-2)a, w=(N-1)a, A=Na and the ten-wall room with boundary

```
(a,b),(a,h),(w,h),(w,c),(l,c),(l,0),(A,0),(A,H),(0,H),(0,b).
```

Every distinct actual interior even-even pair illuminates in this family, without a dimension, area or reflection cutoff. The underlying terminal lemma allows arbitrary integer vertical dimensions with gcd(2(H-h),N)=1 and odd c-v. It directly selects a ray by forbidden congruence classes, a finite coprime cap rotation and a target-period parity correction. It does not rely on the disproved monotone iteration.

This is a restricted family of native lattice marks. It does not cover all real interior points, all ten-wall rooms, all eight-reflex rooms, or universal 22-wall minimality. Historical novelty is unconfirmed. Independent AI review and exact rational replay are not external expert refereeing or formal verification.
