# Negative findings

These results reject specific proposed implications. They do not supply a dark room below the conjectured wall minimum. The [results inventory](../RESULTS.md) gives their full statements and source links.

## Counterexamples to proof mechanisms

| Result | Rejected implication | What remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| [R18: direction complexity](../RESULTS.md#r18) | Few walls force a bounded primitive direction or a universal affine rank descent | A finite universal direction list and the low-wall illumination problem |
| [R19: normalization and limits](../RESULTS.md#r19) | Geometric rescaling or nearby illumination automatically preserves the required exact paths | A valid global optical reduction |
| [R20: whole-copy folds](../RESULTS.md#r20) | Every dark room admits the stated unrestricted Euclidean whole-copy explanation | Factor existence for arbitrary dark rooms; neither control is a room with at most 21 walls |
| [R21: separators and monodromy](../RESULTS.md#r21) | A cone separator, finite blocking, or abstract transitivity alone forces the desired corner bound | The missing geometric implication; the abstract example is not a physically realized low-wall room |
| [R22: one-wall repair](../RESULTS.md#r22) | Every illuminated pair has a direct or one-reflection path | Larger reflection bounds; the prescribed pair's exact minimum is two |
| [R23: joint selection](../RESULTS.md#r23) | The three specified shortest-path, divisibility, and sector-angle shortcuts force a connecting path | Arbitrary-weight, non-minimizing global existence |

## Spiral and affine obstructions

TR06 refutes the stated nearest-period monotone iteration. Its method-only conclusion has an independent review; it does not prove darkness. See [the spiral note](FOLLOWUP-2026-11-08.md#negative-result-and-extension-scope).

[Affine compression counterexamples, R27](../RESULTS.md#r27), reject exact-target one-copy coverage and compression of every given light path at their stated scopes. The surviving fixed direction also has a full-group axis/diagonal obstruction. These do not exclude every possible affine seed.

[Realization-test obstructions, R28](../RESULTS.md#r28), show that endpoint permutations, absolute homology, and local geometry do not establish a genuine global affine realization. They do not refute the proposed full-group norm minimum. The [affine note](UPDATE-2026-11-08.md) links the proofs and reviews for each conclusion.

## Open problems

The archive does not settle general ten-wall illumination, all orthogonal rooms with at most eight reflex corners, or universal 22-wall minimality. A counterexample to one proof method must not be presented as a counterexample to those mathematical claims.
