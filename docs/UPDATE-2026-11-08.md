# Eight-wall proof and affine mechanisms

Research note dated November 8, 2026. This note describes the corrected eight-wall proof, scoped affine mechanisms, their obstructions, and prescribed controls. It does not claim universal minimality, confirmed historical novelty, external refereeing, or formal proof-assistant verification.

## Corrected eight-wall proof

A [PDF manuscript](../preprints/eight-wall/manuscript.pdf) now typesets the complete corrected proof, with figures, a referee checklist and reproduction instructions. [Source and build details](../preprints/eight-wall/README.md). This is a presentation update; no new theorem or novelty claim is added.

The [standalone theorem and proof](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/eight-wall-review-packet/theorem-and-proof.md) states: every finite strictly simple orthogonal polygon with at most eight maximal walls illuminates every distinct real interior pair. Dimensions are arbitrary positive reals. Eight walls means at most two inward corners.

The [coordinator review](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/final-review/eight-wall-review.md) records manuscript corrections, the U-room seam argument and finite bounds, and repaired checker-failure propagation. Original research arguments and their old receipts remain archived. This corrected packet supersedes earlier manuscript transcription mistakes at this exact scope.

The [figures](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/fable/eight-wall-review-packet/figures/) illustrate the six normal forms and verified example paths.

## Affine transport: positive mechanisms and negative results

The [affine endpoint-transport note](../preprints/affine-endpoint-transport/manuscript.pdf) now presents the exact pair-transport argument, uniform-source L criterion and integral shear test, with [source and scope details](../preprints/affine-endpoint-transport/README.md). The full range of obstructions below remains in its cited proof records.

An affine map here means a genuine global affine homeomorphism of the actual reflection unfolding, preserving every physical corner lift, including regular convex corners. A locally affine deformation or an arbitrary integer matrix is insufficient.

| Reviewed conclusion | Exact limit and evidence |
|---|---|
| Both endpoints may move; a regular straight seed pulls back to the exact original endpoints. | Conditional transport; does not assert a suitable map exists. [Proof and L shear criterion](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/affine-seed-reduction/pair-transport.md). |
| An explicit cylinder shear gives seeds from every source to certain L targets. | Target congruence/cylinder hypotheses are required. Existing all-real L illumination already covers more pairs. Same proof. |
| A diagonal twist gives exact seeds for specified pairs in an infinite d=1 mod4 L family. | Not all pairs or all L metrics. [Proof](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/target-fiber-peer-goals/reviewer/diagonal-phase-lemma.md), [reconciled independent review](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/target-fiber-peer-goals/reviewer/diagonal-reconciliation.json). |
| The exact-original-target one-copy seed assertion is false. | Other original target lifts can evade that obstruction; the physical pair illuminates. [Obstruction](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/pair-coverage-peer-goals/author/obstruction.md). |
| Complete known coordinate and diagonal stabilizers can all fail. | Their generated subgroup is not proved to be the entire affine group. [Argument](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/target-fiber-peer-goals/reviewer/complete-diagonal-stabilizer.md), [review](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/target-fiber-peer-goals/author/complete-diagonal-review.md). |
| A valid light path need not admit global affine compression into one polygon copy. | Full-group negative for the specified original path directions, not every connecting path. [Proof](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/target-fiber-peer-goals/reviewer/noncompressible-connection.md), [review reconciliation](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/target-fiber-peer-goals/reviewer/noncompression-reconciliation.json). |
| Compressing the surviving one-reflection path requires strict l1 shortening; no genuine affine map sends its direction to any axis or either diagonal. | Other realized directions remain open. [Norm guard](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/primitive-one-left-peer-goals/author/argument.md), [marked-return proof](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/primitive-one-left-peer-goals/reviewer/marked-return-obstruction.md), [review](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/primitive-one-left-peer-goals/reviewer/marked-return-reconciliation.json). |
| Endpoint permutation, absolute homology and local geometry do not determine genuine global affine realization. | These refute weaker proof routes; they do not refute the proposed full-group norm minimum. [Homology proof](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/full-affine-bound-peer-goals/author/argument.md), [same-surface nonaffine control](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/full-affine-bound-peer-goals/reviewer/topological-control.md), [final assessment](../research/illumination-generalization-2026-11-06/coordination/general-proof-priority/full-affine-bound-peer-goals/reviewer/blocked-handoff.md). |

The proposed minimum over the entire realized affine group remains unproved and unrefuted. Compression of the fixed remaining path, all-L seed coverage, all ten-wall illumination and universal 22-wall minimality remain open. No new positive room coverage follows from the recent affine work.

## Reproduce small prescribed controls

From the repository root, with Python 3:

```sh
python3 tools/archive.py verify-git
python3 tools/reproduce.py eight-wall
python3 tools/reproduce.py affine
```

The runners use disposable copies, adapt only historical workspace-path markers there, and leave archived bytes unchanged. The eight-wall suite runs the twelve prescribed scripts; the affine suite checks four fixed shear paths and the fixed d=5 diagonal example. These are controls of formulas and geometry, not proofs by sampling. No discovery search, old research timer or solver pipeline is started.
